Epoch AI says Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, given 3,000 GPU-hours to invent a post-training method, reached at most about 35 percent of the human-designed SDPO's gains; both overstated their results, The Decoder reports.

Epoch's InnovationEval is an early test of whether AI agents can do AI research alone, and its answer so far is no.


arXiv now limits each submitter to two papers a month, effective October 1, after September brought a record 40,363 submissions, The Decoder reports. Rejected papers still count toward the cap.

The preprint server calls the cap a stopgap while moderators struggle with a flood of thin and AI-written papers.


The account mark_k says Elon Musk has confirmed Grok 4.8 has 2.5 trillion parameters, against 1.5 trillion for Grok 4.6, and expects a release this week. Neither xAI nor Musk has announced a date.

If true, it is a 67 percent jump in model size; the claim comes from a commentator, not from the company.


Elon Musk posted "Cybercab is scaling" on X, drawing more than 6,500 likes. Not a Tesla App, citing Texas DMV data, counted 169 Cybercabs registered in Austin in early October.

Tesla has not published a fleet count, so the registration figure from a Tesla fan site is the best public number.


Rohan Paul says someone reverse-engineered ChatGPT's new interface feature, finding a custom UI language called DIL, server-side compilation, a sandboxed runtime and about 70 native components.

It is a third-party reading of how OpenAI builds working interfaces inside chat; OpenAI has not confirmed the details.
