Hadrian, an agentic offensive-security start-up, raised $40 million co-led by Forgepoint Capital International and SmartFin, taking its total to $65 million, according to tech.eu.

Hadrian claims its platform cuts time to resolution by 80 percent; those figures are the company's own and untested.


MemAdapter, from Jilin and Xiamen universities, is a framework against memory-induced sycophancy in agents; the authors say it cuts sycophancy failure rates by up to 33 percentage points across five memory systems.

Agents that remember users tend to agree with their past beliefs, so a fix that works across memory systems would matter for assistants with long-term memory.
