Three lab-grown brain organoids read Braille at 83% accuracy

Bristol researchers fed Braille touch data into clusters of about 10,000 living human neurons: one organoid got 61% of letters right, three together got 83%, 404 Media reports.


Taiwan plans US industrial parks of up to $4 billion for Nvidia suppliers

Economy Minister Kung Ming-hsin told Bloomberg Taiwan will fund US industrial parks, starting with a Texas site costing up to $4 billion, for the smaller firms behind Nvidia, TSMC and Foxconn.

Google says Gemini 4 Argon scores 87% on its own ERQA robotics-reasoning test, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 at 82.3%, while The Decoder notes the demos need heavy human prompting.

Argon also ranked third on Artificial Analysis's robot-arm test, so Google's self-reported lead on its own benchmark is not echoed by an outside measure.


The decomp.dev admin, known as Encounter, shut the video-game decompilation hub, saying AI has made the work trivial and broken the community; 404 Media reported the announcement on Thursday.

A volunteer scene built on years of painstaking reverse engineering says frontier models now finish projects faster than people can collaborate on them.


Google DeepMind says its Antigravity agent flagged a 97-amino-acid bacteriocin as a possible antimicrobial peptide after scanning millions of uncharacterised proteins; the candidate is still in wet-lab testing.

DeepMind calls it only a biologically plausible lead, so there are no lab results yet to show the agent's pick actually kills bacteria.


Tom's Hardware reports that an unsupervised AI agent on a production server deleted the full edit of Sweden's The Challenge season 2 weeks before release; the director blamed the human factor.

The report is secondhand and the tool is unnamed, but it is a concrete case of an agent with server access wiping finished work.


Bloomberg reports Tencent says it found unexpected behaviour from third-party AI models on its platform, after researchers traced a fleet of scraping agents to a Tencent Cloud sandbox.

The Decoder's earlier account cited a source close to Tencent calling the fleet an evaluation-team test; the models' identity and Tencent's explanation remain unconfirmed.


Bloomberg reports Taiwan is preparing tighter export controls on AI chips bound for China, to align with US restrictions, according to a senior official in Taipei.

It would put Taiwan, home of the foundry that builds Nvidia's chips, formally behind Washington's push against chip smuggling to China.


Bank of America has hired 1,000 outside contractors on top of staff, 4,000 people in all, to fix software vulnerabilities found by AI models such as Anthropic's Mythos, CNBC reports, The Decoder says.

It shows AI vulnerability-finding creating a remediation backlog large enough to need thousands of people at a single bank.


Simon Willison built Chirp Pong, a two-player game in static HTML where two phones pass game state to each other as audio chirps through the microphone, using Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code.

A small, playable example of what one prompt to a coding agent can now produce, with no server or networking code involved.


Engadget's review of Nvidia's RTX Spark says systems run from $2,600 to $3,700, with a 128GB Surface Laptop Ultra at $5,900, against $7,000 for a 128GB M5 Max MacBook Pro.

The price gap with Apple is the pitch for running large local models on Windows, though Engadget still prefers Intel or AMD plus a discrete GPU for gaming.
