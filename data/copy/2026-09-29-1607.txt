Claude.ai, the API, Claude Code, Cowork and the Console suffered elevated errors from 14:21 UTC, and a second fault blocked sign-ins, Anthropic's status page says; service recovered at 14:59 UTC.

Anthropic warns that some messages sent between 14:00 and 14:59 UTC may not have been saved, and the incident reached the Hacker News front page with 154 points.


Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas says Perplexity Computer, running Claude Opus 5.5, built a browser-based open-world game by renting its own GPUs, running headless Blender and designing audio, for about $2,000 in credits.

It is a maker's demo with a playable link, so the cost and the degree of autonomy are Perplexity's claims and have not been independently checked.

https://x.com/AravSrinivas/status/2104941391272956125


Victor Taelin says he is preparing a trip to San Francisco for a $20 million raise for Bend, and plans two internal teams, BendCore for the language and SupGen for symbolic AI research.

Taelin's post, which drew 290 likes, also lists plans to rewrite Bend's proof kernel in Bend itself, so the roadmap is his own and the raise has not been confirmed.


OpenResearch, a tool that turns coding agents into research agents, now ships as a desktop app for macOS on Apple Silicon and Intel, Windows x64, and Linux on x64 and ARM, alphaXiv says.

The desktop builds let people run the research agent on their own machines, which alphaXiv frames as owning the whole stack end to end.


Show HN: Jevstiller trains a small local model on a text-classification API's answers, and its author says it returns the API's label on at least a chosen share of requests.

The author reports about 15 ms on a CPU against roughly 300 ms for the API, at a cost of 4 to 8 points of coverage; the post had 18 points on Hacker News.


Xiaomi's MiMo team proposes grading groups of code-agent attempts together, so passing solutions of different quality get different rewards, in a paper that had 65 upvotes on Hugging Face's daily papers.

Binary test rewards give every passing attempt the same score under GRPO, and the authors argue that hides which implementations are actually better.


Tencent Hunyuan researchers study scaling laws for multimodal models that skip the pretrained visual encoder and learn from raw pixels, in a paper with 53 upvotes on Hugging Face's daily papers.

Encoder-free designs are simpler, but the authors say their scaling behaviour had not been studied systematically until now.


Qwen's QwenGyre is an elastic reinforcement-learning framework for training agents on long-horizon tasks that run for hours and nearly 1 million tokens per rollout, its paper says.

The paper, which had 22 upvotes on Hugging Face's daily papers, targets the cost of applying online reinforcement learning to such long executions.
