The Wall Street Journal reports a survey in which only 11 percent of 396 businesses could forecast their AI spending, and a Microsoft finding that cheaper models cost more on 32 percent of 6,800 tasks.

Per-token prices can mislead: a cheaper model that needs more attempts or tokens may cost more overall, which is why spending caps are becoming a product feature.
