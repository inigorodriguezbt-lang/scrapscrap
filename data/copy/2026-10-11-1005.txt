The vLLM project congratulated the vllm-sr team on releasing Decision 3.0, a multimodal decision model whose weights are now open-sourced on Hugging Face, according to vLLM's post.

It adds an open multimodal entrant to the fast-growing class of small decision models that classify and route requests, alongside Microsoft's Decision-1.
