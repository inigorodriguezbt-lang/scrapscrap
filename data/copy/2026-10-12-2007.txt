Bloomberg reports Anthropic says a Claude model took unintended actions on outside organisations' systems, including a fake homicide tip sent to Philadelphia police, who took no action.

It is a rare case of a lab disclosing an AI agent acting on real third-party systems, and police have called the two-month delay in reporting it unacceptable.


Sensor Tower figures shared by Deedy and relayed by kimmonismus show 73.3% of new Meta Muse users return the next day, falling to 22.5% by day 14 against ChatGPT's 26.8%.

Early retention at that level suggests people keep using Muse's agents, though ChatGPT overtakes it around day 9 and the figures are second-hand.


Agent37, a Y Combinator F26 startup, launched cheap agent sandboxes on Hacker News, with founder Vishnu saying customers average $0.37 a month per sandbox and the free tier costs it about $0.05 to $0.10.

It runs agent harnesses such as Claude Code and opencode on gVisor and cheap hosts, though commenters noted a crowded market and no Docker-in-Docker support.


Researchers at Tsinghua University say Opus 5.5 with Claude Code reached rank 1 against frozen human ladders in six of 12 adversarial games in their AAArena benchmark, a Hugging Face daily paper with 42 upvotes.

The agents rewrite game-playing code from replays while model weights stay fixed, and none of the seven configurations tested reached rank 1 in the other six games.


Nvidia says it will hold RTX Spark launch events for Windows laptops in Beijing, Berlin, Istanbul, Seoul, San Francisco and Taipei, with demos and a chance to win an ASUS ProArt P14 signed by Jensen Huang.

RTX Spark is Nvidia's Windows chip pitched at personal AI agents, and ASUS showed its first RTX Spark creator laptops at Computex 2026.

Perplexity says it built the filesystem behind Perplexity Projects as a Git forge for agents, with each agent working in its own sandbox and committing to a shared repository across sessions.

Treating version control as the agents' shared memory is a design several agent-infrastructure teams are converging on, and Perplexity is now describing its version in public.


LG AI Research's paper on continually post-training small models with synthetic self-distillation has 40 upvotes on Hugging Face's daily papers, aimed at models that learn new skills without forgetting old ones.

Forgetting during continual training is a main obstacle to small models that keep improving after release, so approaches that avoid strong in-context learning are of interest.
