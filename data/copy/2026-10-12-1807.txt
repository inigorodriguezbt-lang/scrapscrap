Zhipu's Z.ai released GLM-Legal, built on GLM-5.3-Flash, saying it matches frontier models on mainstream legal benchmarks and targets contract review, evidence analysis and compliance. API access is by application.

It is a domain model from a Chinese lab that rivals closed legal tools, though the benchmark claim is Z.ai's own and unverified.


Mistral says Large 4 scores 42% on the Dense 200 visual-grounding test, ahead of closed frontier models, and posted demos of it checking engineering drawings and scanning gigapixel satellite images.

Pinpointing where things sit in an image is a weak spot for many multimodal models; the score is Mistral's own and no one else has repeated it.

https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2109687906751086628


Artificial Analysis launched a leaderboard for FrontierCode 1.1, Cognition's private test of whether a model's change to a real open-source repository would be merged by its maintainers.

Maintainers spent more than 40 hours per task building it, so it checks code quality as well as passing tests.


SoftwareGym, from Shanghai Jiao Tong and Zhejiang universities, synthesised 182 working desktop apps to train computer-use agents; the authors say a fine-tuned Qwen3.5-9B rose from 41.4% to 57.0% Pass@3 on OSWorld.

The authors say they used no OSWorld apps or tasks in training, though the figure comes from their own paper, not an outside rerun.


TechCrunch reports WindBorne, a startup that uses weather balloons and AI to forecast weather, won a US Air Force contract worth almost $50 million for its balloon data.

The award shows the US military buying weather data from a startup that gathers it with its own balloon network.
