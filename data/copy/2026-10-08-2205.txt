Anthropic will scan opted-in open-source projects for bugs, free

Anthropic says its OSS Scanner will use its strongest models to run periodic, no-cost vulnerability scans on opted-in open-source projects, with a proof-of-concept and suggested fix in each report.


Developer squeezes an AI chat agent onto a Nokia 110 feature phone

A developer reverse-engineered a Nokia 110 4G's firmware and turned its Calculator app into a DeepSeek chat client that, they say, sets alarms, places calls and flips the torch.

Anthropic says an astrophysicist, Brice Ménard, guided Claude Science to build the first complete ultraviolet map of the sky, finding existing datasets, combining them and filling gaps statistically in a few days.

Anthropic says the work would have taken humans weeks; the claim comes from its own science blog and has not been independently reviewed.


Developer trq212 says Max plans now include monthly Claude API credits, and shared a clip of an AI homepage he built that regenerates daily from sites he reads and replaces Chrome's new-tab page.

It shows what the new credits are meant for: small personal agents that run on your own reading habits.

Cursor says its new /visualize command answers a follow-up question in the same chat with a fresh chart, and posted a short clip of it working.

https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2108289748628566496


Runway says users can animate charts and explainers with Claude Motion, then bring the result into Runway to generate videos and images, and posted a demo clip.

Anthropic's Motion writes animations as editable code rather than generating video, so a handoff to a video model fills the gap.

Nvidia says its KGMON team placed second in the KDD Cup 2026 Data Agents competition with a system built around making an agent's harness smaller, and published its lessons.

The write-up is a rare account of what held up in a data-analytics agent under competition conditions, from a team that finished second.


Rembrandt, a Show HN project under GPL-3.0, is a free photo editor for macOS, Windows and Linux whose maker says AI masks and edits run on-device, with RAW support for over 1,000 cameras.

It targets Lightroom users, and edits are saved as standard XMP; it had 20 GitHub stars and unsigned builds when we read it.
