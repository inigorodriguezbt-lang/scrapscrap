Anthropic says Claude Mythos 5 designed leaner quantum circuits

Anthropic says Claude Mythos 5, working alone for 53 hours, found a quantum point-addition design with 17x fewer gates and cut a decoder's gate count about 12x against Google's earlier construction.


Postgres 19 slips a month as AI code review turns up defects

PostgreSQL 19 now lands around 19 November, with up to 10 features pulled, after an EDB committer said LLM-assisted review found more defects than developers could fix in time.


Mercor says 90% of US work hours lack good AI benchmarks

Mercor mapped 29 benchmarks onto 18,797 US job tasks and found only 924 covered, which it says leaves about 90% of American working hours without solid AI tests.

Andrew Curran, quoting Anthropic's Michael Sellitto at an IMF panel, says Anthropic now projects Claude will do 80 to 90 percent of frontier-model training work in 2027, against 1 percent in March.

It would be Anthropic's first public projection of how much of its own model development its model will carry; the figure is secondhand and unverified.


rStation, a Show HN project from Dualverse AI, is a shared research lab where independent AI agents discuss open problems such as the Riemann hypothesis and publish reviewed papers while humans watch.

The site points agents to a skill file to join; it lists no pricing and has 31 points on Hacker News so far.


Tencent's Hunyuan team opened Hy-Memory-30B-A3B under Apache-2.0, a model that writes long-term memory for agent frameworks such as Mem0 and MemOS; the model card reports a 55.49 macro average.

The card's own evaluations put it ahead of Claude Opus 5 at 54.62 on fixed subsamples; no independent results exist yet.


Perplexity chief Aravind Srinivas says a Reverse-Engineer-Anything skill from morluto is now inside its Computer agent, with up to 10,000 credits offered to anyone who wants to reverse engineer an app.

It is an open invitation to test the agent on real software, though Perplexity has not said what limits apply.


alphaXiv launched Illustrated NeurIPS, a visual map of more than 9,000 NeurIPS papers that can be searched by topic and opened by cluster, in a post with a short demo video.

It gives a browsable way into a conference whose paper count is too large to scan by title.

TestingCatalog reports a hidden notice in Gemini Notebook reading "You can now create Slides and Videos step-by-step", suggesting chat-driven video and slide generation is coming; Google has not announced it.

TestingCatalog found the string in the app and links it to a possible Gemini 4 upgrade, which is its inference, not Google's statement.


Georgi Gerganov posted a llama.cpp v0.7.0 command for 128GB Macs and Spark machines that serves Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GGUF at IQ4_NL with draft-mtp speculative decoding.

It shows the latest llama.cpp release running a new Qwen model locally with a speculative-decoding mode, though he gave no speed figures.
