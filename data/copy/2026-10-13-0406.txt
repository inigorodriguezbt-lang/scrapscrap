STORIES

Janas runs 80-billion-parameter models on a laptop by streaming experts from SSD

A new open-source C project says Qwen3-Next-80B writes 24 tokens a second on an integrated-graphics laptop with 32 GB of RAM, against 2.8 for llama.cpp, by reading experts off the SSD.


TerminalHorizon fine-tune lifts a 27B model to 55.8 on Terminal-Bench 2.0

The TerminalHorizon paper reports a Qwen3.5-27B fine-tune that gains 14.2 points on Terminal-Bench 2.0 from synthetic long-horizon tasks, beating a rival 27B trained on roughly ten times the data.


Nikkei says NTT is near a deal for Anthropic's first Japan data centre

Nikkei Asia reports, citing unnamed sources, that NTT Data could spend $6.3 billion on an Osaka facility for Anthropic, with a partial opening expected by 2028.


AWS puts OpenAI's GPT-6 Sol, Luna and Astra on Bedrock for five Asian regions

AWS says developers in Singapore, Jakarta, Thailand, Malaysia and Taipei can call GPT-6 models through Bedrock, where each output token uses up 10 tokens of quota.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

SemiAnalysis says AMD landed MegaKernel decode support in upstream vLLM before Nvidia, calling it among the first major vLLM features available on ROCm but not yet CUDA, and cutting kernel launches 10-fold.

If it holds, AMD gets a headline inference feature first, which matters for buyers weighing ROCm against Nvidia's software lead.


David Sacks says Anthropic reports cutting data harvested from Claude in distillation attacks by around 90 to 99 percent, and says that supports his view that labs, not government, should stop distillation.

It ties a policy argument over restricting open models to Anthropic's own security claims, which are not independently verified.


Andrew Fader's Blokkily, an experimental pattern-based music workstation, lets agents control it over MCP; its README links a video of an agent building a chiptune. It had 28 points on Show HN.

It is a small but concrete example of a creative desktop app exposing itself to AI agents instead of bolting on a chatbot.


Arena says it is testing Meta's Muse Wonder Max against Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and others on one-shot 3D scenes and front-end designs, with cost per generation, and that scores are coming soon.

It is the first head-to-head promised for Meta's new model on a public leaderboard, so rankings could move within days.


The thsottiaux account, which posts about OpenAI's Codex, says GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol need different default settings because the 6.1 generation differs, and the Sol default has changed.

It hints that developers moving from Astra to Sol may see changed behaviour without touching their code.
