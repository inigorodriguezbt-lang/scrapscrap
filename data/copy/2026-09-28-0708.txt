Nvidia released Nemotron 3 Diarization, a free 100-million-parameter model that identifies up to eight overlapping speakers in real time, topping VoiceArena's Diarization-Bench with a 14.72% error rate.

The weights are free on Hugging Face and cut the error rate of Nvidia's prior streaming model by 41%, useful for anyone building meeting or podcast transcription tools.


Stanford and Caltech researchers wired OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra directly into a Unitree G1 robot and had it explore, map and tidy an unfamiliar kitchen without a separate trained control layer.

The team built a digital twin in Nvidia's Isaac Sim so the robot could plan and self-correct mid-task, but Astra's latency and cost still slowed it, The Decoder said.


OpenAI's head of applied research, Boris Power, said 80 to 90 percent of the company's research now targets GPT-7, GPT-8 and beyond, calling that where "most of the value" lies.

Power made the comment at the Fellows Forum, arguing each new model generation improves broadly rather than needing patches within a version, The Decoder reported.


Google will retire Gems in the Gemini app starting November 17, automatically converting them into a new "skills" format that launches with a slash command in the chat box.

Skills can be combined several at once and used straight from the main chat, unlike Gems, which required opening a separate side panel, Google said in the Gems manager notice.


A non-LLM decision model called Jev beat Pokémon Red on September 23, coached through dead ends by Claude Opus 5, which rewrote its choices whenever it got stuck, Tom's Hardware said.

Harness logs show 474 fixes were needed to keep Jev moving, including 53 straight tries to walk through one closed door, far faster than LLM agents that took months.
