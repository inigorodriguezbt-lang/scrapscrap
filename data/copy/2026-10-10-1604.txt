Sakana AI proposes MASS, a recursive self-improvement method in which one base model writes, runs and grades multi-agent workflows with no external verifier, then fine-tunes on its own traces, says researcher omarsar0.

Removing the need for a checker would let self-improvement loops reach open-ended tasks; the claim comes from a paper summary and has no independent test yet.
