Anthropic says Claude Science built the first full ultraviolet sky map

Agents downloaded, calibrated and merged data from several space missions, then filled the gaps NASA's GALEX never imaged, and the map's predictions land within about 10 per cent of measurements.


OpenAI bans accounts behind Russian and Iranian fake-news placement ops

A Russian campaign called Dark Clark forged audio and documents that drew official denials in Ecuador and Peru, and OpenAI rates it the first category 5 case it has reported.


Boston Dynamics drops Atlas's pinky in a 13-actuator hand

The new hand has 13 identical encapsulated actuators and no cables across joints, and the company says it lifts more than 100 pounds and is built for mass manufacture.


Warehouse robot renter Ultra raises $62 million, deepens Physical Intelligence tie

The Brooklyn startup says its packing robots have handled more than half a million orders, and Physical Intelligence supplies the AI that lets them learn.

Artificial Analysis says HeyGen Voice takes first place on its Controlled Voice TTS Arena with an Elo of 1,201, ahead of Alibaba's Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Plus at 1,182 and ElevenLabs' Eleven v4 Turbo at 1,166.

It is HeyGen's first text-to-speech model on the board, and it beats ElevenLabs and Alibaba on a test using the same eight cloned voices.


Artificial Analysis ran 30 consecutive edits on one photo through four image models and says Ideogram 4.5 kept most of the room intact while GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst drifted most.

The test checks multi-turn consistency, which single-edit leaderboards miss; GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst ranks first on Artificial Analysis's own single-edit board.

Artificial Analysis says it will soon publish AA-Robotics v0.1, a test of whether frontier language models can control a real robot arm zero-shot with no coaching.

A public benchmark for language models driving physical hardware would give robotics claims a shared yardstick; no results or date were given.

TestingCatalog says Anthropic has put dynamic workflows in Claude Managed Agents into public beta, letting users configure agents so Claude plans and runs a fleet of agents toward one goal.

It moves multi-agent orchestration from a custom build to a setting in Anthropic's hosted agent product; the account is TestingCatalog's and Anthropic's own post was not reviewed.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2108597580435231118


Pine AI says its new Pine Computer scores 78.3% on SaaS-Bench v1.1, against 74.3% for Opus 5 with Claude Code, but finishes fewer whole tasks, 27.4% against 31.1%, TestingCatalog reports.

The checkpoint score counts steps passed, not whole tasks, so the headline lead is narrower than it looks, and the benchmark table is the publisher's own.


Nvidia researchers present ARC, which they say teaches existing robot foundation models to reason without new demonstrations, reporting more than five times higher success on reasoning-heavy tasks without changing the architecture.

If the claim holds, reasoning could be added to deployed robot models without new data collection; the figure is the authors' and the paper was not independently tested.


Elon Musk posted that Grok Bot can now set up its own email address, a step towards signing up for services without a person doing it.

It gives the agent an identity of its own on the web; Musk gave no details on limits or safeguards in the post.


Meta Superintelligence Labs' MIMESIS is a 9B user simulator for agent training that dair.ai says beats Claude Opus 5 by 13.4 points on behavioural fidelity to real users.

Agents are often trained against over-cooperative simulated users, so a more human simulator should make them tougher, though the figures come from the paper's summary.


SemiAnalysis says Nvidia's Rubin gives 3.2 times better profit per gigawatt on vLLM inference, and up to 10 times better performance per dollar than GB300 NVL72.

The comparison is on the widely used vLLM engine and is SemiAnalysis's own analysis, not an Nvidia benchmark, and the thread's full method was not reviewed.


Google's Logan Kilpatrick replied to a user on X that something called Argon is coming, and says he is working on getting the most from it.

Neither Google nor Kilpatrick said what Argon is, so treat it as a teaser until a product appears.
