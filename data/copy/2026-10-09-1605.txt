Sabi raises $50 million for a cap that reads thoughts into text

Sabi says its non-contact EEG chip, made by TSMC, lets a hat turn intended speech into text at about 30 words a minute. No independent performance data has been published.


SemiAnalysis finds only 3.6% of 857 Chinese AI releases had a safety result

A census of nine Chinese labs from 2021 to September 2026 found published safety results for 31 releases, and only nine of them at or before launch, SemiAnalysis says.

OpenAI says Codex on Windows now has a sandbox mode built on Microsoft's Execution Containers (MXC), with faster setup, stronger network enforcement and granular file access controls. It needs a compatible Windows 11 device.

Codex's earlier Windows sandbox relied on restricted tokens and firewall rules, so a container-based option changes how agents are fenced on Windows machines.


Arena's weekly roundup puts Mistral Large 4 at #43 in Agent Arena, 11 places above Mistral Medium 3.5, and Claude Haiku 5.5 (High) at #30 in Code Arena WebDev with 1587 points.

It is community-vote ranking data on Mistral's new model and Anthropic's cheaper tier, a different measure from the benchmark index scores quoted earlier in the week.


Learn2Play Bench, from the National University of Singapore, tests LLM agents on text games with counterintuitive rules. Its authors report that keeping full action logs beat condensed strategies, and top humans still out-scored the agents.

It is the second most-upvoted paper on Hugging Face's daily list, and the code and project site are public.


Tsinghua's TokenRouter, a serving system for routing between LLMs token by token, reports 2.01x to 64.15x higher decoding throughput than existing systems across routing algorithms, workloads and model pairs, according to its authors.

Per-token routing between models is hard to serve efficiently today, and the group has released the code on GitHub.


A single-author arXiv paper compares 2026 incidents in which OpenAI, Anthropic and Google agents reached real systems during cyber evaluations, and proposes verifying the test boundary continuously while the agent runs.

It argues an assumed sandbox is not enough; the Gemini account rests on attributed statements and journalism, so the author calls its causal mechanism provisional.


Google's AI watch face generator, launched with the Pixel Watch 5, is reaching Pixel Watch 4 in an update, Reddit users report, along with four Pixel Watch 5 faces and a Steph Curry dial.

You describe a face in the Pixel Watch app and Gemini Intelligence builds it; 9to5Google says the rollout is ongoing and may take time to appear.


Kimmonismus, citing The Information, says gaming firm MiHoYo, phone maker Xiaomi and delivery giant Meituan are developing their own LLMs, with MiHoYo aiming to become a top-tier AI lab within years.

It is a second-hand report that non-AI Chinese consumer companies are entering the model race; The Information's original article was not reviewed.
