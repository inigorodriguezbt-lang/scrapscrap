Meta opens Muse Gadgets, a DIY kit for building AI hardware

Meta says its Apache 2.0 kit pairs ESP32 firmware and a Linux SDK with its Muse agent, and 5,000 USB-C Home Link devices are due within weeks.


White House names Clayton to lead AI 'Super Intelligence Force'

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton will lead a White House task force with 120 days to report on AI's risks and opportunities, the Wall Street Journal reported.

SemiAnalysis says it expects a Qualcomm Snapdragon chip inside Meta's Muse Charm, the pocket AI device Meta showed at Connect, though Meta has not disclosed the processor.

Meta has said only that the Charm's internals are still being finalised for a December ship date, so the chip remains an analyst's expectation.


Kling AI says it will present at Advertising Week New York on October 6 with WPP and Adobe, ahead of the launch of Kling 4.0.

It is the clearest date-stamped signal from Kling's own account that its next video model is close, though no release date has been given.


Korean media report that Japan's Toppan Holdings and DNP will order glass-substrate manufacturing equipment from Korean suppliers this month, with Toppan planning glass-core FC-BGA substrates in 2028, analyst Jukan relays.

Glass-core substrates are a candidate packaging route for large AI chips, so equipment orders hint at where capacity is being built.


Theo, who builds the open-source T3 Code interface for coding agents, says it gained another 20,000 users since an earlier post and is asking testers to try its latest nightly with Orchestrator V2.

The 20,000 figure is Theo's own and the post gives no baseline, but the open call suggests Orchestrator V2 is still being shaken out.
