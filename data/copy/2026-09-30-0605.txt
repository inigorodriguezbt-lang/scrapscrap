Vals AI says GPT 6.1 Sol now ranks seventh on its index, three places above GPT-6 Sol, calling it an overall improvement on its predecessor.

An independent evaluator's ranking lands within hours of OpenAI's launch claims, giving the first outside read on whether the cheaper model holds up.


A Show HN project, PSSA, is a non-transformer language model written from scratch in Rust; its README says a 1.5M-parameter version beats a matched transformer on WikiText-103 perplexity, 54.4 to 83.8.

The author calls it a research prototype, not a competitor, and it is a tiny model, but it reached the Hacker News front page with 47 points.


Bloomberg reports, citing sources, that US-based PaleBlueDot AI is seeking $600 million in private credit to buy chips for a South Korea site that Chinese app Xiaohongshu would use.

Chinese platforms are buying AI compute through overseas sites, a route that sits outside US export-control rules on chips shipped to China.


The New York Times reports Health Secretary Kennedy told a MAHA summit that AI can offer a second opinion much better informed than any doctor in the country.

It signals how the administration frames AI in health policy, a claim that clinicians and researchers are likely to contest.


The Verge reports Representative Ro Khanna, a California Democrat, is calling for a US-China treaty to keep AI from wreaking havoc, in letters shared exclusively with the outlet, and calls the effort a long shot.

The push arrives as Trump meets AI chiefs at the White House, adding a congressional voice for binding international limits.
