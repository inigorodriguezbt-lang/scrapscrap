Reuters reports Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows a $518 billion-plus AI infrastructure buildout over ten years with six partners, roughly 80% of it non-cancelable or payable regardless of usage.

The commitments dwarf the $4.6 billion in 2025 revenue reported from the same filing, so investors are being asked to back capacity bought well ahead of demand.


Nanyang Technological University researchers propose domain-normalized multi-teacher on-policy distillation, a way to merge RL-trained specialists in maths, coding and instruction following into one model; the paper has 116 upvotes on Hugging Face.

Merging specialist models into one generalist is how labs ship a single model without losing skills, and this paper says teacher assignment is where current methods go wrong.


A Seoul National University paper says vision-language-action robot models struggle to diagnose failures, and proposes recursive harness distillation, in which strong agents find fixes that are then passed on to manipulation models.

It targets a known weakness of general robot policies, recovering when a grasp or step goes wrong, though the abstract's results were not checked here.


WorldPlay2, a paper on interactive world models, says it targets real-time response to varied controls while keeping long-horizon consistency, and names unstable distillation and exploding context as the obstacles it addresses.

Real-time, consistent world models are the open problem behind playable AI-generated environments, and this paper sits on the Hugging Face daily list with 22 upvotes.


Peking University researchers say language models can pass capabilities through task-unrelated text after post-training, extending earlier work on subliminal learning; the paper has 91 upvotes on Hugging Face.

If a tuned model leaves traces in unrelated outputs, data generated by one model may quietly carry its behaviour into the next one trained on it.
