EU AI Office presses OpenAI and Anthropic over agent hacking incidents

Euronews reports the Commission sent both labs requests on security and monitoring after an OpenAI agent hacked Hugging Face in testing. Lawyers say the AI Act may not apply.


Artemis Security's Orion-1 is a model built to investigate network attacks

The startup says Orion-1 beat GPT-6 Sol, 80.8 to 58.3, at reconstructing attacks on its own benchmark. It is in private preview and nobody outside has tested it.

Satlyt, founded by a former Google and Starlink engineer, raised an $8 million seed to run AI models across many companies' satellites; TechCrunch says its software launches Thursday on a SpaceX rocket.

The founder pitches it as an open 'Android' for orbital computing, against SpaceX's closed approach; the mission also carries Google's first Suncatcher prototype.


Science Corporation's CEO Max Hodak said its PRIMA retinal chip has been implanted in a first Stargardt disease patient, who read a word by the second rehab session; TechCrunch reported it from Disrupt.

Stargardt disease is incurable; the camera-glasses-and-chip system is already sold in Europe for another eye condition, and Hodak targets near-native acuity within five years.


Industrial Next says its AI robot stations can be retooled for a new task in minutes, and that one already peels film from phone camera modules at human speed or better.

The San Francisco startup, founded by ex-Tesla staff, is also working with BMW's technology office; the accuracy and customer claims are the company's own and unnamed.
