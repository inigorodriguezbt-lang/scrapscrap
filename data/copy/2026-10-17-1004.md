## DeepSeek releases V4.1 Flash-1017 under MIT, says it tops GPT-5.6 Sol

**DeepSeek says its new open-weight V4.1-Flash-1017 scores 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1, ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol at 73.0 and Claude Opus 5 at 74.0, with a 1M-token context window and an MIT licence.**

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 on October 17, announcing it on X as a "Pareto improvement in performance and cost" over the previous Flash. The company says it is live on its website, app and API, with weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope. Its model card calls it a minor update to V4.1-Flash, and says the weights are licensed under the MIT licence.

https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2111375640717033777

The model card's benchmark table is DeepSeek's own. It lists 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1, against 74.2 for the previous Flash, 73.0 for GPT-5.6 Sol and 74.0 for Opus 5. On Terminal-Bench 4.0 it reports 42.3, up from 31.2, and 29.1 on ProgramBench, up from 20.3. Opus 5 leads on several rows, including 51.8 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, according to the same table.

DeepSeek says reinforcement learning with self-feedback improved its ability to build complete projects from scratch, from game creation to 3D modelling, and published animated demos on X. The model card recommends a 1M-token context window and at least 256K output tokens. It ships a Rust and Python toolkit, deepseek-recipe, for prompt encoding. Nobody outside DeepSeek has yet published independent results.

https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2111375646223933650

Commentator kimmonismus, posting on X, listed prices of $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.60 output off-peak, and $0.30 and $1.20 at peak. The same post put GPT-5.6 Sol at $4 and $20, and Claude Opus 5 at $5 and $25. Those figures come from that post, not from the model card, which this report did not find carrying prices. TestingCatalog also reported the release.

DeepSeek also opened its Search and Fetch APIs to everyone on the same day, at $0.75 per 1,000 calls per endpoint. It says calls made through server-side tools in DeepSeek Harness, Claude Code and Codex are free for API users until billing starts on October 31 at 16:00 UTC. The Hugging Face listing had 308 likes and was the 15th trending model about seven hours after release.
