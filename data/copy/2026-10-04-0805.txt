Microsoft and collaborators posted ScholarEvolve, a paper that proposes agent-harness changes from published research rather than from the agent's own failure logs, writes omarsar0 on X.

Most harness optimisers learn from an agent's own mistakes, so mining the literature for improvements is a different route to better coding agents.


A second Microsoft paper, ActiveSaddler, groups an agent's recurring failures into patterns and treats each as an arm in a bandit so training scenarios keep teaching the harness, dair_ai says on X.

It targets a known weakness of fixed training tasks, which stop being informative once the harness has learned to pass them.
