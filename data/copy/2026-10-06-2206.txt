Hark opens its AI assistant to everyone, with a free tier

Hark, the AI lab founded by Brett Adcock, launched its assistant on 6 October with a free tier. TechCrunch says it runs on a model trained for computer use.


Boston Dynamics names former Amazon Alexa scientist Rohit Prasad CEO

Boston Dynamics has appointed Rohit Prasad, who led Alexa and AGI research at Amazon, as CEO from 7 October, The Robot Report says.


Erdős Problems site freezes comments as AI-written proofs flood in

Maintainer Thomas Bloom says erdosproblems.com is now mostly used to advertise AI-generated proofs, so he is freezing comments. WIRED says OpenAI will release 100-plus solutions.


Artificial Analysis ranks Mistral Large 4 top outside US and China

Artificial Analysis scores Mistral Large 4 at 38 on its Intelligence Index, level with GPT-6 Luna, but says it costs about 4x more per task than similar open models.

SNIPPETS

fal released a Claude connector and a GPT plugin that generate images and video inside Claude or ChatGPT Codex without an API key, and posted a YouTube tutorial.

Image and video models become a tool call inside the chat apps developers already use, with no separate key to manage.

https://x.com/fal/status/2107561234778190131


Arena says Google's Nano Banana 2.1 ranks fifth in text-to-image with 1,328 points and fourth in multi-image edit with 1,431, gains of 80 and 67 points over Nano Banana 2.

The leaderboard gives an outside read on Google's new image model, whose gains over version 2 are measured by Arena's own voting.


OpenAI says its new Meetings plugin takes notes in calls and saves summaries and next steps to ChatGPT Space; it is in beta for Pro and Business users on the macOS desktop app.

Note-taking that feeds into ChatGPT's memory of a user's projects puts OpenAI against dedicated meeting-notes apps; Enterprise access is promised later.


Overmind open-sourced a platform that trains small models on records of an agent's real work. In its own test it reports invented quotes on 0.12% of questions versus 3.36% for a frontier model.

The 28x gap is the vendor's figure from 4,000-plus contract questions, so independent replication will decide whether task-specific fine-tuning beats big general models.


TestingCatalog reports Sesame's voice assistant gained a more expressive model, image generation, connectors and computer use, and can now operate coding agents such as Devin, Codex and Claude Code by voice.

It turns a consumer voice companion into a remote control for coding agents, which would be a new use for conversational voice models.


Anthropic says the Every team built a company agent on Claude Managed Agents, used in Slack, to share skills when a new model arrives, then released it to Every's subscribers.

It is a worked example of a team shipping an internal agent as a product, shown by Anthropic with a full video interview.

https://x.com/claudeai/status/2107574195978641911


Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas says the company halved its Decision API price, now 2 cents per million input tokens.

The cut makes Perplexity's decision-model API far cheaper to try, and it is the vendor's own announcement rather than an independent price check.


EV charging firm Xeal plans to put 100,000 Nvidia GPUs in pods at its US roadside sites, calling it the world's first edge inference network built on idle charging capacity.

Xeal is a plan, not deployed hardware, but it tests whether spare grid connections at charging sites can host AI compute.


OpenAI and Synopsys are developing GPT-Synopsys, a specialised model that will directly operate Synopsys's chip-design software, Tom's Hardware reports, aiming to let engineers deliver more sophisticated chips faster.

If it works as described, a model driving electronic design tools would put AI inside the chip-design loop; no launch date has been given.


Musubi announced PolicyLM-1.7B, a lightweight decision model for real-time content moderation, released with open weights, TechCrunch reports.

A 1.7-billion-parameter model small enough for cheap, fast moderation checks shows where open models are heading beyond chat.


TwelveLabs says Pegasus 1.6 understands first-person video and supports workflows including data labelling for robotics developers, The Robot Report reports.

Egocentric video is a key training source for robot learning, so a model that labels it could cut a slow manual step.


Google's AI Edge Foresight for Mac, a demo built on EmbeddingGemma 2, turns scribbles and offline recordings into full notes, 9to5Google reports.

The app is Google's showcase for running its new open embedding model locally on consumer hardware.
