It turns regression triage into a one-click handoff to a coding agent, though the free credits end within a day.

Researchers say Apple's mail servers had two parsing flaws that let attackers send email as any icloud.com address while passing SPF, DKIM and DMARC; Apple's full patch reportedly took 18 months.

The checks exist to stop spoofing, so a pass would have made forged mail from executives look authentic, and Apple paid $15,000.


Anthropic's Sholto Douglas says models as capable as all humans on a computer are likely within a couple of years, and in the physical world once robotics is good enough.

It is a timeline from inside a frontier lab, relayed by Andrew Curran, and an opinion rather than a measured result.


KAIST AI's World Observer, a video world model, generates a panoramic observer view alongside the agent's view, so objects keep their state after leaving the frame. Code and a project page are public.

Most video world models forget what the agent cannot see; this one is built to track it, and the repository is open.

The round would value the robot-software startup five times higher than a year ago, though the company has not confirmed it.


The Information reports SpaceX's AI unit has held talks about leasing computing capacity to Microsoft, as it embraces the neocloud business despite Musk's initial reluctance, citing sources.

The account mark_k posted that Mercor pitted 12 licensed CPAs against frontier AI on four accounting tasks; he says the humans averaged 37% and Claude Opus 5 scored 100% on all 20 attempts.

Nvidia researchers' Long-Transduction benchmark finds accuracy across seven open-weight models falls 62.8% when context grows from 4K to 128K tokens, according to a summary from dair.ai.

It measures whether long-running agents lose their place in tables and ledgers, even when the context window technically fits the task.
