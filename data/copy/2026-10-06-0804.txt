Aleph Alpha released Kolibri-1, a German-English mixture-of-experts model with 78 billion parameters, about 3.5 billion active per token, under the Apache 2.0 licence. Its model card puts the context at 262,144 tokens, extendable to about one million.

It is a European lab's open-weight answer to Chinese and US models, and the card lists dual 80GB A100 GPUs as the minimum hardware to run it.


Google Drive and Docs now open and edit Markdown files natively, with real-time collaboration and comments, 9to5Google reports. Google says Markdown has become the common language between humans and AI agents.

The change is enabled by default, even on admin-controlled accounts, and Google says it may take up to 15 days to reach everyone.
