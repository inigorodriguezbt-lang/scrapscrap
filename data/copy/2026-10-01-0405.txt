The Financial Times reports Oracle has signed a $7 billion deal to lease computing capacity to Tencent, according to a post by analyst Jukan.

Chinese firms are barred from buying top AI chips outright, though US rules still let them rent capacity in overseas data centres.


SemiAnalysis says Apple's M6 chip puts mixed core types in one shared L2 cache domain, so a third core type, branded P but identified as M, needs no separate cluster.

Qualcomm announced the same FlexCache approach a month ago for Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6; SemiAnalysis says Apple did it without fanfare.


The Decoder reports Gemini 4 Argon uses roughly 62,000 output tokens per task on Artificial Analysis's index, against 27,000 for GPT-6 Astra, despite lower per-token pricing.

Cheap tokens do not mean cheap tasks: Argon's verbosity eats much of the price advantage over Astra, the report suggests.


The Wall Street Journal reports Google researchers raised concerns about AI risks to children, including possible cognitive and emotional harm, as the company pushed Gemini into schools.

The story sits against Google's push to put Gemini into classrooms, and the company's own response was not available to us.
