Researchers at P-Zero Research say their TasteVal benchmark, which scores how well models design experiments, has Claude Opus 5.5 at 2.30 times the human-expert baseline, ahead of Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra.

The benchmark's authors say scores have doubled every three months since December 2025; the result is their own and has not been independently checked.


ServiceNow and McGill researchers say AI judges miss subtle errors in long computer-use runs: on their AgentHorizon set of 1,373 tasks, the best judge, GPT-5.5 in Codex, reached 80.9% balanced accuracy.

Labs use model judges to grade agent runs for training, so blind spots in the judge can feed straight back into the agents.


T3 Code's maker Theo says a nightly build ships large performance gains to its sqlite database, and that the "reconnecting" banners some users saw should now be gone.

The fix targets large databases and slow drives, which is where a local coding-agent app tends to stall first.


A new paper, Vela, turns anonymized behaviour records from 100,000 users into training tasks; its authors say fine-tuning Qwen3.6-35B-A3B lifts user-related benchmarks by up to 11.5 points.

The authors also report an average gain of 4 points on general benchmarks, which suggests the behaviour data does not narrow the model.


The Information reports that OpenAI is in early talks to acquire security firm Trail of Bits, according to a summary posted on X by @mark_k; neither company has confirmed a deal.

Trail of Bits already works with OpenAI on Patch the Planet, which OpenAI says turned up hundreds of issues across 19 open-source projects.


Security researchers say an account uploaded at least 15 fake copies of the Arch Linux helper yay carrying malware; International Cyber Digest says one scan shows it pulling code from a Russian git host and running it as root.

Package helpers like yay run with wide access, so a look-alike name is enough to hand over a machine.


Show HN: Building an LLM from Scratch in TypeScript is a book and repo that builds a GPT-style model in Node.js on TensorFlow.js; the repo has 6 stars and chapters 4 and 5 are unfinished.

It is early, but it offers a way to learn how transformers work without leaving JavaScript.
