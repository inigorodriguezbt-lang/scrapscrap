Arizona court throws out AI video of a murder victim forgiving his killer

An appeals court says an AI avatar of road-rage victim Christopher Pelkey carried 'undue emotional weight' in sentencing, and ordered a resentencing without it, 404 Media reports.


Google's Foresight for Mac turns scribbled bullets into full notes, offline

Google AI Edge Foresight records a meeting, takes your shorthand and expands it from the transcript, and Google says audio and transcripts never leave the Mac. It is downloadable now.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Kling says Kling 4.0 is launching soon, and that it will show filmmakers' projects made with the model at the ACFM festival in Busan from 10 to 13 October.

A launch date has not been given, but the festival slot suggests the video model arrives within days.


Simon Willison released llm-openai-decisions 0.1a0, a plugin for OpenAI's new Decisions API, which returns yes/no, multiple-choice or scored answers for text or images at 10 cents per million input tokens.

It is the quickest way to try the endpoint from a terminal, and Willison says GPT-6 Astra wrote it from OpenAI's docs.


Tom's Hardware reports that South Korean officials suspect AI agents were used in cyberattacks on banks that exposed data from about 25,000 customers.

Officials say AI models let attackers hack with ease even without specialised skills, though the attribution to AI is still a suspicion.


Ars Technica reports that OpenAI will watermark ChatGPT outputs by default, but only for users in the European Union.

The EU is where machine-readable marking of AI output is now expected, so the rule splits ChatGPT's behaviour by region.


Microsoft published Nobel economist Daron Acemoglu's forecast that AI will add about 1.5% to GDP over a decade and replace at most 5% of jobs, The Decoder reports.

It is far below some AI labs' projections, and The Decoder notes the thesis suits Microsoft's strategy of building AI into existing products.


Taiwanese media report that memory maker Nanya Technology has told customers it plans to raise DRAM contract prices by up to 20%, according to analyst Jukan.

The report is unconfirmed by Nanya, but it points to the memory crunch that is squeezing AI hardware builders continuing.
