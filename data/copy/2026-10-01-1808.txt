Trump tells TIME the US might take stakes in OpenAI, Anthropic

Asked why Washington holds a 10% Intel stake but nothing in frontier labs, Trump told TIME: "I might. Maybe I could do that."


Anthropic filing shows Broadcom will lend up to $42 billion

Anthropic's IPO prospectus says Broadcom agreed to lend up to $42 billion by convertible note to help pay for a $125.2 billion, five-year TPU lease, Reuters reports.


OpenAI fires three safety researchers over sensitive information

OpenAI says it parted ways with three researchers for mishandling sensitive information, which the Wall Street Journal reports they shared with an outside AI-safety organisation.


California attorney general serves subpoena on OpenAI over cyber incidents

Rob Bonta's office says it is asking OpenAI more questions about cybersecurity incidents and risks involving its models, and cites last month's Hugging Face incident.


Sony brings AI upscaling to the standard PS5 with QSSR

Sony says QSSR, a lighter version of the PS5 Pro's PSSR built with AMD, arrives today as a graphics option in Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yōtei.


ComfyUI opens its Comfy Agent to every Comfy Cloud user

Comfy says its in-app agent plans, builds and fixes workflows on the canvas, is free to try on Comfy Cloud, and reaches the desktop app in a few weeks.

Accenture shares rose more than 22%, their biggest one-day gain on record, after it reported $22.17 billion of fourth-quarter bookings and guided to 3% to 6% revenue growth for fiscal 2027.

The rally lifted IT-services peers such as Cognizant, IBM and Infosys, and eased some fears that AI would hollow out consulting demand.


The Information reports, citing sources, that Anthropic has begun ending customers' discounts, which typically reach about 15%, once they hit their usage limits, forcing them to renegotiate contracts.

It would be a rare tightening of enterprise pricing at a lab whose demand is reportedly outrunning its compute, and it is still unconfirmed by Anthropic.


Perplexity says its Computer agent now draws interactive charts inside a thread, using TradingView's Lightweight Charts for candlesticks, volume and moving averages when the data is financial.

The agent now shows its answer as a chart you can poke at, not only text, which matters for the finance questions many users bring it.

https://x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2105692220280226296


Elon Musk posted that each SpaceX Star Mind superintelligence spacecraft will have over 250 kilowatts of solar power, about 20% more than the International Space Station.

Star Mind is SpaceX's plan for orbital AI computing, which SpaceX has told the FCC could reach up to a million satellites; the figure is Musk's own claim.


Cryptographer Matthew Green warns that personal agents such as Meta's Muse could carry hijacking instructions from one agent to the next through email, Slack or shared documents, the two halves of a worm.

He argues sandboxing alone may not contain that risk; it is a scenario he describes, and no such attack has been reported.


TinyFish, which sells web APIs for AI agents, opened student and ambassador builder programs that pay gift cards of $50 per agentic app, $20 per skill and $10 per prompt, TestingCatalog reports.

Student builders need a .edu address and get $35 in credit; the company says it raised $47 million in Series A funding.


Commentator Kimmonismus says a senior staff research engineer at Google DeepMind called Bloomberg's report on Gemini 4 "bs", after Bloomberg said the model benchmarks well but does less well in employees' real work.

Neither Google nor Bloomberg has added detail in what we read, so the question of how Gemini 4 performs in daily use is still open.
