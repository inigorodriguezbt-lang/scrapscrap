Tavus says its Griffin video model passed for human 48% of the time

Tavus says 48% of study participants took its Griffin model for a real person after a one-minute video call, against 2% for earlier AI systems.

https://x.com/mark_k/status/2105718951778955317


Perplexity and AWS both open-source small decision models in one day

Perplexity released its OneJev decision models under Apache 2.0, and Amazon's Strands Labs shipped a 2-billion-parameter rival, both claiming speed and low price.


Physicist releases BootLoops, an open toolkit for exact LLM calculations

Harvard's Matthew Schwartz says BootLoops, a free harness for any language model, helped finish 30 Feynman integrals, 15 of them calculations not previously completed.


Microsoft adds streaming transcription and two voice models to its MAI line

Microsoft AI says MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming handles 60 languages with words appearing in as little as 320 milliseconds, at $0.54 per audio hour.

Black Forest Labs' FLUX 3 Image is now live on fal and Replicate, which say it makes 4K images, takes up to 10 reference images and edits several things in one pass.

Replicate is charging half price for the first week, so anyone curious about the model can test it cheaply.

https://x.com/fal/status/2105745492474802514


Elon Musk says Tesla cut memory on its AI5 chip to 72GB and on AI6 to 144GB, calling it the only way to get enough volume for Optimus.

He says bandwidth was held constant and expects a negligible effect on Optimus performance; no test results were given.


Arena says Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro ranks fifth among open-source models in its Agent Arena after 8.1K agentic sessions, up from 13th for MiMo-V2.5-Pro.

The leaderboard rates models on real agentic sessions rather than fixed tests, and the Flash version placed ninth among open models.


Anthropic says work that follows a design, deck or doc started in the Claude app uses 50% less of Pro, Max and Team usage limits until October 15.

The offer applies automatically, and Anthropic pitched Claude Sonnet 5.5 as having a strong eye for design and building slides that need minimal editing.


TestingCatalog reports Grok 4.7 is rolling out in the Grok web and mobile apps as the base model across Fast, Expert, Build and Heavy modes.

The model launched in the API three weeks ago; until now the chat apps ran an older version.


TestingCatalog reports SpaceXAI's Grok Bot is becoming proactive, suggesting helpful things on its own, with users asked to pick a Primary Bot as the rollout reaches them.

It is the first sign of an always-on Grok agent in the app, though TestingCatalog says the rollout is gradual and unconfirmed by SpaceXAI.
