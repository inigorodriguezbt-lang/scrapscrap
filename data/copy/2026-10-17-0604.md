## ChatGPT's Chrome extension can now read and drive your open tabs

**OpenAI's updated ChatGPT extension for Chrome can compare products across tabs, fill in forms and group windows, and it is open to every ChatGPT plan, according to 9to5Google and The Decoder.**

OpenAI has expanded the ChatGPT extension for Google Chrome so the assistant can see a user's open tabs and answer questions from them, 9to5Google reported on Friday. The update is in the Chrome Web Store. The Decoder, which cites OpenAI's own announcement on X, says it works with all ChatGPT plans, though neither outlet gives a rollout date.

Two modes do the work, according to The Decoder. Typing @chrome in a chat targets specific tabs, and chat mode can pull information from several at once. Work mode hands control of the tabs to ChatGPT, which OpenAI says can send messages, fill out forms, download files and group tabs. The full chat history appears in a sidebar beside the page being viewed.

OpenAI's own examples, as relayed by both outlets, are comparing products across tabs by size, material, shipping time and return rules, summarising articles in plain language, and matching price pages from different sellers. 9to5Google adds that OpenAI shows it filling out registration forms without submitting them and adding appointments to a calendar.

The Decoder says Work mode runs on OpenAI's Computer Use model and calls GPT-6 Astra the best on the market for complex, multi-step workflows. That is the outlet's account of OpenAI's positioning and has not been independently tested. 9to5Google likens the sidebar to Chrome's built-in Gemini integration, putting OpenAI beside Google's own assistant in Google's browser.

Several things are not in either report. Neither says how Work mode asks for confirmation before it acts, what happens to the content of open tabs, or what limits apply by plan. Those details may sit in OpenAI's documentation, which this report did not find, so anyone installing it should check what permissions it requests.
