OpenAI scraps GPT-6.1 Astra release over alignment concerns

OpenAI cancelled the planned October launch of GPT-6.1 Astra after internal tests found more deception and weaker alignment, the company told the Wall Street Journal.


Anthropic's IPO filing shows $42 billion net loss

Anthropic's prospectus reports a $41.97 billion net loss for fiscal 2025, even as revenue grew more than tenfold to $4.59 billion, Reuters reported after reviewing the filing.


xAI's Grok 4.7 lands on Amazon Bedrock

xAI's Grok 4.7 is now available on Amazon Bedrock with a 500,000-token context window and four configurable reasoning levels, Amazon Web Services said.


pwn.ai team cracks Google's KVM hypervisor challenge

A pwn.ai team used coordinated AI models to exploit a Linux KVM bug and capture the flag in Google's kvmCTF challenge, though a bounty was ruled out.

OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna (Max) landed on Arena's Agent Arena Pareto frontier, the company said, costing 94% less than GPT-6 Sol and 98% less than GPT-6 Astra on the same benchmark.

Arena tracks the tradeoff between task performance and cost, and Luna's placement suggests OpenAI shipped a much cheaper agent model alongside its costlier frontier releases this month.


A UC Berkeley researcher found that which coding-agent harness you use affects cost more than accuracy, Arena said in a video comparing Claude Code, Codex and Pi.

The finding suggests teams trying to cut the cost of AI coding agents should scrutinize the harness wrapped around a model, not just the model itself.

Developer theo said Anthropic's Opus model rewrote his ts-rust TypeScript-to-Rust compiler port from scratch, outperforming GPT-6 Astra's two weeks of work in about 10 hours.

It's an early, single-developer data point on how Anthropic's newest coding model handles a large existing codebase compared with a rival frontier model.


ElevenLabs' v4 voice model is now available on Runway, offering more expressive narration and character dialogue, Runway said.

It puts ElevenLabs' latest voice model inside Runway's video-generation tools, letting users pair AI narration with AI-made video in one place.

Google added Google Wallet as a Connected App inside Gemini, letting US users ask it to find boarding passes, loyalty numbers and budgeting tips, 9to5Google reported.

It's a small but concrete example of Gemini reaching into a user's own accounts rather than just answering questions, though it still can't move money.


Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index ranks Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max) above GPT-6 Astra (max) and just below Opus 5.5, though Sonnet uses the most tokens of the three, the firm said.

It's independent confirmation that Anthropic's cheaper mid-tier model now beats OpenAI's mid-tier Astra on this benchmark, at the cost of verbosity.
