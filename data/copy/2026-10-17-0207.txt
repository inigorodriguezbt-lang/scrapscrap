Gemini 4 Argon scores 97.5% on ARC-AGI-3, or 49.8% depending on setup

ARC Prize results show Google's Gemini 4 Argon at 97.5% on ARC-AGI-3 with a vendor harness, but 49.8% in the standard one.


Junior share of Anthropic technical hires fell from 27% to 9%

Anthropic's own economists report that junior staff were 9% of technical hires, down from 27%, as agents took on more work.


91% of Anthropic staff expect a Nobel-level AI discovery by 2030

Anthropic's survey of 1,334 staff and 10,980 US adults finds 91% of staff, against 39% of adults, expect AI to make a Nobel-level discovery by 2030.


Jev reaches 35% of Vercel's paid teams in two weeks

Vercel says Jev, a small model from TypeSafe AI, reached 35% of its paid teams in two weeks, nearly double Sonnet 5.5's reach.

OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux posted a video from the ChatGPT Space team, which he says is the actual team working on it, and the post has passed 1,000 likes.

Space is OpenAI's shared workspace for teams, and the post invites users to say what they want built next.

https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2111249534072529256


Tibo Sottiaux says ChatGPT Space gained slides support yesterday, available for now on Pro, Business and Enterprise plans while OpenAI collects feedback.

Slides were previewed at DevDay as a collaborative answer to PowerPoint and Google Slides, and are now reaching paying users.


Anthropic says it has given a team from the nonprofit METR inside access to review its AI models and safety practices, The Decoder reports.

It is an outside audit of a lab's models and processes, and the announcement does not say what METR will publish.


Bloomberg reports Anthropic signed its first data-center lease in Canada, buying capacity at an EverWind project in Nova Scotia worth more than C$10 billion, about $7.1 billion.

It is the first Canadian site for a lab that is buying compute in large blocks, though the capacity and start date are not in the summary.


The Decoder reports a new essay argues about 90 percent of neurons in neural networks serve a clear function, and researchers are still debating whether the thesis holds up.

If true, deception or dangerous behaviour could be spotted in a model's circuits directly, which is why safety researchers are watching the argument.


The Verge reports, citing Nieman Lab, that Meta's new business terms, effective 30 October, appear to let it crawl and scrape sites associated with advertisers, including publishers, to train AI.

The wording is read as covering news sites for free, and Meta has not been quoted in the summary on whether that is the intent.


SemiAnalysis says only about 2.3 GW of roughly 21 GW of US data-center capacity in moratorium areas is likely to be delayed, excluding a Texas pause.

It pushes back on the idea that local moratoriums are stopping the US datacenter buildout, and the full analysis is in its newsletter.


Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas says Vercel developers can now use Perplexity Decider on Vercel's AI Gateway.

It extends the decision model Perplexity open-sourced this week to the gateway where many teams already route their model calls.
