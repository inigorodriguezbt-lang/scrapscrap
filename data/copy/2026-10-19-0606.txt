OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux says the company worked with OpenCode to restore service for users who are also OpenAI API customers and refund credits drawn in an attack, and offers defenders 'Daybreak Blue' access.

The post is OpenAI's own account of the fix; the attack's scope and cause are not described in it, and OpenCode has not been checked here.


Stripe says agentic transactions on its Link checkout rose 38x in September, with payment volume up 20x and retail leading, according to data it gave Upstarts Media exclusively.

Link's head of consumer product, Dan Hill, told Upstarts about 200 agents now use Link, up from about 70 in September.


Artificial Analysis lists DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 at 45 on its Intelligence Index, second among 121 models in its class, at $0.30 input and $1.20 output per million tokens, with a 1M-token context.

The firm says the model is open-weight under an MIT licence; the score is Artificial Analysis's own measurement, and DeepSeek has not been checked on the 1017 changes.


Show HN: Project STROT, an MIT-licensed Python library that uses an LLM once to compile a decision pipeline, then runs a 144M-parameter model locally on CPU, about 60 ms per record.

Its author reports 92.5% on a 398-record routing test against 74.8% for a naive baseline; the benchmarks are self-reported and the repo has 22 stars.


Theo, who makes the T3 Code app, says that over the last two weeks more than 60% of its users who used OpenAI's Codex also used Claude Code.

It is one developer's usage figure from his own app, not an industry survey, but it suggests coding-agent users are not picking a single tool.


Commentator Kimmonismus shared a screenshot of Claude flagging that a user's Mac was running a cryptominer they had not installed, while the user was building a temperature app with it.

The post has about 470 likes, but it rests on a single screenshot and nobody has independently confirmed what the malware was or how it got there.
