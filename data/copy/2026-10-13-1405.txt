Apple researchers published Minions, a three-page paper describing a virtual GPU cloud built from idle Apple Silicon Macs for ML inference. It reports no benchmarks and no code.

If it scales, teams could use Macs they already own instead of renting scarce GPUs, but the paper gives no performance numbers yet.


A NeurIPS 2026 paper argues models can hide arbitrary capabilities that evaluations cannot draw out, by embedding encrypted networks in the weights that run only on a secret trigger.

The authors say audits based on prompting a model may have a fundamental limit; they tested only a small chemistry proxy task.


TestingCatalog says Gemini 4 Argon has appeared on a Google Cloud quotas page, which it reads as groundwork for an API release. Google has not confirmed that.

Google announced Argon on September 30 to a limited group of cyber defenders, so a wider rollout would be the next step.


Mistral says its models are going into orbit, joining Orbitworks and Loft Orbital's AI satellite constellation with Nvidia infrastructure, according to Mistral's post on X.

The satellites are meant to analyse imagery in orbit rather than on the ground, so alerts such as wildfire detection could arrive in seconds.


Anthropic engineers told AI Engineer New York that "flows" let Claude write its own orchestration program for sub-agents, which Latent Space reports costs much more than a single agent.

It would remove the need for developers to define a coordinator and agent roster by hand, though no benchmarks or pricing were given.


Apple says Siri AI is not yet available in the EU, so HomeView will not launch there, MacRumors reported after Apple's event.

The region gap shows that Apple's new Siri-based hardware still depends on where its AI features have been cleared.


ScaleDown says its Constrained Decoding Models read up to 1 million tokens of text and images once and point at the answer, claiming 200 times faster and 467 times cheaper than frontier LLM APIs.

The figures are the company's own and compare against frontier APIs on classification and extraction, so independent testing is still needed.
