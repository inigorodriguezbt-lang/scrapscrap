## Meta's Wang admits Muse Wonder was not trained on 3D demo games

**Meta's Alexandr Wang says the team did not explicitly train Muse Wonder on 3D demo games, after users posted weak Minecraft clones, while developer Theo ranks it level with Opus 5.5.**

Hours after Meta released Muse Wonder, users began posting 3D game demos that fell short of rival models. Alexandr Wang of Meta answered on X. "Whole team feels dumb now, but it's true," he wrote. "We did not explicitly train our model on 3d demo games." He added that Meta should "obviously in retrospect" have done some, and asked people to try the model on real coding.

https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2109728684802523156

Wang also conceded limits. Replying to one user, he said Wonder is not as good as Opus 5.5 on coding, "for sure". He said bugs were being looked into. Another user posted a failed "make a minecraft clone" result. Wang said his own run of that prompt gave a much better answer. He suspects a bug.

Defence came from outside Meta. Theo, who builds the T3 Code agent tool and says he added Muse support there last week, argued that people are discounting Wonder over 3D demos. In his words: "3d game demos are cool! They don't represent real code work at all." That post had 773 likes when the feed was read at about 20:00 UTC on Monday.

Theo's own ranking, at 1,220 likes in the same read, puts Opus 5.5 and Muse Wonder in S tier and GPT-6.1 Sol in A tier. He says he mostly pairs Opus with Wonder, using Wonder for code review and architecture review, and calls it cheaper than Fable and Opus for similar quality. These are one developer's opinions, not a benchmark.

https://x.com/theo/status/2109724741234028639

Cost may be the harder question. The Decoder reports that Artificial Analysis measures Muse Wonder at $4.52 per task on its index, against $1.60 for Muse Spark 1.3 and $5.98 for Claude Opus 5.5. Meta lists Wonder at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output. No price has been published for Muse Wonder Full, and the detailed system card has not yet appeared.
