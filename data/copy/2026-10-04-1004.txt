Aleph Alpha says Chinese models give balanced answers 17 to 41 percent

Aleph Alpha says Qwen, DeepSeek and Kimi answered 967 taboo-topic questions in a balanced way only 17 to 41 percent of the time, against 70 to 92 percent for Western models. The Decoder reported it.

Nikkei Asia reports Toshiba will double its hard-disk production capacity by fiscal 2027 from 2025 levels, its first major HDD investment in about five years, at its Philippine plant.

AI data centres are short of cheap bulk storage as well as memory, and the report ties the expansion to that demand.


TestingCatalog says Anthropic has begun prompting users to share voice conversations for model training, with a screen reading "Allow us to use your voice data to improve our AI models".

The prompt is opt-in and can be turned off in settings, according to the text TestingCatalog posted; Anthropic has not commented in the post.

Both accounts are speculating from screenshots, and OpenAI has not announced the model, so the name and timing are unconfirmed.


Valve's Timur Kristóf presented a year of work moving old GCN-era Radeon cards onto the modern AMDGPU kernel driver at XDC2026 in Toronto, Phoronix reports; Linux 6.19 gave roughly 30 percent more speed.

The work lets decade-old AMD cards use the RADV Vulkan driver, which keeps them viable for gaming on Linux.
