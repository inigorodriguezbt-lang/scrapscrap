HackerRank opens Chakra, an AI interviewer that runs coding rounds on real repositories

HackerRank says its Chakra AI interviewer ran more than 500,000 interviews in a six-month beta. The company claims suspicious-activity flags ran 70 to 80 percent below its traditional tests.

GitHub has launched ReviewBench, an open benchmark for AI code-review agents built from 219 pull requests across 187 repositories and 19 languages, with a public leaderboard.

Code review is a fast-growing agent job, and a shared public test lets vendors' accuracy claims be compared on the same pull requests.


Bloomberg reports that Kling AI, the video-generation business of Kuaishou Technology, has picked banks for a Hong Kong initial public offering worth $1 billion or more.

It would be one of the first listings for a standalone AI video model business, and Chinese AI labs are lining up for Hong Kong.


Bloomberg reports, citing sources, that Moonshot AI, maker of the Kimi models, has closed its final private round at about a $50 billion valuation and is targeting a Hong Kong IPO in the first quarter of 2027.

The reported raise of up to $5 billion would make Moonshot one of the largest AI listings out of China if it goes ahead.


Reuters reports that South Korean president Lee said AI appears to have been used in bank hacks, according to the agency's post on X.

It is one of the first times a head of government has publicly linked AI tools to attacks on financial institutions; no technical detail has been given.


SemiconductorsX says industry reports point to HBM4 memory stacks topping $1,400 in 2027, up from estimates of $500 to $550 in 2026, with SK Hynix's chief executive calling 2027 the worst shortage year.

Memory is the bottleneck for Nvidia's Rubin accelerators, so higher HBM4 prices would push up what data-centre operators pay for AI hardware.
