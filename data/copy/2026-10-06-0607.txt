DeepSeek nears $12 billion round led by Tencent and CATL, Bloomberg reports

DeepSeek is close to raising at least 80 billion yuan, about $12 billion, Bloomberg reports, well past its target and ahead of an early-2027 IPO. Tencent and CATL are reported among the biggest backers.


Kandinsky Lab releases open 29B model that generates video with synced audio

Kandinsky 6.0 Video makes five-second clips with 44 kHz lip-synced audio from text or an image, in 3B and 29B sizes. Weights, code and Diffusers support are public under the MIT license.


Amazon releases diffusion language model it says beats Qwen3 at 2.7x speed

Amazon's ALoDLM spends extra compute only on hard tokens. The authors say the 8B model scores 80.3 across eleven benchmarks against 78.5 for Qwen3-8B, and runs about 2.7 times faster on GSM8K.


SemiAnalysis says Claude plans give about 5x the API value of OpenAI's

SemiAnalysis says OpenAI halved the tokens on its $200 plan and cut cached pricing, leaving Anthropic's Opus 5.5 subscriptions with roughly five times the API-equivalent value of GPT 6.1 Sol.

Artificial Analysis says OpenAI's built-in web search scored 74 on its Search Index, fifth behind Perplexity, Octen, Parallel and Brave, at about $0.05 per task using GPT-5.6 Luna.

It is the first first-party search tool on the board, so OpenAI runs the whole search loop inside one Responses API call.


Bloomberg, citing people familiar, reports Seagate and Toshiba are bidding for TDK's hard-drive magnetic head business in a multibillion-dollar deal, competing over AI data center storage demand.

Magnetic heads are a bottleneck part in hard drives, and AI data centers are pulling storage demand upward.


Kandinsky Lab's 1-billion-parameter Kandinsky 6.0 super-resolution model, which upscales five-second clips two to four times, appeared on Hugging Face alongside its video models under the MIT license.

The upscaler is released separately, so people can add Full-HD output to the other Kandinsky checkpoints or try it on their own clips.


Bloomberg reports Moonshot AI has closed a final private round at a $50 billion valuation ahead of a 2027 IPO. The company had not confirmed the report.

With DeepSeek also preparing an early-2027 listing, two of China's best-known model labs are lining up public share sales.
