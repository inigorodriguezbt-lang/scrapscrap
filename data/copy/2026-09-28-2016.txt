Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told CNBC that AI model distillation is "competition," not theft, pushing back on Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's July comparison of the practice to theft.

Bessent has threatened sanctions against overseas firms that use distillation, making the split a live flashpoint in the US-China AI contest.


Researchers from IST Austria proposed "disaggregated quantization," specializing compute formats separately for LLM prefill and decode, and reported accuracy gains on Qwen 3 and Gemma 3.

Prefill and decode reward different precision tradeoffs, so a technique that treats them separately could cut inference cost across widely used open models.


Developer Glyph argued that current AI products are unserious because they warn users to check for mistakes while giving them no tools to actually check anything.

The essay, which reached 96 points and 31 comments on Hacker News, calls for sandboxing, rollback snapshots and visible sources rather than confident-sounding summaries.


Developer Simon Späti wrote that the real risk from AI coding tools is not worse code but teams losing track of their own system architecture and intent.

The post drew 317 points and 205 comments on Hacker News, among the most discussed items on the site Monday.


Investor Patrick O'Shaughnessy said AI agent startup Instinct is growing transactions more than 10% a day on zero marketing spend, with compute demand doubling roughly every week.

The stats add operational detail to Instinct's $1 billion round at a $10 billion valuation, reached with a team founder Noah Shinn built in under a year.


Chip analyst SemiAnalysis said SpaceX is building an AI chip fab it calls Terafab as fast as TSMC's Arizona plant, with tool move-in expected mid-2028 and volume production by mid-2030.

Concrete is already being poured at the site, SemiAnalysis said, making SpaceX the latest non-chipmaker racing to build its own AI compute capacity.


Third-party benchmark firm Artificial Analysis said Claude Sonnet 5.5 uses about 193,000 output tokens per task on its Intelligence Index, the most of any model it has measured.

That is roughly seven times GPT-6 Astra's usage on the same benchmark, showing the token cost behind Sonnet 5.5's strong score.


Nvidia's board authorized a $150 billion increase to its share buyback program, bringing total authorization to $235 billion, wire service Deltaone reported.
