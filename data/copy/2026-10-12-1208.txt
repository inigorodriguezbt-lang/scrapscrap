DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus reaches Science with nine Erdős problems solved

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus, a Gemini-plus-Lean proof agent, solved 9 of 353 open Erdős problems, two unsolved for 56 years, in a peer-reviewed Science paper.


Microsoft's Brad Smith says a washing machine faces more safety rules than AI

Microsoft president Brad Smith told ITV that AI is less regulated than a carton of milk, and that the UK could lead on rules Washington has not written.

Tom's Hardware reports developer Maurice Heumann used Claude and Codex agents and more than 500 billion tokens to rebuild Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2's source, with 83% of functions matching the original byte for byte.

It is a large-scale test of agents on decompilation, though the byte-for-byte figure is the developer's own and the full rebuild is not independently checked.


Phoronix reports Linus Torvalds is working on a hefty Linux kernel improvement with Claude Code, with its headline describing the change as substantial.

Torvalds has been wary of AI in the kernel, so the maintainer using a coding agent himself is a notable shift in practice.


Vals AI says agent teams cost 1.8 to 5.1 times more than single agents on its Vibe Code Bench, and only one of four team-versus-solo comparisons with GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 showed a significant gain.

The result, from one benchmark of 50 apps, suggests higher reasoning effort can be a cheaper route to better scores than adding agents.


Bloomberg reports Tencent told bond investors its planned offering of about $5 billion will mainly finance AI-related capital expenditure, citing people familiar with the matter.

It shows a Chinese platform company turning to debt markets to fund AI spending, with chip purchases reportedly the bulk of it.


Broadcom chief executive Hock Tan said many AI investors resemble gamblers, with little understanding of what they are betting on, Bloomberg reports.

The comment comes from the head of a major AI-chip supplier, which is a notable voice to question the boom's investor mix.


Developer Theo says Claude Opus 5.5 helped him vibe-code a port of "the language itself" that cut typechecking time threefold; the post had 337 likes when read.

It is one developer's own claim and the post shows a screenshot rather than a published benchmark.


A paper on Hugging Face's daily list says multimodal models keep object coordinates in a linear subspace it calls S-Space; the authors report it had 134 upvotes.

If the finding holds, it gives researchers a handle on how vision-language models represent where things are.
