## Ling team open-sources 560B Ling-3.1-flash under MIT licence

**Ling-3.1-flash has about 560B parameters, 25B active per token and a 1M-token context, and the Ling team says it turns a rough brief into a working prototype.**

The Ling team posted on X on Wednesday that Ling-3.1-flash "is now open source", with about 560 billion total parameters, about 25 billion active per token and a context window of up to 1 million tokens. The team describes it as built for real work, turning a rough brief into a page, mobile interface or interactive prototype that teams can review and refine.

https://x.com/AntLingAGI/status/2110390124743970952

The Hugging Face model card, published under the inclusionAI organisation, lists an MIT licence. It describes a hybrid-linear mixture-of-experts design with 63 linear-attention layers and 9 gated attention layers in a 7:1 ratio, 512 routed experts and 8 activated per token. The card does not state a release date, and the safetensors metadata counts 572B parameters.

The card names the benchmarks the team cites, among them SWE-bench Pro, Terminal-Bench 4.0, FrontierSWE, MCP-Atlas and GDPVal-AA, and describes strong results. It gives no scores in text, and the comparisons appear only in images. Those figures are the Ling team's own, and no independent testing has been published yet.

Running it is a data-centre job. The card recommends SGLang, with a reference setup of 8 Nvidia H200 GPUs, and also documents vLLM. Both projects posted day-zero support. SGLang says MXFP4 and block-FP8 quantisation cut the weights from about 534GB to about 292GB, small enough, it says, to fit on 4 Hopper GPUs.

The release is one of several from the same Hugging Face organisation in a day. Its listing also shows LLaDA2.2-flash, a 103B diffusion language model, and Ming-Image-0.1-Design, a 6B image model for interfaces and posters, both updated in the last half-day. Ling-3.1-flash itself showed about 11 likes at the time of reading, early in its first hour.

Read it on the site: https://theaipost.net/story/find-ling-3-1-flash-open/

## Together and Stanford researchers release agents that reason in latent space

**HybridAgents think in hidden states and act through text, and the authors say they beat frontier agents by 10.4% while cutting tokens by 35% to 55%.**

A research team has released Hybrid Reasoning Agents, models that do their thinking in latent space, the model's internal hidden states, and switch to ordinary text only to use tools or answer. The paper was posted to arXiv on 13 October. Researcher Aran Komatsuzaki credited Together and Stanford in a post on Wednesday.

According to the abstract, a lightweight component called HybridLink moves between the two modes within one generation, and a continuous working memory carries text, latent thoughts and tool results across turns. The authors add two training stages: HybridSFT teaches tool use, and HybridRL learns when to reason latently and when to act in text.

The authors report an average gain of 10.4% over frontier language-model agents and agent frameworks, across 11 benchmarks in mathematics, science, medicine, search and agentic reasoning, at three model sizes. They also claim 35.5% to 54.7% fewer tokens, inference 1.8 to 2.6 times faster and cost savings of 2.7 to 3.7 times. These are the authors' numbers, not independent results.

https://x.com/arankomatsuzaki/status/2110370289334816861

The code is public on GitHub under an MIT licence. It includes two models, HybridAgent-8B-Agentic and HybridAgent-14B-Agentic, built on Qwen3 base models, plus the training datasets. The README reports no benchmark scores of its own and asks users for a Tavily key for the search tool. A mode that renders latent thoughts as pseudo-text is included so readers can inspect them.

The repository showed 11 stars and 2 forks when read on Wednesday, so adoption is early. Reinforcement-learning training defaults to 32 GPUs across four nodes, which puts retraining beyond most hobbyists, though the released checkpoints can be run for batch inference. Nobody outside the authors has yet reproduced the efficiency claims.

Read it on the site: https://theaipost.net/story/find-hybridagents-latent-reasoning/

## Alibaba's Qwen reportedly cleared for Apple Intelligence in China

**China's industry ministry has reportedly approved Alibaba's Qwen and Wanxiang models for Apple Intelligence, days before the iPhone Duo is due to launch there.**

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has reportedly approved Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen (Qwen) language models and its Tongyi Wanxiang image and video tool for use with Apple Intelligence, Engadget reported on Wednesday. Engadget attributes the approval to the South China Morning Post, which Alibaba Group owns. No other outlet has confirmed it yet.

Apple Intelligence has still not launched in mainland China, nearly two years after its US debut. Apple received clearance to bring it to the country in July, according to Engadget's account, but the rollout has not happened. Engadget attributes the delay to China's rules on AI and privacy. Much of the feature set already works in Hong Kong.

The updated filings reportedly also include network access permits for the iPhone Duo and the iPhone 18 Pro. Engadget says the iPhone Duo is expected to reach China next week, with the new Siri AI as a flagship feature. That would make Qwen the engine behind Apple's headline AI features for Chinese buyers, as reports earlier this year suggested.

Reporting from this summer said Qwen would run Apple Intelligence on compatible iPhone, iPad and Mac models and on Vision Pro sold in China. In the US, Apple has worked with Google and OpenAI. Engadget notes that Apple was reported to be seeking Chinese AI partners in early 2025, when Alibaba was already named.

The report rests on one outlet's relay of another that shares an owner with the company named. Neither Apple nor Alibaba has confirmed the approval in the coverage reviewed, and the ministry has not published the filing. Whether the launch next week includes the Siri AI features depends on that approval holding.
