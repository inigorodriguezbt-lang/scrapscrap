Researcher anabology says a Grok Bot named Claudia used Suno, Midjourney and Opus to make a music-video teaser autonomously; Elon Musk reposted the pitch for making music videos with Grok.

It is a showcase of one bot chaining several rival tools, though the demo is the maker's own and no one else has reproduced it.

https://x.com/anabology/status/2110224953287070195


LeWAM, a 17M-parameter JEPA from UC San Diego, ETH Zurich, UBC and Brown researchers, lifts planning success on contact-rich tasks from 28.6% to 89.7%, the paper says.

It learns vision, dynamics and policy in one stage, and the authors report it competitive with Diffusion Policy and π0.5 on three physical-robot tasks.


DoSA, a method from dots studio, speeds DeepSeek Sparse Attention's token selection up to 2.74x over SGLang without changing which tokens are picked, according to its paper.

The gains are in speed only; the authors say accuracy is unchanged, and time to first token at 1M tokens improves about 1.9x.


Blumify, a Show HN project with 66 points, collects interviews and podcasts of AI leaders such as Dario Amodei and Yann LeCun, summarises them and links each quote to its transcript moment.

It lets readers see how a figure's stated views change over time, though the summaries are machine-made and the service has a paid tier.

Wang is the lab's own head, so the banter lands on the lab's own subscribers, who are the only ones with access to Wonder Full.
