Meta's Muse agent app reaches iPad after weeks as the top free iPhone app

Meta's Muse AI agent has spent weeks as the number one free iPhone app in the US, and now has an iPad version that connects to Figma, QuickBooks, GitHub, Zoom and more.


Sesame gives its voice agents their own computers

Sesame's October update gives each voice agent a dedicated computer, a more expressive voice model and links to Gmail, Calendar, custom MCPs and coding agents including Devin, Claude Code and Codex.


Google readies Antigravity for Gemini 4 Argon

TestingCatalog says Antigravity code points to Gemini 4 Argon for Ultra subscribers, alongside an Auto mode to cut permission prompts and an early internal voice agent named Concierge.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2107835952924065925


Insurers brace for claims from rogue AI agents

Verisk's Tim Rayner says 'the buck stops with the CEO' as insurers prepare for claims from malfunctioning AI agents, and a lawyer expects litigation modelled on tobacco and environmental cases.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Maket 2.0, a web app from Montreal startup Maket, turns a chat description into AI floor plans, 3D room renders and PDF or DXF exports. A free tier gives 30 credits.

TestingCatalog says the company has more than a million registered users, and paid plans start at $20 a month for 300 credits.


Krea says its new Photoshop plugin brings Nano Banana, Krea 2, GPT IMG 2.5 and every other model on its platform inside Adobe's editor.

It puts several rival image models behind one panel in the tool many designers already use, though only Krea has shown it so far.

https://x.com/krea_ai/status/2107848434128752777


The dair.ai account, via omarsar0, says it has released MCP tools that let Claude, Codex or Grok Bot search its curated index of top AI papers, summarise them and build literature reviews.

The index covers every paper omarsar0 has featured on X over the past couple of years, and a demo video shows the setup.

https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2107828097433137595


Developer midudev says REA, an open-source MCP server for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor, helps agents reverse engineer programs and games; its package page says analysis runs locally on a Mac.

His post had 824 likes by the time the paper read it, and the tool is new enough that no independent tests exist.


Nvidia researchers describe VERA, which turns benchmark trajectories into more than 9,000 restartable sandboxes with rubric scoring and updates both model weights and the agent harness, according to omarsar0.

The paper's bet is that training the harness alongside the model, rather than the model alone, is how labs now own the full agent stack.


AlphaXiv says Claude Opus 5.5 leads models researchers use for autoresearch on OpenResearch with 34.9 percent, ahead of GPT-6.1 Sol at 25.4; open models account for just 7.8 percent.

It is a usage count from one platform, not a quality ranking, but it shows which models researchers reach for first.


Google DeepMind, Meta and Isomorphic Labs are investing $300 million in Biohub's push to build AI models of living cells, part of a $1.8 billion effort, Axios reported via an X summary from kimmonismus.

Biohub's stated goal is a universal virtual cell that lets scientists test experiments digitally before choosing which to run in the lab.


Hugging Face's webml-community Space runs Google's EmbeddingGemma 2 in the browser through WebGPU, and was the 25th most trending Space with 31 likes.

It lets anyone try the newly released embedding model with no install, since inference runs on the visitor's own graphics chip.
