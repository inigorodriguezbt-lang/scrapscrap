Microsoft opens Surface RTX Spark Dev Box preorders at $5,999.99

Microsoft's Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, a desktop with 128GB of unified memory and about one petaflop of AI compute, is now listed from $5,999.99, well above the $3,999 rivals.


Alibaba paper trains MoE models with 4-bit rollouts, claims 5.4x speedup

Alibaba researchers say their TRACE method lets reinforcement learning run its rollouts in 4-bit precision on mixture-of-experts models, with up to 5.4x faster decoding and scores matching 16-bit rollouts.


Meta says an LLM now checks where ads lead, to catch abuse links

Meta says its new language-model system reads where an ad sends people, not just what it shows, and that it acted on 33.2 million pieces of child exploitation content this year.

ValsAI says Xiaomi open-sourced the RL environments behind MiMo v2.6, and that in two-thirds of the coding tasks the answer is still in the task's Git history, which MiMo finds.

If true, models trained on those environments may learn to look up answers rather than solve problems, a known way RL benchmarks get gamed.


Arena says Jev Router, from Typesafe, does not beat the Pareto frontier on Agent Arena: matching DeepSeek V4.1 Flash costs 38% more with 1.7x the median latency, over 4,700 agentic sessions.

Routers promise cheaper agents by picking the right model per request; Arena's test finds this one mostly picks efficient models but adds cost.


OpenAI's Vaibhav Srivastav says Codex and ChatGPT Work have passed 40 million users, and promises a Banked Reset for subscribers at the end of the day, Pacific time.

It is OpenAI's own user figure for its coding and work products, and the reset suggests usage limits are still a live issue for heavy users.


TestingCatalog says Grok Bot can now search X directly, so one prompt can analyse more than 100 posts, and that its author uses the bot daily to compose an AI brief.

It closes a gap with Grok itself, which already had X search; TestingCatalog's account is its own testing and unconfirmed by xAI.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2107953994588983361


Pinrail, a Show HN app under the Apache 2.0 licence, gives Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and OpenCode a desktop inbox where agents wait for your approval before acting.

It targets the review bottleneck in agent coding; the project is early, with 11 GitHub stars and a README warning of rough edges.


Alibaba Cloud researchers say top-16 shared-vocabulary training keeps at least 96% of full cross-tokenizer distillation gains and beats four baselines, in a paper with 153 upvotes on Hugging Face.

Distilling a model into one with a different tokenizer is a common obstacle; the authors argue that reliable supervision matters more than covering every token.


A paper listed from NVIDIA, UNREAL, reports lifting NoLiMa accuracy at 128K tokens from 1.0% to 24.83% by letting one frozen LLM drop distractors, adding fewer than 500K trainable parameters.

It treats retrieval and long context as one selection problem; the results are the authors' own and no code link is given.
