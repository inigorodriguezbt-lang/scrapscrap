Paper Instruments says its open Ultramarine 1 Flash, a post-train of GLM-5.3 Flash, scored 59.3 on its internal client-deliverables benchmark at $0.13 a task, against 57.8 at $8.19 for Claude Fable 5, per TestingCatalog.

The benchmark is the company's own and unverified, but the claim is a roughly 60-fold cost gap on Excel, slide and memo work for banks and law firms.


TestingCatalog says Claude Code Desktop has a new sessions hub, with live session statuses in the nav bar, for starting, tracking and following up on several sessions at once; it says the design has been in testing for a while.

Anthropic has not announced it, so TestingCatalog's report stays unconfirmed, but it points to the desktop app being built around many parallel agent sessions.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2110455911344931130


SemiAnalysis says Claude Code 2.1.299 adds idle compaction: sessions with 200k or more tokens of context compact automatically after about 54 idle minutes, to refresh the one-hour cache and avoid paying for uncached input.

If it works as SemiAnalysis describes, long-running sessions left overnight or over lunch stop costing a full-price re-read when the user returns.


Meta's AI team says it built a human-led research scaffold on Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Wonder with access to 5 million de-identified health records, and that medical experts used it to do original research.

It is Meta's own account of a health-science test; the results have not been independently reviewed, and the post names no findings.

https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2110441716507693450


OpenAI says more than half of AI use among its EU enterprise and business customers now involves agents, and that small-business users in the region have grown 8x, in its latest Economic Research report.

The figures come from OpenAI's own customer data, so they show how its European customers use it rather than the market as a whole.


Aravind Srinivas says Perplexity Computer now supports ultrafast inference of OpenAI frontier models, with a warning that it drains credits, and that users can tag Polymarket inside it for market research.

The 'credit drain' caveat from the company's own chief executive suggests the fast OpenAI models cost noticeably more to run in the agent.


The Wall Street Journal reports, per a Techmeme summary, that OpenAI's computing chief Sachin Katti is leaving next month after about a year and is expected to stay as an adviser; Bloomberg reports Uday Ruddarraju will lead compute.

Katti joined from Intel in late 2025 to build OpenAI's compute, and the handover comes as the company plans a huge infrastructure buildout.


Axios reports, per a Techmeme summary, that AI infrastructure startup Modal raised a $750 million Series D led by Accel at a $15.75 billion post-money valuation and has passed $1 billion in annual recurring revenue.

Modal rents out serverless GPU compute for AI workloads, so the round is another sign of money chasing the infrastructure layer.


SiliconANGLE reports, per a Techmeme summary, that Salience Labs raised a $125 million Series B led by Bullhound Capital for all-optical circuit switching technology aimed at AI data centres.

Optical switching is one of the routes chipmakers and startups are pursuing to cut the power and latency of moving data between GPUs.


Analyst jukan05 says a slide at the OCP summit shows Nvidia's Rubin Ultra NVL576 using co-packaged optics for scale-up, adding that high-volume production remains uncertain but Nvidia looks set to try.

If it holds, optical links would replace copper inside the rack-scale system, which ties in with the optical-switching funding rounds this week.
