Microsoft says its Decision-1, a Qwen3.5-9B-based model for classification and routing, scores 83.5 percent across 36 benchmarks at 85 ms latency; it costs $0.042 per million input tokens on Foundry and OpenRouter.

Decision models are small, cheap judges for agents, a category Jev started in September; the accuracy figures come from Microsoft's own benchmark.


Theo posted a video of OpenAI's Codex remote experience freezing, saying two submissions sat unsent for minutes, and said he was going back to T3 Code.

It is one developer's hands-on complaint about a new remote coding workflow, not a measured reliability test, but it is the kind of friction rivals pitch against.

Apple disclosed in a European Commission filing that it will make offers to some Huxe AI employees and take a non-exclusive license to the shuttered podcast startup's technology, TechCrunch reports.
