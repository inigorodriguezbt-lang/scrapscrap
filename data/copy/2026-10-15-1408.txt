StepFun opens weights for 600B-parameter Step 5 Preview

StepFun says its agent-focused Step 5 Preview, a 600B mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token window, is downloadable now, with vLLM and SGLang support from day one.

https://x.com/vllm_project/status/2110719689127292945


Swiss Apertus 1.5 takes images and audio, with a 262,144-token window

The Swiss AI team's release notes describe 8B and 70B open models under Apache 2.0, trained on data it calls permissively licensed, while admitting they still trail frontier open weights.


Amazon's Bee adds a $75 wristband and a phone-back recorder

Amazon says its Bee Frontier listens all day for $74.99, with seven days of battery, and a $11.99-a-month Bee Pro plan hooks it into Alexa and Amazon shopping.

Amine Dirhoussi says tree packing has landed in TRL's AsyncGRPO, forwarding each unique token once and cutting trainer compute 26 times to reach the same reward on one agentic run.

Agentic rollouts share long histories, so the saving could matter to anyone training agents, though the figure comes from one MiMo Envs run reported by the authors.

https://x.com/DirhousssiAmine/status/2110642116716351663


OpenCode says DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is now free in its coding tool. The post had more than 14,000 likes when read on Thursday afternoon.

A free tier for a fast open model lowers the cost of trying agentic coding, though OpenCode's post gave no limits or end date.


Anthropic opened applications for two fellowships: STEM Fellows and Conceptual Reasoning Fellows, four months from January 2027, helping Claude reason about hard problems in their fields. Applications close October 28.

The second track recruits from philosophy, cognitive science, economics, policy and law rather than engineering, according to Anthropic's own description of the roles.


Google DeepMind says its SynthID watermark has been named one of Time's Best Inventions of 2026, and that more than 180 billion images and videos are already watermarked with it.

The 180 billion count is DeepMind's own figure, and the award recognises the watermarking technology rather than any independent test of how well it works.


Engadget reports Adobe has rearchitected Photoshop, Premiere and Substance 3D for Nvidia's RTX Spark chip, and says the apps run twice as fast for AI tasks and editing.

The doubling is Adobe's claim, without per-app benchmarks, and Engadget could not say when the updated apps will ship.


Bloomberg reports New York City Comptroller Mark Levine wrote to Anthropic chief Dario Amodei on Wednesday urging the company to pause its initial public offering until it has addressed AI risks.

The letter adds outside pressure on a listing, though Anthropic's response was not part of the Bloomberg video summary read here.


Axios reported a scoop that the US Federal Trade Commission has demanded documents from AI giants over recent safety incidents, according to the outlet's post on X on Thursday morning.

The FTC has announced no enforcement, and a demand for documents does not mean the agency has found any violation.
