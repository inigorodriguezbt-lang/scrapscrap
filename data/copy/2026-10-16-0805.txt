NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a 120B-total, 12B-active multimodal mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context, taking text, image and video, listed under the OpenMDW-1.1 licence.

It is an open vision-language model aimed at long-running agents, with a hybrid Mamba-2 and attention design and an NVFP4 variant for cheaper serving.


Alibaba Cloud researchers say Waypoint-OPD, a distillation method that down-weights conflicting reinforcement-learning signals, scored highest in all six Qwen3 student settings they tested; code is on GitHub.

It targets a cheap way to pass RL gains from a small teacher to larger students, with one extra scoring pass.


Authors of 3DCodeVerse say fine-tuning on their 2-million-program dataset lifted a 9B model's executable 3D code rate from 0.0% to 84.9%, and promise to release data, harness and models.

The paper claims its agent harness beat Claude Design in 90% of human comparisons, though nothing is downloadable yet.
