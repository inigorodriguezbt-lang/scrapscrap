EverMind AI's open-source Raven agent system, which builds and evolves a separate harness per model and domain under a host agent, has 5.2k GitHub stars under an Apache 2.0 licence, according to its repository.

Its benchmark claims, such as top SWE-bench results for Raven-Code, come from EverMind's own README and have not been independently tested.


Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman said on The Rest Is Politics: Leading podcast that an AI rewriting its own code with less human scrutiny is the risk behind a recent big Anthropic resignation.
https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2106929459442192743

He is naming a rival lab's departure as evidence for recursive self-improvement risk; he did not say who resigned, and Anthropic has not commented in the clip.


Developer Theo says he is building t3os, a headless, Ubuntu-based operating system for personal servers that he describes as made of tech choices agents prefer, and not meant for humans.

An OS built around what coding agents prefer rather than people is a sign of agent-first tooling spreading; no code or release date has been shown.


A UT Austin study of nearly 35,000 coding-agent runs found, as summarised by Elvis Saravia, that trimming context to about a third of the tokens can make Qwen agents 20 to 80 percent slower on Terminal-Bench.

Cutting tokens is the standard way to save money on long agent sessions, so a result showing it can cost time matters to anyone tuning agents.


Analyst Jukan, citing The Wire China, says China's memory maker YMTC expects the NAND shortage to last another three years, a longer outlook than many chip models assume.

It adds a Chinese producer's voice to the view that AI-driven memory demand will keep storage prices high well into 2029; the underlying report was not opened.


SemiAnalysis says Nvidia's support for non-NVIDIA chips worsened after it bought SLURM maker SchedMD, and asks whether Hugging Face will follow the same path now that Nvidia has acquired it.

It is an opinion from a chip-analysis firm, not a finding, but it voices open-model developers' worry about neutrality under Nvidia ownership.
