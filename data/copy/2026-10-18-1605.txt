Hugging Face's Thomas Wolf estimates, by his own napkin math, that Meta, NVIDIA, Alibaba, DeepSeek and about 30 other labs have shared close to $1 billion of training compute on the Hub, up from under $40 million in 2023.

He calls it a floor, since roughly half the figure comes from official compute numbers and the rest from educated guesses, and it excludes about 900,000 community fine-tunes.


Andrew Curran says OpenCode, under a sustained cyberattack, found the frontier models it used for defence refusing to help fight attacking agents, because analysing the attacks tripped their safety guardrails.

It is the same failure Hugging Face reported in July, and suggests safety refusals still get in defenders' way; OpenCode's own account was not available to us.


Developer mark_k says the new tab-completion feature in OpenAI's Codex is "magical": it anticipates the next step he would take and lets him accept it by pressing tab.

It is one user's early impression of a feature that OpenAI has not detailed in anything we opened, so treat the praise as anecdote.
