STORIES

Google opens Playground, a chat box that builds playable games

Google says its new Playground turns a typed description into a playable browser game, 2D or 3D, with leaderboards. It is US-only, 18 and over, with access tiered by Google One plan.


Google opens SynthID Detector to everyone, and it reads OpenAI and Nvidia marks

Google says anyone can now upload an image, video or audio clip to a public site and check it for an AI watermark. It reads Google, OpenAI, Nvidia and Kakao marks.


Nvidia says a fine-tuned Nemotron beat the top human score at IOI 2026

Nvidia says a 550B-parameter Nemotron 3 Ultra scored 535.4 of 600 at IOI 2026, above the best human's 498.27, and 30 of 42 at the maths olympiad. It published models, data and code.


System76's COSMIC desktop bans LLM-written code as GNOME debates AI bug reports

The Register reports COSMIC contributors must now declare their pull requests hold no LLM-generated code. GNOME's Michael Catanzaro says AI-found bugs are too valuable to refuse.

Push Square reports PS5 modders armed with AI tools have installed Switch and Xbox emulators on a new jailbreak that works on firmware 7.00 to 13.60, though games needing 14.00 stay locked.

The report says the exploit has set off a run on used consoles with vulnerable firmware, and Sony has already patched some earlier holes.

The Rust deep-learning framework Burn shipped 0.22.0 on 6 October, which its developers say rebuilds transformers up to 14.73 times faster and adds LoRA and QLoRA fine-tuning and ONNX export.

Burn removes backend type parameters from user code, so the device now selects execution, which the maintainers say also cuts peak VRAM by up to 49.2% on CNNs.


Mirror Particle's chief executive Abhivyakti Ahuja says the San Francisco startup built a world model from scratch to predict why people act as they do, arguing fine-tuned LLMs are the wrong tool.

The company, which TechCrunch says competes at Disrupt's Startup Battlefield on 13 to 15 October, has not published results, so the claim is untested.
