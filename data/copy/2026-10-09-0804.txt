Xiaomi's MiMo team published MiMo-V2.6, an omni-modal model family trained by scaling reinforcement learning, with batches of 1,568 samples and 2.7 to 3.7 billion tokens per step at contexts up to 1M.

The paper reports no benchmark results and promises to open-source the training dynamics, RL environments and framework; it does not itself announce model weights.


Learn2Play Bench, from the National University of Singapore, tests whether LLM agents can learn the rules of new text games from play; its authors report top human players outscore the agents tested.

The authors also find that keeping full action-and-feedback records beats summarising experience into rules, and that swapping the agent harness alone can raise scores and cut cost.


The vLLM project says its Semantic Router team shipped Decision 2.0, which answers multiple questions about one input in a single forward pass, returning per-option probabilities for routing and classification.

Routing cheap queries to small models and hard ones to large models is a main way serving costs are cut, and vLLM is the engine many self-hosted deployments run.


Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang says Muse, the company's personal agent, launched a month ago today and drew a response that surpassed anything he could have imagined; Meta gave no user numbers.

Muse and OpenAI's Dots, introduced on 29 September, are now the two big consumer agents, both running on cloud computers of their own.


Reuters video shows residents of Thane, next to Mumbai, objecting to a 22-hectare Amazon data center, roughly the size of 41 football fields, being built near their homes.

Local opposition to AI-era data centers is spreading beyond the US and Europe, adding delay risk to the cloud build-out in India.

Korean media reports cited by the analyst account jukan05 say Samsung has secured long-term agreements covering 75 to 80 percent of next year's memory supply, including HBM, with Nvidia, Google and Microsoft.

If accurate, memory for AI servers is largely sold out before 2027. SK hynix and Micron are reportedly signing similar contracts; none of it is confirmed by the companies.
