fal says H3 Max Relight lets users upload a video, design lights in a built-in studio, and relights every frame while keeping the subjects, motion, camera movement and audio.

It turns lighting into an edit after the shoot; fal's claim that H3 Max is the top video model is its own and untested.

fal says Vidu Q4 is live, with image-to-video from one first frame and native audio, plus reference-to-video using up to 12 images and 3 voice clips, in clips of 3 to 16 seconds up to 4K.

Reference voices and images are meant to keep characters consistent across shots, a common weak spot in AI video.

https://x.com/fal/status/2107930326139867264


OpenAI says ChatGPT plugin extensions let developers launch plugins from the sidebar, support @ mentions and add file viewers; it names Adobe, Canva, Figma, Shopify, Instacart and Atlassian among those building.

It moves plugins from chat add-ons toward first-class parts of the ChatGPT interface, and OpenAI has rebuilt its submission flow for review.

The comparison is against vLLM's own day-zero release three weeks ago, and the project, not SemiAnalysis, published the figures.
