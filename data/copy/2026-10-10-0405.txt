2026-10-10 04:05 UTC

STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Cloudflare opens weights for Clef-omni, a model that answers by scoring options

Cloudflare released Clef-omni under Apache-2.0, a 30B-A3B model that takes audio and video and returns a probability for each option, and cut Clef-flash's price by more than half.


Anthropic lets Claude run up to 1,000 sub-agents in parallel

Anthropic says its new dynamic workflows for Claude Managed Agents caught 66 of 70 hidden bugs in a test, where a single agent found 14 to 27.


Google removes the model picker for free Gemini users, leaving only Auto

Free Gemini users can no longer pick a model and get Auto, which Google says routes prompts to Flash-Lite, Flash or Pro, while subscribers get effort levels.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Tom's Hardware reports Artificial Analysis scores Mistral's trillion-parameter Large 4 at 38, the best model outside the US and China but behind open models from Xiaomi, Z.ai, Moonshot and DeepSeek.

It is the first independent index placing Mistral's flagship against Chinese open-weights rivals, which currently lead Europe's best on that measure.


9to5Mac reports the EU's Digital Markets Act database lists Huxe, a shuttered audio app from three ex-NotebookLM developers, as Apple's newest acqui-hire, with Apple receiving a non-exclusive licence to its IP.

It is at least the fourth AI-related Apple deal disclosed this year, and Apple has not said what it plans to build.


Ryan Dahl says Cloudflare is acquiring Deno and will support the runtime for one more year, then end development, while Deno stays open source, Simon Willison reports.

Cloudflare plans to build on celld, Deno's open-source Durable Objects implementation, to make self-hosting its workerd runtime a first-class option.


In a guest post on Terence Tao's blog, Thomas Hales says several Lean kernel soundness bugs found this summer let users prove false results, and urges human audits of AI-produced formal proofs.

With AI systems now generating millions of lines of Lean, Hales argues trust must rest on independent kernels and a human check of each statement.


The Verge says more than three dozen mathematicians described the flood of results OpenAI dropped on the field this week as staggering and overwhelming, and say it will take years to digest.

The reaction is from mathematicians who spoke to The Verge; the results themselves have not yet been checked in full.


Cactus Compute says Whistle, its open speech-to-text model, is 16.9 MB, runs on CPU in seven languages, and beats Whisper base on several benchmarks while losing on TED-LIUM and AMI.

At roughly a ninth of Whisper base's size, it targets phones and watches, though the page names no licence and these are Cactus's own results.
