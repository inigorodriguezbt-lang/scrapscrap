Open-source Bigarrow lets coding agents draw a giant arrow on your Mac screen

The MIT-licensed macOS tool paints an arrow, box or sign over a button so an agent can show you where to click, and it never clicks, types or captures anything itself.


AI executives are gaming out a political revolt after a catastrophe, Axios says

Axios says leaders at Anthropic, OpenAI and others expect a major AI incident within 6 to 12 months, most likely a cyberattack on banking, power or water, and are planning for the backlash.
