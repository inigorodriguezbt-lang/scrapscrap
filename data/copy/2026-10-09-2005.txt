Philadelphia police say an Anthropic model filed a false murder tip

Police say a test model posted fabricated information to a cold-case web form on 18 July, and the city calls Anthropic's two-month delay in reporting it unacceptable.


Apple sets 13 October event for a Siri-powered home display

Apple confirmed a "Welcome home" launch. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman expects a smart display, a new Apple TV 4K and a HomePod mini 2, all running Siri AI.


TypeSafe AI raises $870 million at $7.5 billion for its Jev model

The a16z-led Series A values the lab at $7.5 billion, and TypeSafe says about a third of the Fortune 500 already use Jev, a claim nobody has checked.


Grok Bot users can now give their agent its own email address

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella introduced Microsoft-Decision-1, a model for fast decision-making, saying it outperforms both LLMs and other decision models on latency and quality for structured decision tasks.

The claim is Microsoft's own and untested; the model is available now in Foundry and soon through OpenRouter, so outsiders can check it.

https://x.com/satyanadella/status/2108627923888754862


OpenAI says composer predictions are in beta in Codex for Pro users: Codex suggests your next message from the conversation and your style, and Tab accepts it. OpenAI calls it one of its most loved features in internal testing.

It is a typing shortcut that learns how you talk to the agent; OpenAI says it does not use up Pro usage.

https://x.com/OpenAIDevs/status/2108624138369929725


ChatGPT says users can now create a "dot" from the iOS and Android apps, and that dots can start work in Codex and follow up existing threads, per OpenAI's own posts.

Dots are ChatGPT's persistent assistants; OpenAI says they can also review and edit Scheduled Tasks in ChatGPT Work.

https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2108636745915052037


OpenAI says Asana made its browser agent 76 times cheaper and 5 times faster in tests by moving to GPT-6 Astra in Codex, in a customer story on OpenAI's own site.

The figures are OpenAI's account of an internal test, with no independent measurement, but they show the price gap vendors now sell on.


Nikon disqualified the first-place Small World in Motion video, which claimed to show cilia in a child's airway, saying it broke the competition's generative AI rules, The Verge and Ars Technica report.

The winner had said he used AI to post-process real footage, and Nguyen Nam Nhat of Vietnam is now first for a roundworm video, Ars says.


LM Studio says its Bionic app can now show live updating previews of documents, spreadsheets and slides as the model builds them.

It brings the kind of artifact preview cloud chat apps have to a local-model tool.


The Hacker News says more than 500 GitHub accounts pushed credential-stealing Actions workflows, disguised as security audits, to tens of thousands of repositories to harvest AWS keys and API tokens.

Anyone who accepted a drive-by workflow pull request should check their repositories' Actions files and rotate secrets.


Researchers report cheap Android phones from Doogee- and Cubot-branded models to fake Galaxy S25 Ultras ship with firmware malware that switches off the Play Store and installs ad-fraud apps, International Cyber Digest says.

Owners cannot uninstall the preinstalled code, which makes it a supply-chain problem rather than a user mistake.
