Huntress has not confirmed any data was taken, but a backup tool with broad read access to customer tenants is a high-value target for attackers.


Researchers behind GracefulRL say their training method cut a model's incorrect AIME answers from 79.1% to 8.9% while losing 3.1% of correct ones, and lifted Minesweeper success from 67.4% to 77.4%.

The method rewards safe fallbacks over wrong guesses without teaching models to refuse everything, targeting the incentive that pushes reinforcement-trained models to bluff.
