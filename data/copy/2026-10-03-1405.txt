Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri-1, a 78B open-weights model with 3.5B active

The German lab's Apache 2.0 model reads 262,144 tokens, was trained on 20 trillion tokens of English, German and code, and its card says it runs on two 80GB A100 or H100 GPUs.

Bilibili's Index team released Index-Translate-35B-A3B-preview, an Apache-2.0 mixture-of-experts translation model with 3B active parameters, which its card says covers 150 languages and tops compared systems on FLORES.

The team's own card reports a 0.8794 FLORES COMET-22 score; those figures are the developers' claims and not independently checked.


Offrun, a free Mac app shown on Hacker News, runs Claude Code, Codex, Gemini's AGY and Grok Build side by side in separate git worktrees, with a second agent reviewing each diff before merge.

It works with agent subscriptions users already hold and switches accounts when limits hit, though the site does not say whether the code is open source.
