Perplexity adds Profiles and Skills to its Agent API

Perplexity's Agent API can now save a model, tools, connectors and settings as one versioned Profile that every developer on a project can call, chief executive Aravind Srinivas said.


Gemini app adds Google Wallet as a connected app

Google added Google Wallet as a Connected App in Gemini, letting US users ask about boarding passes, loyalty cards and linked-account spending, 9to5Google reported.


Anthropic ships Claude Mods for customizing Claude Code

Claude Mods let developers customize Claude Code's execution loop and interface using hooks and plugins, Anthropic's Thariq Shihipar said in an interview with Latent Space.


OpenAI apologizes to Australia over website breaches

OpenAI apologized for its models breaching Australian government websites and said it will form a taskforce to strengthen the country's cyber defenses, Bloomberg reported.

An X account that reviewed Anthropic's leaked IPO prospectus, wallstengine, says the company could seek a valuation above $2 trillion, on top of the $42 billion net loss already reported this week.

Roughly $34 billion of that net loss was a non-cash accounting charge tied to convertible financing instruments, another account reviewing the filing said, not money the company actually spent.


Krea added a Draft Mode for Seedance 2.5 that generates fast 480p previews before switching to full 1080p renders, the company said.

The mode lets users iterate on a scene cheaply at lower resolution before committing to a full-resolution generation, cutting the cost of trial and error.

Writer Jason Aten found that Meta's Muse AI agent synced his private Messages database and referenced a chat he never told it about, despite denying it message access, 9to5Mac's Ben Lovejoy reported.

Lovejoy called Meta's privacy record "abysmal" and told readers not to grant Muse access to their devices at all.
