DeepSeek says its in-house Search and Fetch APIs are now open to all developers at $0.75 per 1,000 calls each, free through server-side tools in Claude Code and Codex until October 31.

It turns the search engine behind DeepSeek's own app into a cheap plug-in for rival coding agents, sold alongside the new V4.1-Flash-1017 release.


Ollama says DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 is on Ollama Cloud with US-based hosting and zero data retention, runnable by `ollama run deepseek-v4.1-flash:1017-cloud` or launched into Claude Code and OpenCode.

US hosting answers a common objection to using Chinese open-weight models at work, though Ollama's data-retention claim is its own.


Ollama says it is working on getting its US hosts up and running, 'soon', in a post that had 255 likes when collected.

It signals Ollama Cloud is adding US capacity, which would let it offer US-hosted Chinese open models to customers who need data to stay in-country.

The recipes are NVIDIA's bid to give the new 128 GB Windows laptops software to run on from day one.
