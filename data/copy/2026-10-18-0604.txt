The Decoder reports that Anthropic says Claude worked out in under 30 minutes that the NSA-supplied seeds behind NIST's standard elliptic curves came from the C generator lrand48, seeded with 1997-98 timestamps.

Anthropic says the finding makes a deliberate backdoor less likely but is not a cryptographic break; it published CC0 code so others can reproduce it.
