Reflection's Beam, a 501B open model, ships under Apache 2.0

Reflection's first model, Beam, has 501 billion parameters, a 1 million-token window and an Apache 2.0 licence. Artificial Analysis ranks it second among US open-weights models, behind only Meta's Muse Spark Open.


OpenAI offers US university labs 30 free GPT-Rosalind seats

OpenAI's new Rosalind Academic Plan gives approved US university labs up to 30 free seats on its life-sciences model for up to 12 months, with a Workbench that links to chemistry and genomics tools.

Four Gemini 4 Argon agents beat Minecraft's Ender Dragon in 27 hours 14 minutes, writing 2,783 programs and dying 605 times, according to a paper by Faldor, Singh and Raileanu.

The authors call it the first end-to-end autonomous Minecraft completion by AI agents, with no human-written game tools; the claim has not been independently checked.


Nvidia and IIT Madras say Nemotron-pretraining-Indic-v1 holds about 2.8 trillion tokens across 22 Indian languages, and that an open data-generation pipeline will ship with it soon.

Many of the 22 languages, such as Bodo, Dogri and Santali, have little web text, so synthetic translation is how the corpus reaches them.


IBM says Granite Switch 4.1, in 8B and 30B sizes, runs chat, decisions and speech-to-text from one endpoint, and claims its embedded speech model tops the OpenASR Leaderboard.

Adapters built into one model are meant to avoid running three separate models, which IBM says costs about the same VRAM as the base model alone.


Analyst Jukan, citing DigiTimes and Korean media, reports Samsung will break ground on a second Taylor, Texas fab in November, targeting mass production in 2030, with the first fab due late 2026.

It is a single-outlet report relayed on X; Samsung has not confirmed the schedule in anything this paper has seen.


International Cyber Digest says Bitsight reports an autonomous hacking agent called MONST3R-AI, sold on a cybercrime forum, runs on buyers' stolen OpenAI, Anthropic and Google API keys.

The key owner pays the bill, so the seller's compute costs fall on victims; this paper has not seen Bitsight's own report.


International Cyber Digest says suspected Chinese spies used a zero-day, CVE-2026-102764, to hack BeyondTrust Remote Support servers, one hosted by BeyondTrust, whose third-party triage tool closed the alerts as false positives.

If accurate, an automated triage tool buried the first warning on a vendor's own server; the account has not been confirmed by BeyondTrust here.


Reflection says it will award $10,000 in Hugging Face Hub credits to the best projects built with its new Beam model, with entries shared on the model's discussions page.

The prize is how the lab hopes to get outside demos of a model whose benchmark numbers so far come from Reflection itself.
