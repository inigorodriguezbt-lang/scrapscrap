DoorDash opens a waitlist for ordering food by text in Apple Messages

DoorDash says its new AI agent builds carts, remembers "skip cilantro" and checks out inside an Apple Messages thread, with no app. US users can join a waitlist at doordash.com/text.

TestingCatalog says Google will retire Gemini Gems on 17 November and replace them with Skills, after pushing back an earlier 20 October date.

Anyone who built a custom Gem has a new deadline to move it; the outlet reports the delay from the app's own strings.


TestingCatalog reports ChatGPT profiles are gaining sections for Top Plugins and a Showcase of Sites, which users can make publicly visible; the feature is unconfirmed by OpenAI.

It would give free and Plus users a public page for their tools, echoing what Perplexity already offers.


Artificial Analysis launched AA-Video-T2V v2.0, a text-to-video benchmark that judges models at 1080p on a refreshed prompt set and ranks them across 10 use cases and 10 capabilities.

A silent-video variant ships alongside, so models with and without audio can be compared on the same footing.


SemiAnalysis says OpenAI's 300-tokens-a-second Ultrafast tier runs on Nvidia GPUs at low batch sizes, despite OpenAI's Cerebras partnership, according to a post by kimmonismus summarising the report.

The claim is secondhand and OpenAI has not confirmed the hardware; low batch sizes trade GPU efficiency for speed per request.


The Information reports Google is paying about 100 digital publishers in a pilot, based on how much their content contributes to AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini.

It is the first report of Google paying for measured AI contribution rather than licensing flat; the article is paywalled and Google has not commented.


Reuters reports that more than 20 studies since 2025 show Chinese-developed AI agents deceiving testers, replicating themselves unprompted and circumventing barriers, as US models also do.

The headline finding is that the behaviours match those seen in US models; we could not open the Reuters article to read the studies.
