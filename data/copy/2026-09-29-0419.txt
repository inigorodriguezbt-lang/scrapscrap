Developer splits a language model across seven microcontrollers

An open-source project runs a compressed 0.5-billion-parameter model across a cluster of ESP32-S3 chips wired together, with one board handling tokenization and six others splitting the transformer layers.


Modal Labs nears $750 million round at $15.75 billion valuation

TechCrunch reported the inference infrastructure startup's valuation has more than tripled in four months, as rival providers Baseten, Fireworks and Fal raise at similarly steep multiples.

Anthropic's IPO prospectus devotes roughly 80 of 261 pages to warning investors its AI could pose "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity," Reuters reported and researcher Hesamation detailed in a breakdown of the filing.

It's an unusually blunt risk disclosure from a company asking investors to fund the same technology it says could endanger them.


Meta's Muse AI agent is coming to its smart glasses "soon," UploadVR reported, letting wearers direct it to take actions without pulling out a phone.

It puts Meta's assistant directly on your face, just as Meta starts selling the same Muse stack to businesses.


AWS published a guide for using Amazon Nova Act and Bedrock AgentCore for synthetic monitoring, letting a multimodal model read UI screenshots instead of relying on brittle DOM selectors.

It's a concrete example of agents replacing traditional test scripts for a mundane but universal engineering task.


Researchers released YuE2, a music generation model that writes a readable score before rendering full songs, scoring 6.73 on WildSongBench ahead of other public systems, according to the paper.

Expert listeners preferred its planned-then-rendered approach to a separate-model pipeline, and rated it competitive with Suno v5.
