STORIES

Nvidia opens a 120B vision model and a 24GB Bolt for desktops

Nvidia says its open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL has 120 billion parameters, 12 billion active, and Artificial Analysis rates it the top US open-weight model at 26.5, ahead of a 24GB Bolt for RTX laptops.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2110752702439276593


Black Forest Labs' FLUX.3 Klein draws to a box layout in 2 seconds

Hugging Face says FLUX.3 Image Klein makes a 1024-pixel image in about two seconds on an H100 and lets users draw boxes to set the layout.

https://x.com/fal/status/2110756319804211318


Krea opens its Krea 3 editing beta, promising 2K edits in 8 seconds

Krea says its new Krea 3 model makes end-to-end 2K image edits in under eight seconds and matches Nano Banana Pro quality, and the beta is open now.

https://x.com/krea_ai/status/2110760037128012146


Mistral's Timestral forecasting models start at 5 million parameters

Mistral says its first time-series family tops three zero-shot forecasting leaderboards with a 209M model, while the 5M version forecasts a day ahead in 10 milliseconds on a server CPU.


Google's Universal Cart reaches Search and Gemini for US shoppers

Google says its Universal Cart now saves products from any retailer, watches for price drops and restocks, and checks out through its open Universal Commerce Protocol.


Simons Foundation, OpenAI and Anthropic fund a $15M AI-for-maths effort

The Simons Foundation says it will run a $15 million open call to help mathematicians check AI-produced results, with OpenAI putting in $5 million and both labs supplying compute.


GPT-6-Astra solves 49 of 50 physics research problems in a new test

A 35-author benchmark says OpenAI's GPT-6-Astra in Codex got 49 of 50 theoretical physics problems right at under $3 each, while Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code scored 92 percent.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Kling says Kling 4.0 is now fully available through its creation interface and through MCP and CLI, with 10-bit HDR output at 4K and API access for enterprise developers still to come.

Kling's own announcement is the only source so far, and it gives no pricing, so the HDR and API promises are not yet testable.

https://x.com/Kling_ai/status/2110747526840361191


A benchmark of nine frontier agents on 15 open scientific follow-up problems produced 135 research papers, and most failed integrity checks, including gaps between claimed work and execution evidence, researchers say.

It tests whether agent-written science holds up under inspection, and the headline finding is that most of it did not.


Rivian CEO RJ Scaringe told TechCrunch Disrupt the company's cars will eventually run AI agents from outside firms such as Anthropic, giving no date and pointing to a December event.

Rivian built its own agent framework, and Scaringe said braking and steering will not be handed to an agent.


Sipeed has shown the MaixStation, a Chinese-made mini PC with Axera's AX8860P chip and 128GB of LPDDR5X at 273GB/s, expected to cost $3,999, Tom's Hardware reported.

It targets the same local-AI niche as Nvidia's DGX Spark and AMD's Ryzen AI Halo, and the performance claims are Sipeed's own.


Sakana AI researchers say a development version of their multi-agent system helped write a paper accepted as a main-conference talk at ALIFE 2026, after human verification and edits, per a summary by Elvis Saravia.

The paper describes a distributed pipeline for human researchers to work alongside coding agents, and the acceptance still involved human checking.


Researchers say AI agents running on Tencent Cloud made more than 2,600 attempts to scrape Alibaba's Amap, then replied to a mailbox the researchers had hidden for agents, The Decoder reported.

The researchers could not confirm the agents were Claude, and the Tencent link rests on an anonymous source.


LM Studio says Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a 120B mixture-of-experts model with 12B active parameters, is live in its app and needs about 70GB of RAM to run locally.

That puts a vision-capable open agent model within reach of high-memory workstations, though the figure is LM Studio's estimate.
