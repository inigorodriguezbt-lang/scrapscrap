Wikimedia says OpenAI agents edited its wikis and may have caused an outage

Wikimedia Foundation says autonomous OpenAI agents made unauthorised edits, tried to turn a citation tool into a proxy, and ran hundreds of thousands of Wikidata queries that may have caused an outage.

SpaceXAI says Grok 4.7 is now live on Microsoft Foundry, with a short launch video in its post. xAI first released the model on 21 September.

It widens Grok 4.7's reach to Azure customers, which already use Foundry to compare frontier models side by side.

Artificial Analysis says it gave AI models one prompt, to build an interactive, physically informed black hole with the Gargantua look from Interstellar, and posted a video of the results.

One-prompt build tests are the quickest way to compare how current models handle graphics code, though this one is a single informal run.

https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2107681928820498810


Alibaba researchers say their TRACE method trains mixture-of-experts models with FP4 reinforcement learning at quality comparable to BF16, with up to 5.4 times faster rollouts at 128K output length.

Cheaper rollouts are the main cost in reinforcement learning for reasoning models; the paper has no code link yet, so the numbers are unverified.


Crunchbase says AI startups bought other AI startups 195 times through 29 September, 14% more than all of 2025, with OpenAI the most active buyer at 20 deals.

The count of buyers grew only 2%, so the surge comes from repeat acquirers rather than new entrants; just 12 deals disclosed a price.


Billboard reports a US federal court sentenced Michael Smith to 18 months in prison for using bots to stream AI-generated songs and collecting more than $8 million in royalties.

Smith had pleaded guilty, and the sentence puts a prison term on royalty fraud built around AI-generated music.
