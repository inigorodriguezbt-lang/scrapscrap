Artificial Analysis says Ant Group's Ling 3.1 Flash, a 560B-parameter model with 25B active, scores 41 on its Intelligence Index, up from 20 for Ling 3.0 Flash.

Ant is expected to release the weights soon, which would put a 1M-context agentic reasoner in open hands.


Georgi Gerganov says Qwen3.8-27B users running MTP should switch to DFlash speculative decoding for extra speed, using llama.cpp v0.6.0 and the flag --spec-type draft-dflash.

It is a one-flag change for anyone running Qwen's 27B model locally through llama.cpp.


Researcher account Rohan Paul says a new Meta paper found that pairing Claude Code and Codex to review each other's patches raised fully correct fixes from 45.8% to 62.5% at matched spend.

The paper's argument is that agents from one product make correlated mistakes, so a second vendor's agent catches more; we have not read the paper itself.


TestingCatalog reports Gamma has launched Gamma 5, which puts an agent at the centre of its presentation tool, with PowerPoint import and Notion, Slack and HubSpot connectors.

The agent is said to ask about goal and audience and show its plan before building, rather than generating a deck straight away.


AI watcher Kol Tregaskes says ChatGPT's chat mode now appears to have sub-agents, previously limited to Work mode and Codex, and reads it as a sign the three may merge.

It is one user's observation, not an OpenAI announcement, and the merge is his inference rather than anything the company has said.


Reuters reports Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows CEO Dario Amodei earned $18 million in 2025, with his sister and company president Daniela Amodei at $16.4 million.

Reuters calls the pay middle of the pack for tech chief executives, one of the first compensation details from the filing.


A New York Times report says that for every 30 Chinese AI researchers who went to the US in 2025, only one researcher made the opposite journey to China.

The study suggests China is struggling to recruit foreign AI talent even as its labs compete at the frontier.
