## Musk says Grok Bot will route work to Claude Opus 5.5, Midjourney and Suno

**Elon Musk says SpaceX's Grok Bot agent will pick the best back-end model for each task, naming Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, Midjourney and Suno, not only its own Grok models.**

Elon Musk said on X that Grok Bot, the agent product from SpaceX's AI arm, will in future use "the best back end model for any given task". His post names Claude Opus 5.5, Midjourney, Suno and adds "other leading APIs". He gave the rule as "whatever is most likely to give you the best outcome". The post had more than 13,000 likes when the paper read it.

https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2107724314451878104

Grok Bot launched in beta on 11 August, according to the trade site Trending Topics, as the first product from SpaceXAI after it bought Cursor's maker Anysphere in June for $60 billion in stock. That report says it was pitched as "AI teammates" that work in the cloud and run on SpaceXAI's own Grok models, with access limited to premium SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor tiers. The paper has not confirmed those figures independently.

The change turns a Grok product into a router that can call a rival lab's model. Musk gave no detail on how the choice is made, whether users can override it, or whether prices change. Anthropic had not commented in anything the paper found, and it is not clear whether the two companies have a commercial deal. The post is Musk's own statement, and no one else has confirmed it.

Reaction was quick. The commentator kimmonismus called Grok Bot "already the best always on agent" and said OpenAI and others should copy the move. The account mark_k said it is "getting so good" but wants better control of a user's own computer, since it is focused on the cloud computer. These are individual opinions, not tests.
