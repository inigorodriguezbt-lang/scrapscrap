Pocketty puts an iPhone SSH terminal in front of blocked coding agents

A new iPhone and iPad app pings you when a coding agent is stuck and opens a live terminal to answer it, for a one-off $99 launch price.


Open-source Jevman benchmark has AI models play Pac-Man against the ghosts

Opper AI's Jevman asks a model for a direction probability at every junction, with two seconds to answer, across 100 recorded games per model.

SNIPPETS

Google released AI Edge Foresight, a free experimental Mac note-taker that transcribes meetings and answers questions fully offline using its EmbeddingGemma 2 model, TechCrunch reports, taking on Granola.

It is a showcase for Google's on-device Gemma models, from the team behind an earlier offline dictation app that never reached mainstream use.


Anthropic says Claude Haiku 5.5 scores 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1's offline subset, up from 15.7% for Haiku 4.5, with prices cut by up to 90% and a one-million-token context window.

Artificial Analysis separately ranks it first among small models on its Intelligence Index, though it says the model burns about three times more output tokens than GPT-6 Luna.


Edi Life OS, a self-hosted MIT-licensed life dashboard, ships an optional 26-tool MCP server so AI assistants can log habits, tasks and expenses, according to its GitHub page; it had 14 stars.

It is a small Show HN project, but shows how personal-productivity apps are adding MCP servers so a chatbot can act on their data.


OpenAI says legal-software maker LegalOn cut estimated daily Codex costs by 65% without slowing development, by matching its Astra, Sol and Luna models to tasks and managing budgets.

It is a vendor case study, so the saving is OpenAI's and LegalOn's own estimate, but it shows the model-routing pattern customers are using to control agent costs.


A paper highlighted by the X account omarsar0 says swapping Codex for its HERMES harness lifted GPT-5.6 Sol from 6.5% to 31.0% on whole-repository migration, with the same model and effort setting.

If the result holds up, it suggests the scaffolding around a model can matter as much as the model on large code-migration jobs.


Researcher teortaxesTex says n-gram blocks in models such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash can be edited with a few-megabyte overlay to add new facts, based on experiments run on LongCat.

The poster calls it primitive, limited to subject-relation-object triples, but says it points to per-user continual learning; it is one researcher's unreplicated experiment.


Bitdefender says thousands of cheap Android phones, including Doogee and Cubot models and clones of Galaxy and iPhone handsets, ship with Midnight Mimosa malware in firmware that cannot be uninstalled.

The researchers say it installs ad-fraud apps and could pull phones into botnets, a reminder that supply-chain risk starts at the factory.
