TestingCatalog reports Voyager has launched a Mac desktop app that lets AI agents operate After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Blender and Unity, reading project files and returning editable results.

It is one of the first apps aimed at agents working inside the creative tools people already own, though only TestingCatalog's account has been seen so far.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2108264326977270074


Artificial Analysis added trusted-access models to its Cyber Index, and says GPT-6 Sol (Daybreak Blue), a version with fewer cyber guardrails, now leads the leaderboard.

Until now the index covered only publicly available models, so this is the first look at how much guardrails hold back agentic cyber-defence scores.


Developer synthwavedd says StepFun's Step 5 preview built a detailed snow globe in an hour for a $2.26 API bill, and that the model is now on OpenRouter at $1 in and $2.70 out per million tokens.

The poster is a user, not StepFun, but the prices put a new agentic model on routes such as Kilo Code, Cline and OpenCode.

https://x.com/synthwavedd/status/2108271207992930765


Artificial Analysis says Harvey LAB-AA v1.1 adds a hallucination check to its Legal Agent Benchmark, so a task counts only if every rubric criterion is met and nothing materially misstates.

Gating scores on hallucinations makes a pass harder to earn, which should separate legal agents that sound right from ones that are.


SemiAnalysis says memory suppliers are now acknowledging its HBM de-spec call, with SK Hynix publicly saying 16-high stacks are difficult and the market prioritising bandwidth over capacity.

If stacks stay at 4 and 8 high, hybrid bonding for HBM loses its main argument, which SemiAnalysis frames as a question of if rather than when.


Bloomberg reports Hone, founded by former OpenAI and Cognition staffers to build AI agents that help run a business, raised a $60 million seed round at a $285 million valuation.

A seed round that size shows how much investors are paying for agent startups with lab pedigree before any product track record.


Elon Musk posted that the Grok Bot app works well on a phone and can be downloaded from Apple or Android stores; the post had 1,715 likes when the paper read it.

It follows Musk's earlier statement that the bot will route work to other labs' models, now with an app to try.


Product Hunt lists Semwright, which pitches itself as giving AI agents structured access to real software, and Polylane, an AI on-call engineer for Vercel apps, among its newest AI launches.

Both are one-line launch claims from their makers and have not been tested, but they show where agent tooling is heading.
