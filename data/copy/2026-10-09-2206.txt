Telegram Desktop users are told to update to 7.2.9 after researcher BeakSec found a flaw, CVE-2026-107181, where one clicked link could expose session files and allow account takeover, International Cyber Digest says.

International Cyber Digest relays the researcher's finding; Telegram has allegedly already fixed it, so updating closes the hole.


SemiAnalysis says H100 rental prices have risen, though the GPU should be sliding down its depreciation curve, and cites $7 trillion of debt coming by 2029 as it launches a Compute Capital Markets group.

The depreciation curve is what lenders underwrite when financing GPU clusters, so rising rental prices bear directly on AI datacentre debt.


Alaya Lab and NYCU researchers released OuroWorld, which they say turns a static 3D Gaussian Splatting scene into a looping 3D cinemagraph, preferred in 70.8 to 99.0 per cent of comparisons in a 30-person study.

The loop is guaranteed by a Fourier-series deformation, the authors say, so static 3D worlds from world models could gain motion without hand-drawn masks.


Tencent Hunyuan's MC-Sparse paper, with 27 upvotes on Hugging Face, studies why sparse attention degrades quality in diffusion transformers at high sparsity, and proposes a way to close the gap with dense attention.

Sparse attention is a main route to faster video and 3D generation, so closing the quality gap would cut latency without a visible penalty.


KAIST AI's paper on a multi-agent egocentric world model, with 42 upvotes on Hugging Face, predicts first-person views of several agents acting together using fine-grained interaction, rather than coarse locomotion actions.

Most egocentric world models cover one agent, so a shared-environment version is a step toward simulators for teams of robots or game characters.


Vesta, which builds AI agents that automate much of loan origination, raised $30 million led by Conversion, taking its total funding to $85 million, TechCrunch reports.

It is one more sign that lenders are adopting AI agents for back-office workflows, a category now attracting steady venture money.


Microsoft says Decision-1 was post-trained from Alibaba's open-weight Qwen3.5-9B and that Microsoft plans to rebase it on MAI and OpenAI models later, TestingCatalog reports.

A Microsoft flagship decision model built on a Chinese open-weight base is notable; the planned rebase suggests the base is a stopgap.
