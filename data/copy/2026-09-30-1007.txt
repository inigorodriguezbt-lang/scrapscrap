Altman says OpenAI will not list until it can make confident safety claims

Speaking to reporters after his DevDay keynote, Sam Altman gave no date for a listing. Going public now would be "ill-advised", he said, though waiting too long is "bad for the world".


Meta tells tax authorities its AI data centers are experiments, NYT reports

The New York Times reports Meta is claiming research-and-experimentation treatment for AI data center build-outs, including its Louisiana Hyperion campus, to cut its federal tax bill.

Debt, not just equity, is now paying for the AI build-out, and it is widening to new currencies and investor pools.


Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo says Nvidia is testing hydrocarbon resin for the printed circuit boards in its Rubin Ultra NVL576 racks, aiming to improve manufacturing yields.

Board materials are a quiet bottleneck for the next Nvidia generation; this is one analyst's supply-chain claim, and Nvidia has not commented.

The excerpt lands the day Altman talks down an IPO and Anthropic files for one, so it is the backstory readers will want.

A government running an algorithmic matchmaking trial is odd enough to watch; the pilot's size and rules were not in the post.
