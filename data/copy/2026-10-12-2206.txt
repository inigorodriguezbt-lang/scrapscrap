Bloomberg reports Epic told developers on its Disney shooter that "no more hand-written code" will be written and art will come from "full AI workflows", with the game slipping to March.

TestingCatalog reports Google is preparing Nano Banana 3 Pro, an image model built on Gemini 4 Argon and referred to internally as Gempix 3 Pro, expected in November or December. Google has not confirmed it.

The report rests on references spotted in Google's systems, so the name, base model and timing could still change before any launch.


Tom's Hardware reports AMD introduced Helion at the OCP Global Summit, a rack-scale reference design for agentic AI servers holding up to 32,768 Zen 6 Venice CPU cores, in CPU-only and all-in-one configurations.

It pitches CPUs, not just GPUs, as the hardware for running swarms of AI agents, with the design shipping later, according to the outlet.


AI commentator kimmonismus says he had early access to Meta's new Watermelon model for days, that trick questions did not fool it, and that as a coding agent it wrote, ran and checked its own code.

It is one early tester's view of a model Meta launched today, and he adds that its creative writing still felt a bit dry.


Developer Theo says the full version of Meta's Muse Wonder is limited to subscribers with no price information available, calling that disappointing, in a post that drew 254 likes.

Meta has put the standard model in its API, so the restriction on the larger variant limits who can test its benchmark claims.


Vals AI says it asked Chinese and American models hundreds of politically sensitive China questions: MiMo V2.6 Pro and GLM-5.3 sometimes openly criticised China, while Gemini 4 Argon, Mistral Large 4 and Grok 4.7 occasionally repeated propaganda.

The result runs against the assumption that Chinese models are the more pro-China ones, though it is Vals AI's own test and has not been independently checked.
