Arena says Meta's Muse Wonder (Max) scores 77.5% on its Alignment Index, ahead of new entrants Reflection's Beam-1.0 at 75.3% and StepFun's Step 5 Preview at 75.1%.

Two labs joined the leaderboard at once, and Arena says Beam is the top US open model on its Agent Arena overall.


NVIDIA researchers say adding one midtraining stage on 500B tokens of repositories, pull requests and build logs lifted Nemotron 3.5 Lightning from 55.8 to 62.8 on SWE-bench Multilingual, per a summary from dair.ai.

The recipe is open, and NVIDIA says it kept post-training identical, so the gain is attributed to the midtraining stage alone.


NVIDIA released Nemotron-Labs Dory1.0-4B, a 4-billion-parameter looped model with a 256K context whose layers are reused so users can set compute per task, under the OpenMDW 1.1 licence.

NVIDIA says accuracy rises as loops go from one to four, on its own benchmark harness, so the figures are untested elsewhere.


Analyst Jukan, after TrendForce's San Francisco roadshow, says China is on track to localize about half or more of most chip-equipment categories, excluding lithography and track.

If right, China could add mature-node capacity with domestic tools, even as AI demand for power chips keeps those foundries busy.
