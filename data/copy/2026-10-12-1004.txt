Researchers teach a Unitree G1 humanoid to catch thrown boxes from camera alone

A University of Washington-led paper says its Reflex controller let a Unitree G1 catch human-thrown boxes 65 percent of the time at 2.5 metres, using only an onboard depth camera.


Kling team says new diffusion training method cuts GPU hours 12-fold

KlingAI researchers say DiffusionART matched rival reinforcement-learning methods on a 13B video model with about 12 times fewer GPU hours, and removes the need to tune EMA.


Palantir CTO says Pentagon already replaced Anthropic

Palantir's Shyam Sankar told CBS the Pentagon's drop of Anthropic had no operational impact because it "has already been replaced", and that AI makers own the liability for defective models.
