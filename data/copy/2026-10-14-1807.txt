STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Linus Torvalds posts his first patch series written with an LLM

Torvalds posted a ten-patch Linux series built with a language model to speed up page-cache reads, and LWN reports an order-of-magnitude gain on one benchmark.


Epic says its vibe-coded PC launcher starts almost twice as fast as Steam

Epic Games' preview launcher cold-starts in 5.47 seconds against Steam's 10.07, by Epic's own tests, and is the company's first native Linux version.


Grok Voice 3.0 debuts atop Artificial Analysis's speech-to-speech ranking

Artificial Analysis scores SpaceXAI's Grok Voice 3.0 at 86.9 on its Speech to Speech Index, ahead of GPT-Live-1 at 83.2 and Gemini 3.8 Live at 82.6.


Blog post asks why every LLM draws the same pelican on a bicycle

Aman Rusia lines up thirteen models' answers to the pelican-on-a-bicycle prompt, finds them near-identical and calls it a diversity collapse. The post reached 116 points on Hacker News.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Graphicle, a Rust port of the Graphify code-graph tool, adds project memory for coding agents; its README says an extract of the Kubernetes repo took 3m18s against 26m38s for Graphify.

It runs locally under Apache-2.0 and serves agents through MCP, but had only 54 GitHub stars and the speed figures are the project's own single runs.


Arena says Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 (High) ranks 27th in its Agent Arena after 20,500 real agentic sessions, at a median $0.09 per task.

The small model scores a net +1.48 percent and ranks 15th in Code, close to GPT-6 Luna at $0.08 per task.


OpenAI says ChatGPT Space, introduced at DevDay, lets teams build living documents with ChatGPT and edit them together, with comments and tagging; it is for Pro, Business and Enterprise plans.

It is a shared workspace in ChatGPT rather than a solo chat, and OpenAI's own post has a demo clip.

https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2110424821331034550


Perplexity says ultrafast versions of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and SOL 6.1 are now in Perplexity Computer for Max subscribers, chosen under Custom by setting speed to Ultrafast.

Faster token generation is aimed at long Computer agent tasks, which Perplexity says will finish sooner.

https://x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2110415710073819389


Perplexity says its SPACE sandbox runs millions of executions a day with an error rate below 0.0002 percent, and has published how it ties requests to the right execution as sandboxes pause and move.

The write-up covers the plumbing behind agent code execution, which is where coding assistants most often fail at scale.


Aravind Srinivas says Perplexity Computer now has an /anatomy skill available to everyone, originally built by community member wheresryan22, and shared a demo clip.

A community-built skill moving into the main product shows how Perplexity is borrowing from users' agent add-ons.
