OpenAI to test labeled visual ads while ChatGPT users generate images

OpenAI says ChatGPT will show clearly labeled image ads during image generation, tested with US users and a first group of advertisers later this month. It says ads do not influence answers.


Munich robotics start-up RobCo reaches $1bn valuation and plans a March launch for Alfie

RobCo says an employee share sale has taken its valuation past $1 billion, double its level nine months ago. Its autonomous industrial robot Alfie is due to launch on 4 March 2027.

The Hacker News reports Apple is tightening macOS Full Disk Access, the permission that can let AI agents read files, mail, messages and browsing history; future access will require explicit user action.

Agents that read a Mac's private data lean on this permission, so a stricter prompt changes how coding and desktop agents are set up.


Analyst Jukan, citing TrendForce, says system-level testing and validation issues persist on Nvidia's Rubin platform, with volumes expected to ramp gradually from the fourth quarter of 2026 or the first of 2027.

A slower Rubin ramp would stretch the wait for Nvidia's next AI accelerators; the account is secondhand and Nvidia has not commented here.


AlphaXiv highlights Sphere Encoder 2, a paper claiming sharper one-step image generation, reaching an FDr6 of 4.92 on ImageNet in four steps with about 23 times less sampling compute than a baseline.

It targets the blurriness that has held back one-step generators; the numbers are the authors' own and unreplicated.
