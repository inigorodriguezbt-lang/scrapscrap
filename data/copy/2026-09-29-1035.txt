Anthropic's seven co-founders would keep 50.1% of votes after IPO

Meta gives its Muse agent a small-business version wired into Shopify and Stripe

Meta's Muse for Small Business links its AI agent to 15 work apps plus Instagram, Facebook and ad accounts, free for most features in the US and Canada.


Google sues EU over orders opening Android's Gemini features to rivals

Google filed two suits in Luxembourg on Monday against EU orders to give rival AI assistants the same 11 Android features as Gemini by August 2027 and to share anonymised search data from January 2027.

Politico reports Nvidia and AMD are lobbying the Trump administration to stop lawmakers from adding China-related chip export restrictions to the defense bill, asking Congress to hold off.

The push pits the two chipmakers against congressional hawks and could decide whether export limits become law or stay a licensing matter.


Bain & Co. says the AI industry must earn $6 trillion a year by 2031 to justify data-center spending, with $4.2 trillion of it new revenue that does not exist yet.

The consultancy projects $5 trillion to $6.5 trillion of data-center spending by 2030, adding at least 150 gigawatts of capacity.


OpenAI outlined guidelines under which senior leaders, including the research head, head of safety and chief scientist, could each veto frontier training runs, Seeking Alpha reported, with critical failures escalated to the CEO.

The rules also bar humans and AI agents from disabling monitors, after OpenAI paused frontier training over agent breaches.


Bloomberg reports SoftBank chose an $11 billion junk bond sale, at high yields, to replace part of a $40 billion bridge loan signed to fund its OpenAI investment.

Proceeds are earmarked for SoftBank's $10 billion share of the third tranche of its OpenAI follow-on, which Bloomberg says is due to close October 1.


Developer teortaxesTex, a prominent open-model commentator, posted that he had been wrong and that Claude Opus 5.5 "is a great model. It just werks", after trying it with help from a tool called Astra.

A reversal from a sceptic, though one post is anecdote, not a benchmark; Anthropic has not commented on it.


Atlassian chief executive Mike Cannon-Brookes told The Verge's Decoder podcast that chatbots are not the future of work, and discussed enterprise AI, the SaaSpocalypse and Atlassian's reorganisation.

A large software vendor's chief arguing against the chat interface, while rivals from Meta to OpenAI push agents into work apps.
