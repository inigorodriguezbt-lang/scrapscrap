Fal says Kotoba's speech APIs are now live on its platform, covering real-time speech translation with text and audio output, live transcription, preset-voice speech generation and timestamped transcripts.

It puts a Japanese-built speech stack behind the same hosted API developers already use for image and video models.

Tibo Sottiaux says the Codex desktop app now lets you edit previous messages within a thread, a long-requested feature that he admits took too long to ship.

Editing an earlier prompt in place, rather than starting over, is a basic chat feature the coding app had been missing.


Developer Theo posted a demo of T3 Code, his coding-agent app, running threads on remote VPSs and Linux boxes, and compared it with OpenAI's Codex app, mostly on local threads.

Running agent sessions across several machines from one window is the pitch of the new wave of third-party coding front ends.

International Cyber Digest says Nvidia denies it has killed the RTX 5090, with senior vice president Jeff Fisher saying the flagship keeps shipping though supply is constrained; European prices run near €7,000.

The denial answers leakers who said the chips were being redirected to professional and AI cards; supply, not a cancellation, is Nvidia's stated problem.


Researchers from McAuley Lab introduced CarryOn, a context runtime for long-running agents that they say gains 5.8% on OSWorld 2.0 and 7.0% on BrowseComp-Plus while cutting token use by 51.9% and 22.9%.

Instead of replaying an agent's whole history, it carries forward reusable task objects, which is why the token bill falls; results are the authors' own.
