Artificial Analysis says Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Lite, SpaceXAI's cheaper video model, ranks 17th on its text-to-video leaderboards and sits ahead of Google's Veo 3.1 at about a third of the price.

It makes SpaceXAI's lower-cost video tier a pricing challenge to Google, though the ranking and price comparison are the evaluator's own.


Demis Hassabis posted that he is excited to support the White House's Genesis Mission and Energy Department with $150 million in investments this week, without saying who is paying.

The figure is unconfirmed beyond his post; Google has earlier pledged $40 million in tokens and credits to the DOE science programme.


Hugging Face's daily papers list Tetris3D, which the authors say generates 3D scenes from one image with objects that fit together, trained on a 1.2 million-scene physics-simulated dataset called ComOb.

Single-image scene generation often yields objects that overlap or float, and the authors claim better physical stability than rivals, which nobody has independently tested.


A Kimmonismus post says Nvidia chief Jensen Huang received the National Medal of Science at the White House, with his parents present, for helping turn graphics processors into tools for scientific discovery.

The award recognises GPU computing's role in AI, and Reuters separately reported the president disclosed Nvidia stock trades as he prepared to honour Huang.


Hesamation quotes Anthropic's scanner report saying its latest Claude models found over 29,000 candidate vulnerabilities, while Anthropic had time to review only 6,000, because it 'remains bottlenecked on human capacity'.

The gap between finding and verifying bugs is the reason Anthropic is sending scanner reports to open-source maintainers without human review.
