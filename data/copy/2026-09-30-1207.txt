OpenAI research chief says monitors now run during training

Mark Chen tells MIT Technology Review OpenAI moved 5 to 10 percent of its compute to safety monitoring and flagged a new agent hack in 15 minutes. A nonprofit has sued over the breach.


Pi coding agent adds MCP to its core after saying no to it

Earendil has folded Model Context Protocol support into the Pi agent's core, reversing its earlier public stance. Its post says MCP has improved but composes poorly, and pushes JavaScript Codemode instead.


Bloomberg says Apple's HomePad home hub launches October 13

Bloomberg reports Apple's 6-inch home display, styled after the iMac G4, arrives October 13 with a new HomePod mini and an Apple TV 4K with Siri AI, under CEO John Ternus.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Alibaba's Qwen team says its 27-billion-parameter dense model Qwen3.8-27B is now available through Nebius, pitching it for agent and deep-research workflows.

A mid-sized dense model on a hosted API gives developers a way to try Qwen3.8 without running the weights themselves.


Kling AI posted a clip it says shows the full-powered Kling 4.0 video model in action, under the tags #Kling4 and #KlingModeOn.

It is the first look at Kling 4.0 output from its maker, so the quality claims remain untested by outsiders.


Tom's Hardware reports a developer trained a small JEPA world model, built on Yann LeCun's LeWorldModel research, on one RTX 3080 Ti to play Pokémon Red.

The developer's write-up says it picked a starter in 52 percent of 100 trials, so it plays a single opening rather than the game.


OpenAI's head of hardware Richard Ho told Tom's Hardware that AI-assisted design of its Jalapeño chip "established a new baseline" for the industry, with other companies asking how it was done.

OpenAI is the source of the claim and no outside engineer has checked how much of the design AI actually did.


Bloomberg reports Singapore received its first AI-linked data breach notification, after a food company accidentally exposed customers' email addresses in a bulk email, citing the Straits Times and the privacy regulator.

The breach was an email mishap flagged as AI-related, so the regulator's definition of an AI-linked incident is the open question.


Samsung launched the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra and Tab S12+ Android tablets plus a SmartTag 3 tracker, which Bloomberg says offers better precision and longer-distance tracking than earlier models.

The tablets are pitched on upgraded performance, and the report gives no prices or AI features in the summary seen by the paper.


The Decoder reports AMD is buying Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion in stock, with Li joining as executive vice president and chief scientist reporting to CEO Lisa Su.

AMD gains an in-house lab for physical-world models, including World Labs' Atlas system that generates 3D environments from minimal input.
