Interconnects' Nathan Lambert calls Meta's Muse Spark Open the strongest US open model by a wide margin, ranking it above GLM 5.2 and behind GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3.

He adds that its custom licence is complex and untested in court, which he argues could hold back the adoption Meta wants.


Meta has removed wording from its commercial terms page that suggested it could scrape content from news sites to train its AI, The Verge reports, after backlash.

The Verge says the change came after backlash; the removal alone does not show whether Meta's scraping practices change.


The Verge reports that websites are increasingly closing their doors to AI agents, just as OpenAI, Meta and Google pitch agents that book, shop and clean out inboxes.

If more sites block automated visitors, the agent products being promoted may find much of the web closed to them.


Taiwan is expanding its Foreign Trade Act to list AI servers and semiconductors as controlled exports, with prison terms of up to five years for illicit shipments to China, Tom's Hardware reports.

It follows Taiwan's August charges against nine people, including Nvidia and Super Micro staff, over alleged B300 server exports to China.


AI researcher omarsar0 says a Tencent paper lifts a Qwen3.8-27B terminal agent from 20.68% to 50.58% on a four-suite Terminal-Bench average over five rounds of training.

The post describes alternating fine-tuning on verified trajectories with reinforcement learning; the figures come from the paper's authors and have not been independently checked.


OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux says his work agent "Burger" and his personal agent "Dorito" coordinated by email with his accountant to file tax paperwork in 20 minutes.

The post, which has about 1,950 likes, is one user's account of two agents handing work to each other; the workflow is his description alone.


Bloomberg reports that the OpenAI engineer charged with using a hidden recording device in one of the company's offices has asked to be freed on bail and denies planning to flee.

He says his purchase of a one-way ticket to Saudi Arabia, his native country, was not part of a plan to flee US justice.
