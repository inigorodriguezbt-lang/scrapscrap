Nikkei reports, as relayed by @jukan05, that Toshiba plans to invest about ¥60 billion to expand its Philippines factory and double hard-disk capacity for AI data centres in fiscal 2027.

Hard drives hold the bulk training and archive data behind AI clusters, and demand for them is now tied to data-centre builds.


@rohanpaul_ai describes a paper in which eight models, including GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 4.8, ran a year of linked decisions; the best, Qwen3.7-Max with Hermes, ended with 27.3% of the average human's money.

The result, as summarised, suggests long-horizon tasks with delayed feedback remain a weak spot even for the leading models.


Meta Superintelligence Labs researchers say a dedicated controller deciding what work to run next lifts GPT-5.5 on ProgramBench from 63.7% to 71.5% with the same workers and budget, against 58.0% for Codex, per dair.ai.

The paper points to orchestration, not bigger models, as a source of gains on long agent runs.
