Reka's Rho-1 puts text, video and robot control in one 19B model

Reka says its 19-billion-parameter Rho-1 draws images, edits video and outputs robot joint trajectories from one network, trained on 320 H100 GPUs. It is a research preview, and Reka lists its own limits.


Researchers track an AI 'agent fleet' asking Amap for zoo directions

Researchers say many parallel AI agents, apparently on Tencent servers, query Alibaba's Amap for routes to a park, a zoo and a hospital. They see no sign the agents talk.


Etched is reported to be fielding funding offers at a $40bn to $50bn valuation

TechCrunch, citing sources, says the inference-chip start-up is in early talks at $40 billion to $50 billion, up from $21 billion last month. Etched says it has $1 billion in customer orders.

TikTok says its new Shopping Assistant is a conversational agent that remembers preferences and answers questions on sizing, shipping and availability, alongside one-click Buy Direct checkout from the For You feed, TechCrunch reports.

The aim is to keep shoppers inside TikTok instead of sending product questions to outside chatbots such as ChatGPT.


Instinct says its AI agent now works in group chats, letting friends plan trips and carpools together, even those without an account, with personal agents needing permission before sharing information or acting, TechCrunch reports.

Shared agents in group chats test whether assistants can work for several people without leaking what each person told them privately.


RemoveMacAI, an open-source command-line tool on GitHub, deletes about 12GB of Apple Intelligence models that macOS 27 keeps on disk even with every AI setting off, The Verge reports.

The Verge says macOS 27 dropped the single Settings toggle for disabling Apple Intelligence, leaving the models on disk.


Android Authority reports an app teardown shows Google may extend Gemini's Call for Me beyond business calls, with examples such as 'Call Mom and tell her I will be 15 minutes late'.

It is a teardown of unreleased code, so the feature and its wording may change or never ship.


Ars Technica reports that trust gaps in the MCP agent-to-agent protocol let malicious prompts spread from one agent to another, including in agents from Google and others.

If one compromised agent can pass instructions to the next, a single poisoned message could travel through a whole chain of tools.


AI developer omarsar0 says he built a proactive personal agent on the open-source Pi Durable, which checkpoints every step so he can kill it mid-task and it resumes where it stopped.

Durable checkpointing is the kind of plumbing that lets agents run unattended on schedules without losing work to a crash.

OpenAI's developer account posted a video showing how to start tasks by voice, manage agents across projects and branch into separate worktrees with the Codex command-line tool.

Voice-started tasks and parallel worktrees show OpenAI pushing Codex toward running several coding agents at once from the terminal.
