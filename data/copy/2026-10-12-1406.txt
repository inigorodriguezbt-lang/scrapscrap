Alibaba's Qwen team introduced QwenGyre, an elastic reinforcement-learning system for extreme-length agent runs, and says it scaled to Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B at about 700K tokens per rollout.

Qwen claims 1.85x speedup over colocated training by shifting GPUs between rollout and training, a cost question for anyone training long-horizon agents.


Vals AI says teams of AI agents cost 1.8x to 5.1x more than a solo agent and beat it by a statistically meaningful margin in only one of four tests with GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5.

The Decoder's write-up suggests agent swarms often buy little at maximum reasoning effort, undercutting a popular way of spending more compute.


Hesamation posted a video of someone giving Claude a physical body, with Opus 5.5 singing through a buzzer, one of the weekend's stranger Claude experiments.

It is a small hardware hack, not a product, but shows how people are wiring frontier models into physical gadgets.

https://x.com/Hesamation/status/2109625493544919203


QGIS merged a policy on 7 October that bans AI-assisted contributions, after maintainers said reviewing AI-generated code put unbearable pressure on them; proposer Nyall Dawson called it a door that can be reopened.

The pull request reached the Hacker News front page with 216 points, one of the clearest open-source bans on AI-written code so far.


Hesamation says the REA repository reached 120,000 GitHub stars in seven days, which it calls the third fastest to 100,000 stars, behind DeepSeek Harness at two days and OpenClaw at 67.

This paper could not verify the repository or the star count independently, so the figures are Hesamation's own.

It is Kling's own marketing for Kling 4.0, but it shows what the new video model is being used for.

Intelligence Cyber Digest, citing Ukrainian investigators, says a Russian drone that killed three people in Zaporizhzhia in July left its strike decision to an Nvidia Jetson Orin; Nvidia says the module is a consumer product.
