SemiAnalysis says AMD reached above 90% parity with CUDA on upstream vLLM's gating test groups this week, after the firm helped supply vLLM with more AMD GPUs for continuous integration.

Gating tests block merges, so parity there is the measure of whether AMD GPUs are a first-class target for the most widely used open inference engine.


A Meta Superintelligence Labs paper finds base models with a light harness often solve more agentic tasks than RL post-trained versions given enough samples, according to dair.ai's summary of BFCL v4, ACEBench and WebShop results.

Post-trained models win on single attempts, the summary says, but lose coverage at large sample counts, which challenges the default assumption that post-training always helps agents.


AutoCompact is a new paper that trains agents to decide for themselves when to compact context, what working state to keep and how to resume, according to @omarsar0.

It follows AutoHarness and AutoContext in a trend of moving duties from the agent harness into the model's own training.


A @thsottiaux post asked users what single feature is missing from Codex; the post has drawn more than 1,700 replies and 830 likes in about an hour.

The thread is an unusually large, public feature-request list for a coding agent, and a rough guide to what developers want next.
