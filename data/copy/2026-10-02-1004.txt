webAI releases a 3.66B formal-logic model that runs in 2 GiB

webAI says its TwIL-LM3-Pro, a 3.66-billion-parameter model that checks whether conclusions follow from premises, roughly matches Qwen3-8B on formal logic and fits in a 2.09 GiB file.

Earlyn, a Mac app that keeps an encrypted, searchable record of your screen and meetings, launched on Product Hunt; its maker says capture and transcription run locally and it feeds Claude Code, Codex and Cursor over MCP.

It joins a run of local screen-memory tools, and the MCP link means coding agents can query what you saw earlier; the privacy claims are the maker's own.


@omarsar0, who writes up papers on X, describes ProVer, which has an LLM judge flag the decisive step in an agent's run, then scores that step by how often rollouts started just before and after it succeed.

GRPO gives every token in a trajectory the same advantage; the paper's pitch is a credit signal that can tell the decisive step from the rest, per that summary.
