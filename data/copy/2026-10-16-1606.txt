Four Gemini 4 Argon agents beat Minecraft in 27 hours, dying 605 times

Researchers say four Gemini 4 Argon coding agents beat the Ender Dragon with no human help, writing 2,783 programs for about $810. It was a single run.


Models write StarCraft bots in an hour, and the best wins 7% of games

StarSkirmish Bench v0.2 had each model write a Protoss bot in C++ in an hour. GPT-6 Astra scored highest, and Gemini 4 Argon placed third, tied with two Claude models.

Perplexity says Gemini 4 Argon set a new record on WANDR, its Wide-And-Deep-Research benchmark, and is now available to Pro and Max subscribers; chief Aravind Srinivas called it a frontier model.

The benchmark is Perplexity's own, so the record is the company's claim, but it puts Google's new model inside a rival search product on launch week.


ComfyUI published a horror short, The Disturbance Call, built with Blender and Seedance 2.5, where a 3D blockout sets camera movement, action and timing for the generated video. It says workflow and prompt are included.

Using a rough 3D scene to steer an AI video model is a practical answer to the camera-control complaints creators raise about text prompts.

Bloomberg reports Trump's new AI task force held its first meeting and plans to commission a report on AI risks and develop protocols for labs to provide early warnings.

It is the first concrete output from the group, which follows voluntary safety commitments from labs that critics say fall short.


The New York Times counts $137 million spent by US candidates and PACs on 220 AI-generated campaign ads this year, mostly tied to Republicans, with about 70 percent not disclosing AI use.

Several states require disclosure of synthetic media in political ads, and the count suggests many ads run without it.


Korean media, citing a senior Samsung official, report Samsung is considering acquisitions to strengthen its silicon photonics foundry capabilities, analyst Jukan relayed on X.

Silicon photonics links chips with light, and is one answer to the bandwidth limits of large AI clusters, and Samsung has not confirmed any deal.


FDE-Gym, a benchmark of 95 real customer-delivery tasks, found its best agent configuration fully resolved 14 of them, with a top mean score of 0.56 across 13 configurations from seven providers.

It tests whether agents can question stakeholders and uncover unstated constraints, a gap that tidy coding benchmarks do not measure.


SemiAnalysis quoted a speaker saying sparse attention helps memory bandwidth but not memory capacity, because every token must stay available in context and loaded into HBM when selected.

It explains why offloading KV cache to slower, larger memory tiers such as HiCache is getting attention for long-context serving.
