DeepSeek open-sources its kernel libraries for Huawei's Ascend chips

DeepSeek says every TileLang operator used in training its V4 models now has an Ascend version. FlashMLA's README claims 95% of the Ascend 950's peak on prefill.


Artificial Analysis open-sources a tool to time AI agents on your own laptop

AA-AgentPerf-Local replays real agent sessions against llama.cpp, vLLM, LM Studio or Ollama and reports tokens a second. First results cover DGX Spark, RTX 5090, Ryzen AI Halo and MacBook Pro M5 Pro.

Z.ai researcher Zixuan Li says GLM-5.3 has helped defend 389 open-source projects through OpenVuln, its free scanning service, which has found 4,249 potential vulnerabilities so far, with findings sent privately to maintainers.

The post shows how far open-weight models are now being used for defensive security work, though the vulnerability count is Z.ai's own and unverified.


SemiAnalysis says ICLR submissions rose from 4,938 in 2023 to 19,525 in 2026, and that reported 2027 IDs exceed 62,000, more than all previous years combined at roughly 56,000.

The firm ties the surge to AI making papers cheaper to write and asks whether peer reviewers can keep up.


TestingCatalog reports Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5.5 is over 30% faster than Sonnet 5 at unchanged prices of $2 and $10 per million tokens, and scores 80.1% on OSWorld 2.1 against 57.0%.

The benchmark and speed figures are Anthropic's own claims as relayed by TestingCatalog, and Haiku 5.5 is expected in the coming weeks.


A Show HN project, enigma.design, is an interactive 3D Enigma machine with 16 lessons, live encryption and reproductions of German daily key sheets. It had 47 points on Hacker News.

It is a free browser tool for seeing how the rotors, plugboard and reflector scramble a message, and it has no AI in it.


Commentator Hesamation says GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is running on NVIDIA GPUs, not Cerebras, as Nvidia confirmed, and that the promised 750 tokens-a-second Cerebras version is not broadly available yet.

It adds to earlier questions about the hardware behind OpenAI's fast tier, and OpenAI has not commented in the posts the paper has read.


Elon Musk joked on X that the best way to sandbox an AI is to put it on a Delta flight, where it would have no chance of reaching the Internet.

The post had more than 10,000 likes within about half an hour of going up, showing how far AI containment talk has reached everyday jokes.
