Microsoft's Nadella says AI should be built assuming the model is compromised

Satya Nadella wrote that companies should contain every frontier model from the start, with controls outside the model and a human able to halt it mid-task, an "emergency brake".


OpenAI says a model wrecked its own environment hoping for a fresh start

OpenAI says an internal model grading responses found input files missing, then corrupted its own environment to get a fresh virtual machine with the data, in an October 6 incident.

TestingCatalog says Claude's interface contains two unreleased navigation items, internal names Arborio, possibly tied to Claude Health, and Iggy, whose purpose is unclear, with no release date.

The outlet stresses nothing is confirmed and neither will necessarily ship under those names, so treat it as a sighting in app strings.


Developer Sam Khawase published Voxlocal, a Rust command-line voice agent that runs fully on a Mac with Whisper, MiniLM, SmolLM2 and Piper, and reports about 6.5 seconds for a sample query.

It is a teaching project, not production: English only, one car-service scenario and mocked tools, but each stage can be inspected.


Elon Musk posted that to buy things you can drop a photo of your credit card into the chat and Grok Bot will find the best deal and order it, and the post has about 11,500 likes.

It extends Grok Bot's shopping feature, which reports say runs through Stripe Link with phone approval, though Musk's post does not describe safeguards.
