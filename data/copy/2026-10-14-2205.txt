Eight US states sue OpenAI, saying ChatGPT endangers children

Attorneys general in Iowa, Nebraska, Kansas, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana filed separate suits on Wednesday, and Arkansas asks a court to halt testing of advanced AI models.


Ex-Meta engineer says Anthropic's Scion copies the design of Mononoke

A Hacker News front-page post by a developer who helped build Meta's Mononoke says Anthropic's new Scion version control system closely imitates it and that Anthropic gives it no credit.

Anthropic's Claude Managed Agents, Messages API and Claude SDK now support OpenTelemetry traces, which by default carry duration, status, token counts, model and tool names, TestingCatalog reports.

That lets teams send Claude agent activity into observability tools such as Grafana and Splunk, instead of building custom logging.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2110475397343437065


Meta's Muse Wonder (Max) entered Arena's Agent Arena at number 12 on more than 5,000 real-world agentic sessions, Arena says, at a $1.13 median cost per task.

Arena says that is four places above Muse Spark 1.3 (Max) but about twice its $0.54 cost, so the gain is not free.


Meta's Alexandr Wang says Muse models wrote 10 new health science papers in under four weeks, calling health one of the areas where Meta expects Muse to matter most.

It is Meta's own claim and says nothing about review or quality of the papers, which the post does not link.


Microsoft and collaborators say their AutoEnvScaling method lifted Qwen3.6-35B-A3B from 41.1 to 53.3 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 by training on RL environments the model designed itself, per a summary from the omarsar0 account.

A proposer agent builds environments in a terminal from the trainee's rollouts, which the authors say raises the share of valid environments up to 25 times over prompt-only generation.


TestingCatalog reports that New Material, a studio that has built AI tools for Ford, Rafael Nadal and the Wellcome Trust, is joining Mistral AI to help build its frontier solutions.

The post gives no deal terms; it quotes Mistral's statement that New Material is joining to help build its frontier solutions.


The rohanpaul_ai account reports Anthropic is licensing Opaque Systems' confidential-computing technology and hiring some of its engineers, including CEO Aaron Fulkerson and CTO Rishabh Poddar. Neither company's announcement is linked.

Opaque's approach runs AI jobs in hardware-sealed environments that produce cryptographic evidence of what ran, which matters for banks and hospitals; the report is unconfirmed.


The neckbeard plugin, pre-alpha with 155 GitHub stars, has Claude Code and other agents scaffold AWS, GCP or Azure infrastructure and give low, expected and high monthly cost estimates.

It reached the Hacker News front page with 55 points; the README says static validation never claims deployability and calls its deploy lifecycle experimental.


The DeMickyD account on X says a big Elder Scrolls mod project is entirely vibe coded with Claude, music and art included, and that its Patreon asks over $13,000 for the top tier to pay for the AI model.

The post drew 461 likes; the mod's team has not responded in it, and the claim about how the project was built is the poster's.
