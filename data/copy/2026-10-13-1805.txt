Anthropic replaces git with its own system as merges pass 30,000 a day

Anthropic says its monorepo went from 2,500 merges a day in May to over 30,000 in September, and that seven engineers and Claude built a git replacement called Scion in five months.


Google Play lets US adults install their own AI Studio apps without a computer

Google says anyone 18 or older in the US can now build an Android app in AI Studio and launch it privately from Play, with sharing among friends promised in the coming months.


Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-24B-A2B, a 24B model that activates 2.45B parameters

Liquid AI says its largest release yet runs at 184 tokens a second on an Apple M5 Max and beats Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on agent tasks, but trails it on GPQA.

Cognition says Gemini 4 Argon scores 60.5% on its FrontierCode 1.1 test, the best Gemini result on the leaderboard, and is 75% off for Devin users until October 27.

It is an early third-party-style coding score for Google's new model, though Cognition runs the test and sells the product.


Arena says Gemini 4 Argon (High) ranks fifth on its Agent Arena, behind Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra; the best open-weights model is GLM 5.2 at 15th.

The first 14 places all go to proprietary models, so open weights still trail the closed frontier on agent work.
