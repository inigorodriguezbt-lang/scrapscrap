## Liquid AI releases open d1 models that answer questions without writing a word

**Liquid AI says its 3B d1 model returns yes/no, choice and score answers in one forward pass, in 8 milliseconds on an RTX 4090, with a 600M sibling that takes audio.**

Liquid AI has put two open-weight "decision" models on Hugging Face: d1-3B, which reads text, JSON and images, and an experimental d1-omni-600M that takes an image or an audio clip. Neither is a chat model. According to the model cards, both return typed answers to named questions with zero output tokens.

The cards describe three question types: yes/no, pick one named option, and an ordered score from 2 to 10 levels. Liquid AI says d1-3B, built on its LFM2.5-VL-3B, has 3.12 billion parameters and a 32,768-token context. The card lists the licence as lfm1.0 and gives no terms beyond that name.

On speed, Liquid AI says one warm decision takes 8 milliseconds on an Nvidia RTX 4090, 9 on an AMD MI325X and 30 on an Apple M5 Pro. The card scores d1-3B at 48.57 on its Decision Index 0.2.1, which it calls the best under 10B and ahead of a 35B-A3B model at 47.11. Those are the maker's figures; no one else has published results.

The omni model is much smaller. Its card lists 587 million parameters, a 94M vision encoder and a 112M audio encoder, and it takes one 16 kHz clip of up to 30 seconds. It scores 15.95 on the same index. The authors call it an early research release and say audio training covered English speakers talking to an assistant, so other audio may do worse.

The pitch, as the AI commentator kimmonismus put it on X, is a camera that flags a defect or a device that routes a voice command locally, with less data leaving the device. Liquid AI also lists GGUF builds and an 8-bit d1-3B variant. It names transformers, vLLM and SGLang as ways to run it. Independent tests of the models have not appeared yet.
