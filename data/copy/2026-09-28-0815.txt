A 16-author paper introduced RayOrch, a data pipeline system that the team says sped up the MinerU document pipeline 15.14x scaling from 4 to 64 GPUs, and cut end-to-end time 13.1% versus Ray Data.

Foundation-model pipelines increasingly bottleneck on data prep, and the authors' own benchmarks credit the gains to preserving lineage while batching pipelines of uneven size.


Red Queen Bio, an OpenAI-backed biosecurity startup, has raised $36 million to design antibody drugs against pathogens including AI-designed bioweapons, the Wall Street Journal reports.

The round is a sign investors are betting on AI-vs-AI defense as the same model capabilities that speed up drug design raise fears about accelerating bioweapon design too.


The US Department of Homeland Security says it will use AI to process certain Freedom of Information Act requests and recommend what information gets redacted, the Washington Post reports.

Handing an AI system a role in deciding what public records citizens get to see raises fresh questions about transparency at the exact agency responsible for immigration and border enforcement.
