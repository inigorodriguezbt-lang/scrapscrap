## Musk says SpaceX will repay Grok Bot users for errors

**Elon Musk posted that SpaceX will "make you whole" if Grok Bot botches a purchase or leaks a card, adding it has never happened. No written policy backs the pledge.**

Elon Musk said on X on Thursday that SpaceX will "make you whole" if Grok Bot makes a mistake with a purchase or leaks a user's card. In the same post he wrote that this has never happened yet. The post drew 464 likes and 149 replies when the paper read it, and he gave no amount, process or limit.

https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2110610960474079445

An earlier Musk post, about 30 minutes before, told users to ask Grok Bot to buy what they want elsewhere and get a better deal. He wrote that there are far more purchasing websites than Amazon. Neither post says which stores the bot supports, what it asks users to approve, or what plan is required.

The pledge appears to repeat one he made in August. Magica, a news site, reported on 27 August that Musk told an X user weighing bank-account access: "Try it out. If Grok Bot messes up, we will make you whole." The site called that a personal assurance rather than a published reimbursement programme.

Magica also reported that the consumer terms make users responsible for agent actions and generally cap the company's liability at the greater of fees paid or $100, except for gross negligence or wilful misconduct. It did not establish how the pledge relates to those terms, and SpaceX has not published a written policy on repayment that the paper could find.

Magica quoted a security researcher, Rasa Jurgutyte, who warned that an agent could misread a request or be manipulated through prompt injection. The paper could not confirm Musk's claim that no card has leaked. Grok Bot launched in beta on 11 August, according to Magica.
