The Information reports a source says Microsoft is on track to pay Anthropic more than $2 billion a year, as part of a profile of Copilot chief Charles Lamanna.

A 2025 report put Microsoft's Anthropic spend near $500 million a year, so the figure would have roughly quadrupled.


Bloomberg reports DeepSeek has agreed to lead a seed round of tens of millions of dollars in Winverse AI, a robotics AI startup, at a valuation near $400 million, according to sources.

It is a rare outside investment by DeepSeek, and it points the Chinese lab toward embodied AI.


Broadcom's Hock Tan and Intel's Lip-Bu Tan said in Singapore that access to capital and rising borrowing costs could become AI's main bottleneck, CNBC reports.

Two chip chiefs naming money rather than silicon as the limit is a shift from the usual supply-chain talk.


Krea posted a video teaser saying "something is coming" and asking who wants early access; it had 246 likes and more than 100 replies within an hour.

Krea has not said whether the release is a model, a tool or a feature, so the teaser is all there is.

TestingCatalog says DigitalOcean has opened a public preview of Signals and Simulations, which flag failed agent sessions and replay them with a proposed fix, plus a private preview of Agent Data Services.

Replaying the exact failed conversation against a fix is a testing step that agent teams have mostly built by hand.

Microsoft researchers' MiniCorp simulator runs a firm of AI agents through an e-commerce business against a simulated market; a four-week 20% discount lifted units sold by 71%, DAIR.AI summarises.

It is a testbed for agents on long-running business work, with the market checked against published studies of real ones.


SemiAnalysis says Taiwanese prices for advanced-packaging substrates have tripled since mid-2025 to an all-time high, while Japanese prices are rising far less sharply.

Substrates are a quiet constraint on AI chip output, and the gap between the two markets is unexplained.
