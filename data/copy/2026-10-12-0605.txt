Samsung is developing Z-NAND-O for on-device AI, plus Z-NAND-T (high-bandwidth flash) and Z-NAND-I, a high-IOPS NAND intended for Nvidia, according to Korean media reports relayed by analyst Jukan.

Flash that sits closer to the processor is one route to holding larger models in memory, if the reports prove accurate.


Former DeepMind scientist David Silver told the Financial Times his startup Ineffable is building an AI "super learner" with reinforcement learning as an alternative path to large language models.

Silver led DeepMind's reinforcement-learning work, so a lab built around that approach is a notable bet against pure LLM scaling.


Theo says he added a proper "/goal" flow and a visual callout to the nightly build of T3 Code, his coding-agent tool.

It is a small feature, but it shows how quickly independent coding-agent tools are copying each other's workflow ideas.


ProgramGym, a paper trending on Hugging Face with 30 upvotes, proposes scalable, verifiable training environments in which coding agents must work out what a program needs and design it from scratch.

Most coding-agent training fixes bugs in existing repositories, so environments that test building from nothing fill a gap the authors describe.


Qwen's VisionWeave paper, with 25 upvotes on Hugging Face, proposes letting multimodal models encode images at variable detail instead of dense fixed-size patch tokens, to cut visual-encoding cost.

Image tokens are a major cost in multimodal models, and the authors argue detail is unevenly needed across an image.


SoloQ, from the University of Southern California, is a calibration-free quantization method for diffusion language models, according to its paper, which has 21 upvotes on Hugging Face.

Diffusion language models are an alternative to autoregressive ones, and compression methods like this decide whether they become cheap to deploy.


A Hugging Face paper with 23 upvotes targets stereo audio for joint video-audio generation, so that perceived sound location follows the motion of the visual source.

Stereo sound is needed for AR, VR and games, and the authors say current video-audio generators largely overlook it.
