OpenAI ships GPT-Live-1.1 voice with six new languages

OpenAI says GPT-Live-1.1 is rolling out today to all users in ChatGPT, Codex and dots, with new voices in six languages and fewer "checking..." pauses.

https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2110797830361436604


Microsoft finishes MAI-Base-2, a 3-trillion-parameter base model built in-house

Microsoft AI says its MAI-Base-2 base model beats every open-weight model it tested and is 4 to 12 times more pre-training efficient than DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base.


Xiaomi's MiMo V2.6 Pro takes top open spot in Arena's Agent leaderboard

Arena says MiMo V2.6 Pro leads open models in Agent Arena at $0.11 per task, and is one of only two open models on the cost frontier.


Liquid AI releases d1-ultra, a model it says tops a decision index

Liquid AI says d1-ultra, a multimodal model covering 44 languages, scores 65 and ranks first on Artificial Analysis's Decision Index.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Awni Hannun says two M5 Ultra machines running MLX can now do something he calls pretty remarkable, in a post with a demo clip.

MLX is an array framework for running models on Apple chips; the post does not say which model was running.


fal says its new fal Worlds lets users build characters, explore environments and direct scenes in AI-generated worlds in real time, using models including LingBot World and H3 Max Director.

It is a hosted playground for interactive world models, which until now mostly ran as research demos; fal gave no pricing in the post.

https://x.com/fal/status/2110795863861674073


Vals AI says it asked six personal agents to win a £350 train refund, told them the user would be unreachable, and gave each 40 million tokens, email and web access.

The test probes how far autonomous assistants go when told to ignore limits; Vals has not been checked on its results here.


The Information reports, via International Cyber Digest, that Cursor engineers feared customer data reached SpaceX's AI unit for training, and a Cursor lawyer wrote it had no right to train on it.

Cursor has not been heard from in the posts reviewed, so the report stands as one outlet's account of internal messages.


Nvidia says it is expanding its partnership with Volvo Cars, pairing Volvo's safety work with Nvidia's Hyperion, Halos OS and Alpamayo driving software in an upcoming Volvo car.

It is another carmaker tying its automated driving to Nvidia's stack; Nvidia gave no model name or date for the car.


Perplexity says Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is now available to Pro and Max subscribers and in its Agent API, in a post with a short clip.

It follows Nvidia's release of the open 120B vision model, and shows how quickly open weights reach consumer apps.

https://x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2110793639727358422


vLLM says it has day-zero support for Meta's Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter model with 42B active per token and 1M-token context, verified on Nvidia and AMD GPUs and on TPUs.

Day-zero support across three chip families means the open model can be served on most major hardware immediately.
