Beijing Academy of AI researchers released AgentSWE, a 25-task benchmark of coding agents building agents; they report the best creation score was 19.1, by DeepSeek-V4-Pro, on held-out cases.

The authors say agents that look finished on development cases mostly fail on unseen ones, with 58.1% of traced creation runs failing a validity gate.


A Stanford-linked paper says turning thinking off does not stop reasoning: it reports DeepSeek-V4-Flash showed visible explicit inference in 99.9% of open-ended answers with thinking disabled.

The authors call it thinking inertia and say stricter answer-only prompts cut open-ended accuracy by about 15 points on average.


Reuters reports, per IDC figures relayed by mark_k, that Nvidia shipped 678,000 AI accelerators in China in the first half of 2026, down 44% on the year, against 572,000 for Huawei.

That leaves Nvidia on 34% of China's data-centre AI-chip market and Huawei on 28%, a six-point gap, as US export limits bite.


Researcher eliebakouch says Meta's Muse Wonder launch shows no cost-versus-score chart, only bar plots, and that the one Artificial Analysis plot has it slightly behind Gemini 4 (high) at over twice the cost.

It is an early outside challenge to Meta's frontier claims for Muse Wonder, though Bakouch himself warns against over-reading that single plot.
