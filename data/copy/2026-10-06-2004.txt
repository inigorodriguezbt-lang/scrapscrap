Anthropic opens Mythos 5.1 to vetted security teams in three tiers

Simon Willison has Opus 5.5 write six game tracks and a player in one prompt

OpenAI says it is cutting its API usage tiers from five to three, Build, Launch and Grow, and developers can reach the top Grow tier with $500 in total payments, down from $1,000.

The lower threshold gives smaller teams higher rate limits sooner, and OpenAI says paid organisations move to their new tier automatically.


Google's Logan Kilpatrick says Nano Banana 2.1 is live in the Gemini API, AI Studio and the Gemini app, with higher image quality, bug fixes from earlier models and a lower price.

It is the official launch of the image model testers spotted earlier, and the price cut applies to developers paying per image.


TestingCatalog reports AgentMail has launched AgentID, a sign-in option that lets AI agents use apps with their own verified email, linked to a named human owner, much like Sign in with Google.

Apps that blocked agent signups could now admit them while seeing who is responsible, with per-owner limits and the ability to disable individual agents.


Fal says its media-generation service is now available inside ChatGPT and Codex, so users can generate images and video, browse them in the conversation and pin their fal Media Library to the sidebar.

It is one more generative-media provider arriving inside OpenAI's chat apps, rather than users switching tabs to make and fetch assets.

Mistral says its new Large 4 model solved 18 of 19 challenges in a cybersecurity capture-the-flag speedrun, and posted a clip of the run, with tool calls and solve times it says come from actual runs.

The figure is Mistral's own, with no independent tally yet, but it is the most concrete cyber result the company has shown for the model.

Engineer Poteto says he automated his team's release and QA with Grok Bot, using two Slack bots, a release manager and an engineer, plus a fuzz swarm of 10 or more agents running Grok 4.7.

The post drew over 900 likes within two hours, a sign of interest in chat-bot teammates handling releases, though the setup is the author's own account.
