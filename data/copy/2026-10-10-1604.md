## Hidden Voice tab in Claude hints Anthropic may be building its own voice models

**TestingCatalog found a "Preview" Voice item in Claude's web navigation, days after Anthropic added an opt-in to share voice data for training. Anthropic has said nothing.**

TestingCatalog reported on Saturday that Claude's web navigation now contains a separate, hidden Voice item labelled "Preview". The outlet says the label points to Anthropic's internal preview environment rather than a public release. Anthropic has not commented, and nothing is known about what the tab does or how it would differ from Claude's existing voice mode.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2108940487658807525

The finding follows a new setting in Claude's mobile apps. According to TestingCatalog, Anthropic added an optional switch, off by default, that lets users share voice recordings and voice chat data for model training. It is separate from the consent for text conversations, and TestingCatalog posted a screenshot of the prompt on 4 October.

TestingCatalog says the two together could mean Anthropic is preparing its own speech models, but it adds that the data could equally support the voice features Claude already has. Claude's voice mode today works on mobile, desktop and web and uses the same language models to generate spoken replies.

The outlet also notes that Anthropic reportedly held earlier talks with ElevenLabs about voice partnerships, which it says has not been officially confirmed. As far as TestingCatalog reports, Anthropic has not released its own text-to-speech models through its API or a native real-time audio model. No timeline has been reported, and no one else has independently confirmed the Voice item.
