Apple says AI agents force new limits on macOS Full Disk Access

Apple says it will make users take "very explicit" action before an app gets Full Disk Access, citing AI agents that may read files, mail, messages and browsing history.


US charges California CEO over $300 million of Nvidia servers sent to China

Prosecutors say Greg Lui of Earthmade Computer shipped Nvidia-powered servers through Malaysia and Singapore on false paperwork, in a scheme they say ran from 2023 into August 2026.


Ataraxos beats Stratego's top human 15-1 after a week on 16 GPUs

A Carnegie Mellon-led team says its Ataraxos system beat Pim Niemeijer, the game's most decorated player, in a 20-game match on under $8,000 of compute.
