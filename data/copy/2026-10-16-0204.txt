SemiAnalysis says Google submitted its first TPUv7 agentic benchmark result, claiming 1.42x better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72 at FP4 and 2.5x better than AMD's MI355X.

The firm also says GB200 still holds a 1.2x edge at low interactivity, so the advantage depends on the workload and the cost basis it uses.


Theo says T3 Code, his coding-agent app, is out of alpha, in a post that had about 300 likes when read on Thursday.

It adds another independent coding front end to a field crowded with editor-based agents, though the post gives no feature list.


PrismML says it is running an open challenge with Darkbloom on Yukon Research to make its new Bonsai 2 9B model run faster, and that the model is also available through Darkbloom's API.

It is a community speed contest on a ternary 2 GB model, and the rules and prize are on Yukon Research rather than in the post.

Reuters reports, citing sources, that some Anthropic investors discussed a valuation near $1.5 trillion for an IPO, down from the roughly $2 trillion they had expected, after disclosures showed slower third-quarter revenue growth.

No IPO has been filed, and the figures come from unnamed sources, so the valuation talk could change before any listing.


The Information reports OpenAI is cutting ad rates in ChatGPT, chasing small and midsize advertisers and hiring talent from Meta as it grows its advertising business.

Lower prices aimed at smaller buyers suggest OpenAI wants ChatGPT ads to scale quickly, though the report is behind a paywall and OpenAI has not commented.


Google says more than 400 publications, including the Financial Times, CNN and the Texas Tribune, are now in its News AI pilot, which tests features such as article overviews, Nieman Lab reports.

The pilot is Google's test of AI summaries with publishers who have worried about lost traffic, so its expansion shows how many have agreed to take part.
