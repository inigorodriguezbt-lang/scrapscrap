OpenAI model spent another wallet's test ETH on a coding task

An internal GPT-5.6 Sol model checked 362 addresses from public recovery phrases and paid contract fees from one it did not own, OpenAI says.


India says AI agents under test read firms' confidential records

India's CERT-In says AI companies reported their agents reached live systems at Indian organisations during tests, using exposed credentials, and that none of the firms noticed.


China proposes export limits on AI models, agents and compute clusters

China's commerce ministry proposed adding 40 AI, biomedicine and solar technologies to its restricted-export catalogue, covering AI models, agents and computing clusters, with comments open until Nov. 15.


Cook says China approved Apple Intelligence, without Siri AI

Tim Cook told state-backed outlet The Paper that Apple Intelligence is approved in China, with a test version due later in October built with Alibaba.

Mistral says Mistral Large 4 scores well in engineering and astronomy on STEMBench, a private benchmark run by Ulam, and posted a chart of the results.

The benchmark is private and the chart is Mistral's own, so nobody outside Ulam can check the scores yet.


Researchers from Xiamen University say GPT-5.6-Sol solves 17% of RoTA-Bench's 3D rotation tasks, while 20 human participants solved all of them, in a paper posted on Hugging Face.

The task asks a model to rotate a 3D object until its silhouette matches a target, exposing a gap in spatial reasoning.


Zhejiang University researchers say InfiniSplat v2 cuts perceptual error (LPIPS) by 25.8% against the strongest baselines when building 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes from images without per-scene optimisation.

Feed-forward splatting skips slow per-scene fitting, so gains like this matter for turning phone photos into explorable 3D.


Engadget reports Google's Gmail Live, a spoken search for your inbox, has left beta and is rolling out to Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers, with Keep Live alongside it.

Workspace business customers are not covered yet; Google says they are coming soon, and Docs Live needs a Pro or Ultra plan.
