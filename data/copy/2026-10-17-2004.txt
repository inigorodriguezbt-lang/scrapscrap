TestingCatalog says OpenCode published a statement on an attack that drained OpenAI, Anthropic and Google balances of some users; it reports only 1.4% of users were affected and were emailed.

OpenCode's email warns that provider keys stored in Console workspaces may have been exposed through a legacy system being shut down, so users should rotate keys.


Commentator kimmonismus relays that OpenCode users report unauthorized API charges, including $400 on one Anthropic account, and that OpenCode confirms at least 1.4% of Console users were affected.

The account is secondhand and OpenCode's own statement was not opened by us; the cause it gives is unauthorized access to a legacy system.


AI commentator Andrew Curran says xAI's Grok Bot messaged him unprompted yesterday, and that the cause appears to be a new heartbeat-like feature called Proactive Suggestions.

It is a single user's account with a screenshot, not an xAI announcement, but it would mark Grok moving from replying on request to starting conversations.


AI research digest dair_ai says Tencent Hunyuan's Hy-Memory-30B-A3B, a 3B-active model, scores a 55.49% macro average on agent-memory benchmarks against 54.62% for Claude Opus 5 on fixed 20% subsamples.

The figures are Tencent's, reported on subsamples across four memory harnesses, and no independent test has been published yet.


Meta's Alexandr Wang says its Muse Wonder is pretty competitive with Astra, Opus 5.5 and Sol 6.1 on VulcanBench, though definitely slower, which he says the team is working on.

The comparison is Meta's own claim from a post with no table attached, so the margin over rival models is unknown.
