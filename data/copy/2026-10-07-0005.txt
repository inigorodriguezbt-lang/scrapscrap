OpenAI publishes 722 math manuscripts from an unreleased internal model

OpenAI says its internal model was posed about 4,000 problems and produced results grouped into 372 families, at roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro compute each. Many have Lean proofs, and many do not.


Google ships Nano Banana 2.1 image model at half the price of 2.0

Google says Nano Banana 2.1 improves on its predecessor across the board and costs $0.0336 per 1K image, down from $0.067. It is live in Gemini, Search AI Mode and AI Studio.

Cursor says its iOS app can now control agents running on your own computer from your phone: check in, reply, or start new tasks, with a demo video posted.

It moves the coding-agent loop off the desk, so a long job on a laptop no longer needs the laptop in reach.

https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2107618653701296162


Developer Theo says Opus 5.5 produced real UI mocks for five different design treatments and rendered them in the chat thread, in a video he posted.

It is one developer's demo of a model drawing working interface variants inline, not a benchmark, but it shows the workflow people are testing.

https://x.com/theo/status/2107615261893464226


Commentator kimmonismus says a developer used Opus 5.5 to build seven free Rust replacements for Adobe apps; the PhotoCraft repo calls itself early alpha and shows 6.5k stars.

The README says PhotoCraft is not yet a Photoshop replacement for daily professional work, so the star count outruns the software.


OpenAI's Vaibhav Srivastav says the Decisions API is in public beta, using GPT-6 Luna to classify and route inputs about 10 times faster than the Responses API, from $0.10 per million input tokens.

It targets cheap high-volume sorting jobs, though a published rate for the API itself was not found, so the price is OpenAI's own claim.


Korean broker note shared by Jukan says Meritz Securities expects some mobile DRAM prices to jump 50% in the first quarter of 2027 as makers shift output to server DRAM and HBM.

It is one broker's channel-check forecast, but it shows AI server demand starting to push up phone memory costs.
