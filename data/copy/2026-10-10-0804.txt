A paper on Looped Prediction Transformers reports that sharing one KV cache across loops cut context memory by 76 to 79 percent at five loops, with lower perplexity than a same-size Transformer.

The trade-off, per the alphaXiv summary, is roughly 3 to 3.5 times the inference cost, so it saves memory rather than compute.
