Google's Gemini desktop app is testing a "Full Access" mode for computer use

A hidden setting in Google's Gemini desktop app would let it read, change or delete files anywhere on a Mac and act without asking first, TestingCatalog reports.


llama.cpp adds an endpoint for decision models

Georgi Gerganov says the latest llama.cpp builds serve decision models through a new /v1/systemone endpoint, and ggml-org has posted Kev-4B, a 4-billion-parameter model under Apache 2.0.

Bloomberg reports, citing people with knowledge, that Broadcom's Wall Street syndicate, with Blackstone and banks, has begun gathering $60 billion of fresh AI chip financing to benefit Anthropic and other companies.

It follows an Anthropic filing that showed Broadcom lending up to $42 billion, and shows how chip purchases are now being financed with debt.


The Decoder, citing the Wall Street Journal, says a fourth OpenAI safety researcher, David Robinson, left shortly after three were fired, according to an anonymous X account.

The departure is unconfirmed by OpenAI, which has only said an investigation found violations of its rules on handling sensitive information.


Hugging Face says the same model weights score 62% in one coding-agent harness and 33% in another, and has released an open guide to multi-harness reinforcement learning built on a recording proxy.

It suggests agent benchmark scores depend heavily on the surrounding harness, not only on the model being tested.


Tom's Hardware reports Amazon and Synopsys have signed a multi-year agreement worth over a billion dollars, in which Amazon licenses Synopsys chip designs and tools to build AI chips, and Synopsys adopts Amazon Bedrock.

It ties Amazon's custom-silicon push to the chip-design software vendor, and puts Bedrock agents inside chip design work.


AWS published a guide to using Claude Platform on AWS from one subscription, covering cross-account SigV4 for AWS workloads, workspace-scoped API keys for developers and OIDC federation for outside environments.

It shows how companies are expected to split Claude access across teams and accounts while keeping workspaces isolated.


Arena says it post-trains image models on a composite reward, including a Bradley-Terry model trained on about 5.6 million pairwise human votes, plus checks for faithfulness to the prompt.

Arena argues human preference alone can reward images that look appealing but miss details, so it adds checks against reward-hacking.


Bloomberg reports Nvidia hit a record high, taking its market value to roughly $5.7 trillion, as relayed by commentator Kimmonismus, who says optimism over AI agents drove chip demand.

It comes after a nearly 25% rebound from late July and a record $150 billion rise in the buyback authorisation announced Monday.
