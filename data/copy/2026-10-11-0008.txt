A Hacker News front-page post by Nish Tahir builds a one-forward-pass multiple-choice model on Qwen3-1.7B; a quick finetune lifts CommonsenseQA accuracy from 59.4% to 62.4%, and temperature scaling fixes its overconfidence.

It is a small, reproducible recipe, with code on GitHub, for the answer-by-scoring-options approach that Microsoft and Cloudflare models recently used.

It is SemiAnalysis's own reading of a deal the companies have not detailed, and the thread sets out how that price pencils out.


Andrew Curran notes that a 4x internal productivity uplift for Mythos Preview matches what Anthropic self-reported in its August 2026 Risk Report, still below the company's Dramatic Acceleration threshold.

The figure is Anthropic's own and covers an older model; Curran adds that the company is "many generations" beyond it now, which is his claim.


A paper highlighted by DAIR.AI proposes MemoType, which labels each agent memory and query by type and retrieves with a separate strategy per type; it also introduces a dataset, TriMEM, for training the classifier.

The authors argue that no single retrieval method can be best across mixed memory types, which challenges the one-embedding-search default in many agent setups.
