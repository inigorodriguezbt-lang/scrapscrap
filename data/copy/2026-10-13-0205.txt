Reuters reports Japan's Omron will buy US industrial PC maker OnLogic and its parent Interlogic for $675 million, the company said on Tuesday, to push into what it calls physical AI.

Rugged factory-floor computers are the hardware layer physical AI runs on, so the deal shows automation firms buying compute rather than building it.


A PhotonCap summary of AMD's OCP keynote says Lisa Su introduced a 6th Gen EPYC "Verano" CPU with an LPDDR memory interface, available in 2027, alongside a Helion agentic-AI rack.

LPDDR on a server CPU targets the memory demands of agent workloads; the details come from a second-hand summary, not AMD's own release.


Intel's product page for the D270 inference card lists up to 480 GB of LPDDR5X, 350 W air cooling and a PCIe 5.0 form factor, with no shipping date and no performance figures.

Trading HBM for cheap high-capacity LPDDR is Intel's bet that long-context inference needs memory size more than raw bandwidth.


StockSavvyShay says Meta is adopting Nvidia Spectrum-6 switches for its Vera Rubin AI factories, using its own open-source software to connect more than 100,000 GPUs with fewer networking layers.

It shows Nvidia selling networking to Meta even as Meta builds its own MTIA chips; the claim comes from an investor account, not Meta.


Hesamation quotes an Anthropic report saying requests to Moonshot's Kimi models were secretly forwarded to Claude, including one from a suspected People's Liberation Army-linked user analysing CCTV footage of a person.

It is Anthropic's own account of a distillation case, and Moonshot has not been shown responding to it in the material seen.


Axios reports Suki, an AI ambient scribe for healthcare providers, raised a $90 million Series E led by Healthier Capital; a source told Axios it projects $60 million to $80 million in revenue this year.

Clinical note-taking is one of the few AI product categories with revenue already at scale, and the revenue figure is a single anonymous source's.
