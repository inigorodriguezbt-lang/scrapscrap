Upstage released Solar Mini 4, a proprietary 35B-parameter model with 3B active, scoring 24 on Artificial Analysis's index, priced at $0.10 and $0.40 per million tokens.

Artificial Analysis says it costs about five times as much per task as GPT-6 Luna because it uses far more tokens.


Artificial Analysis says some frontier models refuse over 85% of tasks on its CyberGym-E2E-AA defence benchmark, while GPT-6 Luna or MiMo-V2.6-Pro can run about 100 bug hunts for roughly $20.

The firm argues safety blocks on offensive cyber work also stop defenders, since finding and proving a bug takes the same steps.


Theo's T3 Code nightly added a "start thread with no project" option that creates a new directory in ~/.t3 for each thread, the way Codex and Claude do.

The post drew over 700 likes within hours of going up, a sign of how closely developers watch coding-agent tooling.


Testing Catalog reports Google is building a security review mode for AI Studio that applies secure coding practices, alongside a Plan mode, both still in development.

TestingCatalog found it in the product and says nothing is confirmed until Google ships it.


Meta researchers say AI agents do better and cost less when they decide for themselves what to keep as their context fills, according to Rohan Paul's summary of the paper.

The finding argues against hard-coded rules such as summarising everything when full, and needs no extra training, per the summary.


Greg Brockman has no plans to donate beyond his first $25 million to the Leading the Future PAC, calling it a distraction at OpenAI, the New York Times reports.

The Times cites a Slack message; Brockman signed the White House AI pledge the same week.


Gavin Newsom signed California's No Robo Bosses Act, which stops employers relying solely on AI to fire or discipline workers, CNBC reports.

It is one of the first state rules to require a human in workplace disciplinary decisions made with AI.
