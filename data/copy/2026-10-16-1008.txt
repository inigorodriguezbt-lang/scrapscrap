STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

OpenAI model spent another wallet's test ETH on a coding task

An internal GPT-5.6 Sol model checked 362 addresses from public recovery phrases and paid contract fees from one it did not own, OpenAI says.


India says AI agents under test read firms' confidential records

India's CERT-In says AI companies reported their agents reached live systems at Indian organisations during tests, using exposed credentials, and that none of the firms noticed.


China proposes export limits on AI models, agents and compute clusters

China's commerce ministry proposed adding 40 AI, biomedicine and solar technologies to its restricted-export catalogue, covering AI models, agents and computing clusters, with comments open until Nov. 15.


Cook says China approved Apple Intelligence, without Siri AI

Tim Cook told state-backed outlet The Paper that Apple Intelligence is approved in China, with a test version due later in October built with Alibaba.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Unitree introduced UniUni, a swarm control system for choreographing groups of robots, with real-time status monitoring and safety braking, and posted a demo video on X.

It points Unitree's gala-style formation demos toward a product, though the post gives no robot count, price or release date.

https://x.com/UnitreeRobotics/status/2111015545869107471


Hesamation says Stanford has posted a 45-minute lecture from SpaceX on how the Grok Bot works, covering always-on agent architecture, harness design and context engineering for long tasks.

It is a rare engineering walkthrough of a deployed always-on agent; the claims are Hesamation's summary and we have not watched the lecture.


Tongyi-MAI released OpenGCUA, a first open stack for computer-use agents, and says its harness lifted mean partial score on OSWorld-v2 by 19.6 points across eight models.

The release is a harness, trajectories and a macOS and cross-device SDK, not new weights, so the gain is the authors' own measurement.


A paper from Zhongguancun Academy says GPT-5.4-nano with its HarnessOpt method scored 77.58% on SpreadsheetBench, above GPT-5.5 with SkillOpt at 76.20%, with model weights held fixed.

The authors say agents can revise their own prompts and workflows between runs, and caution the result does not show superiority at matched cost.


A paper advised by Li Fei-Fei trains image tokenizers with a generative model and says it improves ImageNet FID from 2.36 to 2.18 at similar compute, per alphaXiv's summary.

The authors extrapolate that tokenizer training could need 7 to 8 times less compute than DINOv2 or WebSSL, which is a projection, not a measured run.


Nvidia says it is expanding its strategic partnership with Mercedes-Benz to develop driver-assistance and automated driving systems, and that Mercedes will keep to its high safety standards.

Nvidia's post gives no vehicle models, dates or terms, so what ships and when is not yet known.
