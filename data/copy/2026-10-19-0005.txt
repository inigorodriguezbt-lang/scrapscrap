SemiAnalysis says Inferact, the makers of vLLM, and Google made a first preliminary TPUv7 submission for the 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3, claiming strong performance per total cost against Nvidia GB200 NVL72, B300 and AMD MI355.

If it holds up, Google's TPUs become a credible rival to Nvidia for serving the largest open models, and vLLM's makers are driving it.


TestingCatalog says Anthropic's Claude personal-finance feature, which reads bank data through Plaid and builds dashboards, has been renamed from 'Claude Money' in the latest app update, and may launch soon. Unconfirmed.

Andrew Curran separately called the product 'Claude Monet'; Anthropic has announced nothing, so the name and the timing both rest on app strings.


Hugging Face's Thomas Wolf says the most built-upon model on the Hub is Black Forest Labs' FLUX.1-dev, with 43,000 derivative models, ahead of Llama and Qwen.

He also says Europe trained about 8% of the Hub's open-model compute but accounts for about 18% of what is built on top of it, his own estimate.


International Cyber Digest says a draft Chinese export-control list would require licences to export AI that finds five or more unknown severe flaws in a major OS, or models above 2 trillion parameters.

Chinese ministries published the draft on October 16 for comment until November 15; it reads as restrictions and registration rather than a blanket ban.


Developer finereli released whatsapp-mcp, an MIT-licensed read-only server that lets an AI agent search your WhatsApp messages, running on Cloudflare Workers. It had 14 GitHub stars and 25 Show HN points.

The README says it breaks WhatsApp's terms, stores messages in plaintext in your own Cloudflare account, and has been tested on only one account.


Researcher Elvis Saravia summarises a paper in which an engine generates data-analysis training environments from scratch; reinforcement learning on 2,135 tasks lifts Qwen3.6-35B-A3B by 20.9 points on DABstep-Hard.

Because the generating process is known, answers can be checked, which the authors use to train without distilled trajectories; the results are the paper's own.
