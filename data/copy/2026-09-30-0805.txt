Mathematicians advise OpenAI to release AI proofs with prompts and costs

An independent group including Timothy Gowers, Martin Hairer and Edward Witten wants AI labs to publish prompts, formal proofs and failed attempts, and to fund human understanding of any result.


China's ModelScope and MoArk race to replace Hugging Face at home

Rest of World says Alibaba's ModelScope hosts 170,000 models and OSChina's MoArk about 20,000, against more than 3 million on Hugging Face, which Nvidia is reportedly buying for $12.9 billion.

Context Language Models, a paper Aran Komatsuzaki credits to Meta, treat context as an editable file; the authors report 11.4% higher accuracy with 21.5% fewer FLOPs on BrowseComp-Plus.

The paper argues that context management should be learned by the model rather than handled by an external harness, and reports gains on 12- and 24-hour agent tasks.


The Financial Times reports Singapore is doubling down on chipmaking as the AI boom lifts its economy, while high costs, scarce land and labour and US trade scrutiny threaten those ambitions.

A small trading hub is betting on semiconductors just as US export policy and land constraints tighten around the industry it wants to grow.


Citrix NetScaler flaw CVE-2026-88772 is already exploited in the wild, and watchTowr says 120 crafted DTLS records overrun a 35,840-byte buffer to reach root-level code execution, The Hacker News reports.

The analysis gives defenders the exploit chain in detail; NetScaler gateways sit at the edge of many corporate networks, including ones that host AI services.
