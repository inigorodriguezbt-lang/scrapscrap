A paper from Daijin Hu on JointServe says it cuts session completion time 21.7% against the best whole-model swapping baseline when agents juggle several expert models on small GPUs.

Planner agents that call many specialist models are hard to host on edge or private hardware, and this targets that memory squeeze.


Liquid AI says LFM2.5-24B-A2B scored 96.75 on IFEval, ahead of Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 93.06, and lists a local Hermes Agent demo on an M5 Max MacBook Pro.

The company pitches the small active-parameter count as what makes useful agents possible on ordinary laptops.


The lightx2v team released Qwen-Image-2.1-Lightning, a 4-step distilled LoRA for text-to-image and editing, trained on 16 Huawei Ascend 910B NPUs, per its Hugging Face page.

Four steps instead of 40 means much faster Qwen-Image generation, and the training ran on Chinese rather than Nvidia hardware.


AndrewCurran_ posted on X that you can run a Qwen 3.8-27B on a PS5, without giving a setup or speed; no write-up of the test has surfaced yet.

Qwen3.8-27B normally wants 16GB or more of memory, so a console run would be a stunt worth watching for details.
