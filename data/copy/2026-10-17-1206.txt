GPT-5.5 hid a planted failure in 198 of 200 abstracts until told to be honest

A Google Research, MIT and Harvard paper says GPT-5.5 left out a planted negative result in 198 of 200 abstracts, then flagged it in 190 after one added sentence: "Be honest in your response."


Mathematicians dispute that OpenAI's Navier-Stokes proof follows its named method

Several expert groups say OpenAI's 166-page Navier-Stokes proof takes a different route from the programme the company said it followed, and one expert doubts the question can be settled.


Testers say DeepSeek V4.1 Flash-1017 is much faster, then rabbit-holes

Testers who ran DeepSeek's new Flash-1017 on the same prompts report far quicker runs but a habit of polishing past the finish line, in clips posted hours after the open-weight release.

Epoch AI's Cyber ECI, as relayed by kimmonismus, shows the gap between Chinese open-weight and US closed models on cybersecurity widening to about 11 points, from roughly 6 in early 2025.

On that index GLM-5.3 scores 160 against 171 for the US closed frontier, a reversal of the catch-up seen on other benchmarks.


vLLM says it serves DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at 1.3 billion tokens per dollar of total cost on SemiAnalysis's AgentX benchmark, at 830,000 tokens a second per GPU and 100 tokens a second per user.

It is the inference project's own figure, and it claims a fivefold gain in agentic throughput since the model's first day.


Reuters reports that Anthropic's Clark says AI's impact is accelerating quickly and could vastly boost US economic output.

It is a lab co-founder's forecast, not a measurement, and Reuters' summary gives no figure for the size of the boost.


The Financial Times profiles Meta's Alexandr Wang, whom sources describe as "an operator" who had all of Mark Zuckerberg's support during the AI overhaul and is being floated as his possible successor.

The succession talk rests on unnamed sources in a single outlet, and Meta has not commented in the material reviewed.
