Axios reports Reflection AI is about to release an open-weight model

Axios says Reflection AI's first open-weight model is close and should compete with top Chinese open models. Reflection, which pays SpaceX $150 million a month for compute, declined to comment.


OpenAI's Codex lead promises a fix or usage reset daily for 28 days

Tibo Sottiaux, who leads Codex at OpenAI, said each day for 28 days will bring either a clear improvement or a full usage reset. The post drew more than 9,000 likes within two hours.

Google Research's RRSI repo, which reins in agent harnesses that overfit their own benchmarks, has 1.2k GitHub stars; the authors say it gained up to 4.7 points on unseen benchmarks.

Self-improving agents tend to memorise their test tasks, and the Apache-2.0 code lets anyone try the authors' fix with budgeted edits and a critic.


Reuters reports Elon Musk said he will rename SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI.

Reuters's post gives no reason or date for the change, so how it would apply to the xAI brand is not yet clear.


The Information reports, citing sources, that Anthropic's stock match of employee charity gifts passed $660 million in the six months to March and could reach billions after an IPO.

The match is paid in company stock, so the report says it would dilute shareholders.


The Verge says in StarSkirmish, which pits AI-written StarCraft bots against each other, GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 tied as best AI-made bots but could not top the human-made Stardust.

The Verge's headline says the AI cheated after failing to win, citing Kotaku's account of Friday's matches, which this paper did not read.


Tom's Hardware reports the California State Athletic Commission issued a cease-and-desist after a robotics startup staged a human-versus-humanoid cage fight, and threatened misdemeanor charges after a YouTuber fought three robots.

It is an early sign of regulators treating robot fighting as a sport they license, though the commission's own order was not seen.


Bloomberg reports former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon will testify Monday at a New York City Council hearing alongside representatives of Google, OpenAI and Meta.

Coxon has warned that AI could become too powerful for humans to control, and the hearing puts him in the room with the labs.


Kimmonismus's rumour watchlist says Qwen 4 is being discussed for October or early November, with Alibaba confirming it is in training; the other open-weight dates are his estimates.

It is speculation from a commentator, not a lab date, but it shows what open-model watchers expect as Reflection's release nears.
