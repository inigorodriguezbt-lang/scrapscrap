Accelevation and backer Olympus raised $540 million in a US IPO, selling 30 million shares at $18 each, below the marketed $20 to $24 range, Bloomberg reported.

An AI-infrastructure listing pricing under its range is a small read on how public investors are valuing the AI buildout.


Reuters reports Anthropic's IPO filing shows 47% of its 2025 sales, about $2.16 billion, ran through cloud partners Amazon and Google, which took roughly $351 million back in distribution fees.

The filing shows how much of Anthropic's revenue depends on the same cloud companies that also invest in it and compete with it.


Digiday reports Amazon Ads used its unBoxed conference to move AI from a feature to a central role in ad buying, folding previously separate ad workflows together.

Amazon is one of the largest ad platforms, so its buying tools becoming AI-led changes how a lot of advertisers work.


Meta chief Mark Zuckerberg called it a significant positive step that leaders of every major American lab committed to strong internal controls and multiple layers of audits and reviews.

His comment on the White House accord makes Meta's public stance on the voluntary agreement explicit, alongside the other signatories.


OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux says a few million Dots, its always-on agents, will be online within days, with the ability to create a team of them held back while OpenAI watches usage.

The head start on single agents suggests OpenAI is deliberately staging multi-agent teams behind data from how people use one.


OpenAI's Sottiaux says a Dot's own direct work draws nothing from a plan's usage, while Codex tasks it creates are billed as usual; faster, higher-bandwidth Dots will be a paid feature later.

It answers a community note on how Dots are billed, and shows where OpenAI plans to charge.


ChatGPT now has shareable profiles that bring a user's Sites and plugins together for others to find and reuse, OpenAI says, for Free, Go, Plus, Pro and Business users.

Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare plans follow soon, and skills shared with teammates stay inside the workspace.


Krea showed Window Lab, an app built with its Krea Agent: users upload a video and adjust motion controls to get the result they want.

It is a small example of an agent building a working creative tool rather than producing a single image.

IQuest-Q1 is a 320-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model, 15 billion active per token, with a 524,288-token context, its makers say; it is text-only and trending on Hugging Face with 112 likes.

The card admits substantial limitations and warns code may be wrong, and the benchmark claims are IQuestLab's own.
