## Yandex says a third data centre is shut after drone attack

**Reuters reports Yandex shut a data centre near Vladimir after a drone attack, the third in four days. Two of the firm's three AI-model supercomputers are in Sasovo, hit on October 8.**

Yandex said a data centre in the Vladimir area was shut down after a drone attack, Reuters reported on Sunday. It is the third Yandex site disrupted since Thursday. Reuters' short report did not say who launched the drones, and Ukraine has not claimed responsibility for any of the strikes on the company.

https://x.com/Reuters/status/2109216194917990726

The first was Sasovo, in the Ryazan region, on October 8. Recorded Future News reported that Russian state media, citing Yandex, said the facility was shut down, and that NetBlocks measured connectivity on Yandex's network falling to about 70 percent of normal. Affected services reportedly included Yandex Cloud, Yandex Disk and Yandex Documents.

Al Jazeera, drawing on AFP and Reuters, reported that Sasovo houses two of the three supercomputers Yandex uses to develop its AI models. Yandex did not say whether those machines were affected. On Friday, October 9, Ukrainian drones struck a data centre in the Kaluga region and knocked part of it out of action, Al Jazeera said, citing Yandex.

On Telegram, Yandex warned that the ability to create new resources in its cloud may be limited and advised customers to move workloads elsewhere, Recorded Future News reported. Al Jazeera quoted the company as saying its teams were working day and night to keep services running. Yandex has not said how long the Vladimir site will stay offline.

Al Jazeera quoted Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy as saying Ukraine responds in kind to Russian strikes on Ukrainian data centres, without sharing details. Recorded Future News reported that Russia began hitting Ukrainian data centres in September, including at least six damaged in late September. No outlet has confirmed what was lost at Vladimir.
