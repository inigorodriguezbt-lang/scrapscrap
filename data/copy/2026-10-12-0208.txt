STORIES

Sakana AI says a 27B model got better by training on its own team

Sakana AI says two rounds of a 27B open model organising copies of itself into a team, then training on the best runs, lifted research scores per output token 1.2 to 1.6 times.


Coinbase's Armstrong says a fine-tuned 9B open model beat frontier models at fraud checks

Coinbase's Brian Armstrong says a Qwen3.5-9B fine-tuned on internal data beat frontier models at payments fraud checks, faster and cheaper. No figures were published.


Karpathy puts people who have watched frontier agents work at about 20 million

Andrej Karpathy's napkin maths: 6 billion people have barely touched an LLM, 20 million have watched agents finish months of coding from a prompt, and about 5,000 have frontier systems internally.


Hugging Face's Delangue says OpenAI's maths proofs are now open training environments

A community dataset turns 369 Lean 4 results OpenAI published this month into tasks where an agent writes the proof. Clément Delangue calls it rough: an equivalent proof can still score zero.


Apple tested an Apple TV with built-in Siri microphones, Bloomberg's Gurman reports

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports Apple tested an Apple TV 4K that hears Siri directly, and a remote with a speaker, ahead of an event expected on Tuesday.

SNIPPETS

Dolphin AI says its new Inference API, running DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, delivers "Opus 5-class" coding and agent performance at 97 percent lower cost than frontier models, with $30 credits for the first 1,000 signups.

The performance and price claims are the vendor's own; no independent benchmark of the hosted model has been published yet.


The Information reports Anthropic has signed a previously unannounced multiyear cloud deal with IREN, the former bitcoin miner, and estimates a 245 MW Louisiana site could earn $1.3 billion a month at full inference use.

IREN had disclosed only an unnamed frontier-AI customer in August; the revenue figure is the outlet's estimate, and the article is paywalled.


The Telegraph reports the White House has reinstated the UK AI Safety Institute's access to unreleased models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google for pre-release testing, after lobbying by British ministers.

The earlier US restriction had threatened Britain's plan to lead on AI testing; neither government has been quoted confirming the reversal.


SemiAnalysis says in a clip that even frontier labs did not forecast their own demand, and calls what is happening "a revenue and profitability fast takeoff" rather than an AI fast takeoff.

It puts the labs' scramble for GPUs and premium-priced contracts down to unexpected profitability, which is one analyst firm's reading.

OpenAI's Codex lead, posting as thsottiaux, says a poll on whether Pro plans should get 2X usage in exchange for bringing back 5-hour limits came out less lopsided than he expected; the post drew about 2,400 likes.

It shows how strongly users dislike short rolling limits, which Theo, replying, said people "really hate".


Elon Musk posted that people should "join the Terafab project", saying the value of its output will rank among the top 20 country GDPs; the post had about 1,300 likes when read.

Terafab is the chip-fabrication project analyst Tim Culpan has written about; the GDP comparison is Musk's own, with no figures in the post.
