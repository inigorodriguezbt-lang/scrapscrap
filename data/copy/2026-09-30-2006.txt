Runway's Praxis-1 teaches robots from web video, weights promised

Runway says its first world action model, Praxis-1, matched robot-video pretraining on placement error, 16.1 cm against 16.0 cm, using only web video.

https://x.com/runwayml/status/2105358708599619790


OpenAI says Moonshot-linked users tried to extract its models' reasoning

OpenAI says 16,000 requests from more than 4,000 users tried to pull hidden reasoning from its models in two days, with a core cluster tied to Moonshot AI.


OpenAI and Synopsys announce GPT-Synopsys, a model that runs chip-design tools

The two companies say GPT-Synopsys will operate Synopsys' chip-design software on its own, with engineers delegating goals and revenue shared with OpenAI.


Ant Group launches Ling-3.1-flash, a 560 billion parameter model, in free trial

Ant's InclusionAI says Ling-3.1-flash activates 25 billion of 560 billion parameters and will be open-sourced after a two-week trial, TechNode reported.


Reddit ends RSS feeds in November, citing scraping by AI bots

Reddit says RSS has become a common surface for large-scale scraping, and will end feeds on 13 November and its public API in March.


dot.com now points to Grok, and X thinks xAI is trolling OpenAI's Dots

The domain dot.com redirects to Grok's download page, and the internet is convinced xAI bought it to needle OpenAI's new Dots agent.


Magnitude, an open-source engine that tunes itself to your hardware

Magnitude says it compiles kernels on your own machine and runs up to 2x faster than llama.cpp. Its Apache 2.0 repo has 5,600 stars.


Dutch security group DIVD says an AI agent breached it via two Zammad zero-days

DIVD says an attacker chained two zero-days in open-source helpdesk Zammad to reach root, and that an AI agent decided each step itself.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Perplexity says pplx-embed-v2-context-9b-preview, a model that embeds each chunk with the whole document in view, sets a new best on ConTEB and turbopuffer's new context-bench.

CEO Aravind Srinivas said the contextual embedding models are being open-sourced; the scores are Perplexity's own and come from a preview release.


Arena says GPT-6.1 Sol (Max) landed third on its Code Arena WebDev board with 1,759 points, 70 above GPT-6 Sol at the same blended $8 per million tokens.

Arena also says Sol sits within 30 points of GPT-6 Astra at 80% lower token cost, which shifts its price-performance frontier.


Perplexity's Aravind Srinivas says anyone, with no account needed, can forward or CC computer@perplexity.com and its agent will run the task in the background, free for a limited time.

It is an email-native way to hand work to an agent, with no app to install; Perplexity has not said how long the free period lasts.


Runway says CTO Kamil Sindi showed Solaris, its first World Interface Model, at an AI Summit keynote: an operating system whose every frame is generated in real time in response to user intent.

Runway frames it as a future where all software is generated without code; the demo is the company's own and unreviewed.

https://x.com/runwayml/status/2105365494085951662


OpenAI's developer account says shareable profiles will bring a person's Sites and plugins together in ChatGPT, so others can find and reuse what they have built.

It gives the new plugin and Sites features a public showcase page, a step toward the marketplace OpenAI announced at DevDay.

https://x.com/OpenAIDevs/status/2105365703654310113


TestingCatalog reports Anthropic is preparing a separate Skills attachment menu for Claude chats on mobile, based on its findings in the app. The company has not announced it.

Skills currently live behind other menus; a dedicated entry would make them easier to reach on phones, if it ships as found.


The Financial Times reports MI5 has issued a rare espionage alert urging UK universities to cut ties with the China General Technology Research Institute, which funded AI research.

The FT calls the alert rare, and it puts research partnerships in AI under national security scrutiny.


Axios reports Alex Stamos, most recently of Corridor and SentinelOne, is joining Cognition as its chief security officer.

Stamos previously oversaw security at Corridor and SentinelOne, and the hire gives the coding-agent maker a dedicated security chief.
