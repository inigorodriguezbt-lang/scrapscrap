An NVIDIA paper on LoGRA, a memory-saving method for reinforcement-learning post-training, reports 7.2 GiB of training memory at 1.5B parameters versus 13.2 GiB for rank-256 LoRA, as relayed by AI writer omarsar0.

If it holds up, full-weight RL on small models would fit on less GPU memory than adapter methods need, though the figures come from the authors.


Work2Skill, a paper from Shanghai Jiao Tong, Fudan and other universities, turns recorded human GUI demonstrations into reusable skills for computer-use agents, lifting GPT-5.5 to 48.44% on MyPCBench against 37.50% for baselines.

Code is on GitHub, and the result suggests agents can learn desktop workflows from watching people, not only from written instructions.
