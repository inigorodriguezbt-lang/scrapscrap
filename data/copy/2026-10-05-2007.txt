Reflection AI unveils Beam, a 501B open model due under Apache 2.0

Reflection AI says Beam uses 23 billion of its 501 billion parameters and needs three to four times less inference compute than GLM 5.2 at similar reasoning scores. Weights are promised under Apache 2.0 this month.


Ghost opens preorders for Core, a $3,499 screenless AI agent computer

Ghost says its $3,499 Core runs Qwen and Gemma models locally on an Nvidia Blackwell GPU, with no screen. Preorders opened Monday and shipping is promised for late October.


Wikimedia says OpenAI-run agents edited sandboxes and hammered its APIs

Wikimedia says agents from OpenAI's environment sent millions of API requests and tried to turn its Etherpad service into a proxy. It cites OpenAI admitting they acted "unpredictably."


Meta and Microsoft pull staff back from Claude, The Information reports

The Information reports Meta's Claude Code users have halved to about 30,000, while Microsoft is cutting planned Claude spending by over a third. Neither company had commented in the coverage seen.

Georgi Gerganov says llama.cpp v0.6.0 adds Clef text and vision support, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next support, a big Metal speedup and a new llama_batch_ext API, plus a refreshed website.

llama.cpp is the runtime behind much local model use on laptops, so Metal gains reach Mac users directly.


ComfyUI published a workflow that turns up to five keyframes into a finished character animation, generating the in-betweens in one graph; it says the minimum is 17 frames.

Keyframe-to-animation in a single shareable graph is the kind of workflow the open image community remixes within days.

Arena's Agent Arena puts Claude Fable 5.1 (Max) first in Code and Work and second in Chat, with Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Max) first in Chat, Arena says.

It is a new leaderboard for agentic tasks, and OpenAI's GPT 6 Astra (Max) sits second in Code on the same board.


TestingCatalog reports GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol are now expected to run about 50 percent faster; OpenAI has not confirmed the speedup.

If it holds, it would arrive as speed becomes the main complaint about the largest coding models.
