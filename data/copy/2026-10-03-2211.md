## Altman says religious framing of AI is 'a real safety issue'

**Sam Altman says he is "very uncomfortable" when people ascribe religious force to AI models, three years after he described OpenAI's goal as "magic intelligence in the sky."**

OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman said he feels "very uncomfortable" when people "ascribe religious force or a surrender of human judgment to AI models," and called the habit "a real safety issue," The Decoder reported on 3 October. The outlet did not say where Altman made the remarks, and the material this report found carries only the quoted lines.

The Decoder notes the stance sits oddly with Altman's own earlier language. As recently as 2024, it says, he spoke of building "magic intelligence in the sky" and of feeling on the side of the angels. The outlet frames the new comments as a shift in tone, which is its reading rather than anything Altman has said about his past remarks.

The comments follow a New York Times report on Anthropic's private meetings with religious thinkers about Claude's possible consciousness and moral development, according to The Decoder. They also follow a statement by Pope Leo XIV that algorithms lack the spark of humanity. The Decoder does not say Altman named Anthropic, and the link is the outlet's inference.

Commentator kimmonismus read the remark as a jab at Anthropic, writing on X that it was a "based Sam take" he could not agree with more. That is one observer's interpretation. This report has not seen the New York Times article, and neither Anthropic nor OpenAI has commented on the exchange in the sources reviewed.

## TestingCatalog finds a 'coming soon' wallet inside ChatGPT's settings

**A ChatGPT settings screen now reads "Your Wallet is on its way," TestingCatalog reports, weeks after World App became World Money, though nothing public links the two.**

TestingCatalog reports that a "Coming soon" wallet entry appeared in ChatGPT's settings around 30 September, with the text: "A little wallet. A world of possibilities. Your Wallet is on its way." The outlet published the finding on 3 October. OpenAI has not announced a wallet, and no one else has reported on the entry so far, so it stays unconfirmed until the company ships it.

The outlet ties the timing to World App, which rebranded as World Money on 17 September. Sam Altman co-founded World and leads OpenAI, and TestingCatalog speculates the two launches could be coordinated. It also says plainly that nothing public suggests the ChatGPT Wallet and World Money share a balance, so the link is the outlet's theory, not a finding.

A more concrete context is OpenAI's Dots agents, which need a way to pay for things. TestingCatalog says OpenAI's head of app platform has indicated the company is thinking carefully about how to let agents pay. It adds that Instant Checkout, launched in September 2025, proved too inflexible, which is the outlet's assessment of why OpenAI may be looking for another route.

What the wallet would hold, who could use it and where it would launch are all unknown in the material reviewed. The finding rests on a settings string TestingCatalog saw in the app, a type of evidence that can change or vanish before release. Readers should treat the wallet as a possibility, not a product, until OpenAI says otherwise.
