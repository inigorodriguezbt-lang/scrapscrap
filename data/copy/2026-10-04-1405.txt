Strata repo runs 125B Qwen model on a single gaming GPU

Strata, an MIT-licensed runner for Alibaba's 125B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, has 9,500 GitHub stars after four days. Its author reports 53 to 94 tokens a second on an RTX 5070.

Tom's Hardware reports a free browser game called Taipei GTA, vibe-coded by AICodeWith, reached 1.2 million concurrent players in three days. The developers reportedly spent $10,000 in AI tokens building it.

If the figures hold, it is one of the biggest audiences yet for a game written almost entirely by a coding model.


Tom's Hardware reports Carlos Antelo's open-source ldraw-nova lets GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 design LEGO models as LDraw CAD files with more than 2,000 real pieces; none has been built yet.

It tests whether frontier models can handle precise 3D assembly, though the report says no physical build has confirmed the designs hold together.


Tom's Hardware reports that an AI engineer used GPT-6 Astra to read a 217-year-old Napoleonic-era cipher, starting from one image and one prompt and finishing in six hours.

A single-prompt codebreaking run is a striking demo of the model's vision and reasoning, though the report rests on the engineer's own account.


Tom's Hardware reports Figure scrapped its old F.02 humanoid fleet by lowering the robots into a 75-ton vat of molten steel, one of them giving a thumbs-up on the way down.

Figure chose melting over costly disassembly for its retired second-generation robots, a rare public look at what happens to humanoids when they are replaced.


Tom's Hardware reports an 'AI Torture Chamber' repository, which puts chatbots in simulated pain, has drawn heavy online criticism, with some demanding GitHub remove it over the treatment of the models.

The row shows how far debates about machine suffering have moved from philosophy seminars into ordinary open-source repo fights.


Commentator mark_k wrote on X that users say Anthropic may be routing some Claude traffic to a newer model than Fable 5.1, with a Tuesday, October 6 release rumored. Anthropic has not confirmed either.

The evidence is user-posted animations and short films, which cannot show which model is running behind the picker.


SCM, a Show HN project under an MIT licence, searches photos and video on Apple Silicon Macs offline, by meaning, on-screen text and spoken words transcribed with Whisper. Its README lists 53 GitHub stars.

It is a local-only alternative to cloud photo search, with scene search for video returning results by timecode.
