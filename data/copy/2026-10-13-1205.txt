PDF TO MP3, a Show HN project, turns PDFs into audiobooks through an MCP server added to Claude or Codex, with speech billed to the user's own OpenAI or Gemini key. It listed at $0.

It routes the work through an AI subscription people already pay for, rather than adding another monthly fee; the Show HN post had 34 points.


TestingCatalog says Nature Will has launched Natura-1, a foundation model for physical tasks that the company says was trained on one million hours of egocentric video of people handling objects.

Nature Will claims the model can handle objects it has never seen and track what it has already moved on longer tasks; no independent tests have been reported.

Bloomberg reports, per a Techmeme summary, that Alibaba launched Qwen Glasses N1 from about $450, with 50-megapixel cameras, and a Pro model near $520 with eye-tracking, alongside Qwen Clip AI earbuds.

It extends Alibaba's push from software into consumer AI hardware; the pricing comes from the Bloomberg report, which could not be read in full.


The Financial Times reports, per a Techmeme summary, that Chinese tech companies bought more than one million Nvidia gaming and workstation chips, mostly RTX 5090 and RTX Pro cards, for basic AI workloads.

The FT's sources say the purchases came during an AI chip shortage, a sign of demand spilling from data-centre parts into consumer-grade hardware.


Reuters reports, per a Techmeme summary, that Anthropic and OpenAI have pushed for substantial control over what outside AI evaluators can publish, citing concern that trade secrets could leak.

Independent testing is one of the few outside checks on frontier models, so limits on what evaluators can publish bear on how much the public learns.


Reuters reports, per a Techmeme summary, that US banking regulators are questioning banks about their use of agentic AI, Chinese-developed AI models and safeguards for autonomous systems.

It is an early sign of supervisors moving from general AI guidance to specific questions about agents and model origin at lenders.


TestingCatalog says Google appears to be rolling out custom MCP support in the Gemini desktop app to more users, and speculates that Computer Use and Superprojects may follow Gemini 4.

MCP support would let Gemini's desktop app connect to the same tools that Claude and Codex users already plug in; the Computer Use link is TestingCatalog's guess.


The Financial Times reports, per a Techmeme summary, that a global chip sell-off deepened after Broadcom's chief executive likened AI investing to a casino, with SK Hynix down as much as 9.2%.

Samsung fell as much as 8.8% and Micron 4% in the same report, showing how quickly memory makers reacted to doubts about AI spending.
