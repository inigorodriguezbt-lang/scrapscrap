Alibaba releases Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo, an 8-step image model with open weights

The 7-billion-parameter checkpoint makes 2K images and edits them in eight denoising steps, and Alibaba's Qwen team says quality does not drop.


Simon Willison builds a blog feature by voice with Codex while cooking dinner

The developer talked a Django Newsletters page into existence with the ChatGPT desktop app's Codex tab, then spent another half hour typing fixes.


Google tests an Ultra mode in AI Studio Build, TestingCatalog reports

TestingCatalog says the unannounced mode sits beside Plan, Build and Security review, and may be meant to work with Google's upcoming Gemini 4 Argon.

International Cyber Digest says a researcher found Lime's app lets anyone track the route of every rental bike, and that Lime told him this is intended behaviour and it will not fix it.

If the claim holds, routes could reveal where riders live and work; the account's video follows one route near a US Army site.

https://x.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2108549889600688260


Reuters reports the Chinese developer of ARTEX has made the AI pentesting agent closed-source, after CrowdStrike said it was used in the hacks on South Korean banks.

The tool was open source when the campaign hit; closing it does not recall copies already released, so the change may do little to limit misuse.


Bloomberg reports a run of cyberattacks on Japanese companies in September exposed the data of millions of people, and that AI is lowering the barrier to hacking, prompting calls for security checks.

It follows the South Korean bank breaches that CrowdStrike linked to an AI pentesting agent, suggesting AI-assisted intrusions are spreading across East Asia.


Rohan Paul says a new Meta paper finds agent memory works better when the confidence cutoff is raised only for claims about a user's values and beliefs, which were 21.5% of candidate memories.

Per his summary, those claims produced more unsupported memories than all other categories combined, so a bad write can later be treated as settled fact.


The Financial Times reports, via Rohan Paul, that HSBC plans to cut about 70% of its UK financial advisers and move wealth clients toward AI-backed digital service.

Half of the unit's management and specialist roles would also go, according to people familiar with the plans, making it a concrete example of AI replacing advice staff.


Kimmonismus says ChatGPT's new interactive interface made a Paris packing checklist he could tick off, and that he had underestimated how useful it is.

It is one user's early impression of a ChatGPT interface change, shared with screenshots, and OpenAI has not been quoted on how widely it has rolled out.


Trace2Env, an Apache-2.0 repo from a Hugging Face daily paper with 164 upvotes, builds a text-world simulator from recorded agent traces, with no access to the original system's code.

The authors say tens of traces suffice, which could let teams train and test agents on replicas of systems they cannot run; the repo reports no scores yet.


Analyst Kyle Chan says the combined free cash flow of Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu has turned negative as Chinese hyperscalers ramp up AI capital spending.

If accurate, it shows how heavily the AI build-out is now drawing on the cash flow of China's three biggest AI spenders.
