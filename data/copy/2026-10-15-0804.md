## TSMC guides fourth-quarter revenue up to $54.3 billion

**The chipmaker's results page shows $46.72 billion in third-quarter revenue against a $44.6 billion to $45.8 billion forecast, with a $52.3 billion to $54.3 billion range for the next quarter.**

TSMC reported third-quarter net revenue of US$46.72 billion on Thursday. That beat the US$44.6 billion to US$45.8 billion range it had guided, according to the quarterly results table on its investor page. The foundry makes most leading AI accelerators, so the gap between forecast and result is closely watched.

The table lists a gross margin of 66.4% and an operating margin of 58.7%. The operating margin is above the 56.0% to 58.0% range TSMC gave. Its guidance assumes 31.98 Taiwan dollars to the US dollar. The page as retrieved carries no net income, capital spending or management comment on AI demand.

For the fourth quarter, the table gives net revenue of US$52.3 billion to US$54.3 billion. It also gives a gross margin of 65.0% to 67.0% and an operating margin of 56.5% to 58.5%, on an exchange rate of 31.8. The page does not say how much comes from AI chips. These are guidance, not results.

Coverage Techmeme points to from CNBC says net income rose about 69% to a record for the sixth straight quarter on AI demand. We could not open that report or TSMC's full earnings release, so the profit figures are unverified here. The investor page itself attributes no growth rate to AI.

## Figma lets teams write design rules in Markdown for AI agents

**Figma Specs is a Markdown editor for design rules, and the company's code layers feature enters public beta, TechCrunch reported from Figma's first Config event in India.**

Figma announced features on Wednesday to control how AI agents work in its design platform, according to TechCrunch, which reported from the company's first Config conference in India. The headline item is Figma Specs, a Markdown-based editor where a team writes down its design language. TechCrunch is the only account we have read so far.

Per the report, Specs can be a standalone Figma file or sit on the canvas. They can reference canvas elements and embed FigJam diagrams, code layers and designs. Figma says teams and agents can use a Spec as a reference while designing. Teams can also gather libraries and guidelines in one place that agents consult, which Figma says should make AI output more predictable.

Code layers add code to canvas designs for more interactive prototypes. They move to public beta after a private beta that began in June, TechCrunch said. Users can ask the agent to turn a design into code. Figma's MCP server now supports more than 20 new tools, including Open Code, Dia, Grok Bot and Sarvam, the report said.

Figma chief design officer Loredana Crisan told TechCrunch that 17 times more lines of code are produced today than in the past. Releases have grown only 1.3 times, she said, and much of that code is made to discover what a product should be. The report gives no date or pricing for Specs. It says the agent on canvas reached more users the week before.
