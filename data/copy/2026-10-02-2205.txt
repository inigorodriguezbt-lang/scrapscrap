Meta open-sources Muse Gadgets firmware and gives away 5,000 Home Link devices

Meta's Alexandr Wang says Muse Gadgets, an open-source ESP32 firmware and Linux SDK, lets anyone build hardware for its Muse agent, and 5,000 Home Link devices are free to subscribers.

Redis creator Salvatore Sanfilippo's DwarfStar 4 (ds4), an MIT-licensed C engine for Mac, CUDA and ROCm, lists 39.4 tokens a second on DeepSeek V4 Flash on an M5 Max with 128GB.

It is a deliberately narrow engine for running near-frontier open models locally, and its Hacker News post reached 89 points within hours.


Show HN: ldraw-nova, an AGPL-3.0 project, has an AI agent plan and script buildable Lego models as LDraw code, with Blender-editable GLB export and Meta Quest 3 viewing; it has 42 stars.

The agent writes a generator script rather than placing bricks directly, which its README says makes vehicles, spaceships and Technic builds tractable.
