STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Google unveils Gemini 4 Argon, limited to cyber defenders for now

Google says Gemini 4 Argon tops GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 on 13 of 19 published benchmarks and can write 1 million tokens at once. Only vetted cyber defenders can use it today.


Cognition is first to run Nvidia Vera Rubin on CoreWeave, claims 4.8x

Cognition says Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 racks deliver up to 4.8 times the token throughput of GB200 on its SWE-2 inference, and that Devin workloads moved within days of delivery.

https://x.com/cognition/status/2105408461701824732


Study finds 543,699 leaked GitHub credentials that still work, one from 2009

Truffle Security tested secrets in 224 million public repositories and found over half a million still valid. Most Google Cloud service keys survive, while almost no npm tokens do.


Hegseth names Musk, Luckey and Gingrich to lead Pentagon future-warfare study

Project Meridian has 120 days to name the AI, autonomy and robotics the US military needs. Hegseth also announced a four-star Autonomous Warfare Command, due by October 2027.


Google pays about 100 publishers for AI answers, The Information says

Payments for content used in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini range from under $1,000 over several months to more than $1 million a year, and some publishers cannot tell how they are calculated.


Bank of America says Meta's Muse agent threatens Apple's services revenue

An investor note argues AI agents could intercept transactions that flow through the iPhone. Muse has topped the US App Store for almost two weeks, with 3.4 million downloads, 9to5Mac reports.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Google says Gemini 4 Argon agents have freed over 300 TiB of memory across its data centers and helped move 800,000-plus lines of kernel code from C/C++ to Rust.

The claims come from Google's own launch post and are unverified, but they are the first concrete examples it gives of Argon working on production infrastructure.


Arena says Gemini 4 Argon (High) ranks eighth in its Agent Arena at $0.62 a task, and first for steerability, the model's ability to course-correct when users push back.

It is an early, crowd-voted read on agent behavior, which differs from the vendor benchmarks Google published at launch.


Cursor published a short film on Innate, a seven-person robotics startup whose co-founder Vignesh Anand says the team does the work of 50.

The film is a look at a tiny team building home robots with AI coding tools, and it comes from Cursor, which has an interest in the story.

https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2105406306966511773


OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux posted a screen recording of his Dots agent painting his portrait, first cartoonish, then better after he asked it to find a recent photo of him online.

It is a rare look at what OpenAI's new always-on agents do on their own cloud computers, shown by an OpenAI employee.

https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2105395200248156556


SemiAnalysis says it tested GLM-5.3 on its ExploitGym cyber benchmark and found the model spent much of its budget checking whether hidden runtime conditions changed its conclusion.

It adds an independent look at the open-weight model Anthropic's red team said nearly matches Mythos Preview at building exploits.


Bloomberg says HPE raised its networking revenue forecast and announced a $1.2 billion order from cloud company Vultr, a sign it is benefiting from the AI build-out.

The order shows AI demand reaching networking gear, not only GPUs, a year after HPE bought Juniper Networks.


Strata, a Show HN with 17 points, pitches a semantic layer for business intelligence that refuses LLM questions it cannot answer correctly. It says it was born at Netflix.

It is proprietary, with a beta license and no repository, so the claims of sub-second answers and 15-minute setup are the maker's own.
