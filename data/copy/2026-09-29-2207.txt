Anthropic says Z.ai's downloadable GLM-5.3 built working exploits in 50 of 410 attempts against Mythos Preview's 56, and that its safeguards fall to a cover story.

OpenAI's Decisions API lets GPT-6 Luna pick from answers you define

OpenAI's new Decisions API returns one predefined answer with a confidence score, powered by GPT-6 Luna, and is in limited preview with no published pricing.


OpenAI lets ChatGPT Plus and Pro plans power 16 outside developer tools

Sign in with ChatGPT lets Plus and Pro subscribers spend their existing allowance in Devin, Notion, Vercel and 13 other partner tools, with per-partner limits.


ChatGPT plugins get sidebar panels, file viewers and a Plugin Creator

OpenAI says plugin extensions give apps a home in ChatGPT's sidebar, with interactive panels and file viewers, and a Plugin Creator builds plugins through conversation.

fal says its fal Agent, a chat tool that picks models, writes prompts and runs generations across image, video, audio and 3D, is now available to everyone from a single browser interface.

fal's docs say model runs are billed at standard API rates while the agent's own reasoning and tools are currently free, and the product is still labelled early access.

Developer HarshithLucky3 ran the same prompt, animate a cyclist riding a bicycle, on GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Sol at xhigh effort in Codex, and says the new model shows a huge gap.

It is one user's single-prompt test of OpenAI's newly released model, with 266 likes on X, not a benchmark, but the clip shows both outputs side by side.

https://x.com/HarshithLucky3/status/2105025923095675353


Drafted's AI floor plan generator is now a plugin for Autodesk Revit, turning a house footprint and room list into five plan options built from real walls, floors, roofs, doors and windows.

TestingCatalog reports results take about 45 seconds according to Drafted's own tests, and teams can swap in their saved components and edit every element by hand.

https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2105032750789210235


General Intuition, which trains agents on billions of action-labelled gameplay videos from sister company Medal, raised $220 million at a $6.2 billion valuation, GamesBeat reports, bringing its total funding past $650 million.

CEO Pim de Witte says leading robotics and world models train on less than 1 percent of the action data Medal lets the company use.


Baseten says it is one of the first open-model inference providers in OpenAI's B2B Marketplace, so OpenAI enterprise customers can spend existing commitments on open models served by Baseten in Codex or the Responses API.

Baseten's post names no specific models and points interested customers to a waitlist form, so availability is not yet open.


A Show HN project, TurboGPT, trains a tiny byte-level GPT in CUDA C++ in under a minute, and its README reports 2.5295 bits per byte after 1.5 billion training tokens.

The MIT-licensed repo had 8 stars and 19 Hacker News points at the time of reading, so the result is the author's claim and unreplicated.


OpenAI says ChatGPT can now be mentioned in Slack and Microsoft Teams channels, threads and DMs to turn a messy thread into a plan, slides or a spreadsheet, on Business and Enterprise plans.

The integration draws on approved connected sources, and OpenAI says teammates can refine results in the same conversation without needing individual ChatGPT licences.
