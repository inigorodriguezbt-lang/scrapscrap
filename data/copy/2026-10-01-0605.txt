260M-parameter image model tops one 6.5 times bigger, authors say

A new arXiv paper says Looped-DiT, with 260 million parameters, tops a 1.7B baseline on five text-to-image benchmarks by rerunning shared transformer blocks inside each denoising step, with 4.9 times less inference compute.


Agent speed benchmark puts GPT-6 Astra first, open models nowhere

A new benchmark times AI agents on desktop tasks. Its authors report GPT-6 Astra scoring 90.8% on OSWorld in 90 seconds at $0.71 a task, while Gemini 3.8 Flash matched the top score at $0.11.

Tencent has leased about 100,000 advanced AI chips from Oracle for five years, worth about $7B, the Financial Times reports, with the chips sitting in Southeast Asian data centres unavailable inside China.

Rental of overseas compute sits outside US export controls, which govern where chips physically go rather than who rents their capacity, the FT says.


Artificial Analysis says the gaps between score milestones on its Intelligence Index have shrunk from 239 to 126 to 99 days, with the current leader scoring 57.6.

It is one outfit's index, but it puts a number on the sense that frontier gains are arriving faster than a year ago.


OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux says ChatGPT now lets users build and deploy MCP servers directly, restricting access to chosen people or sharing them publicly; the post had 1,663 likes.

It moves tool-building for agents inside ChatGPT itself, where until now MCP servers had to be hosted and wired up elsewhere.


Salesforce has agreed to buy AI customer-research startup Listen Labs, reportedly for $2B, Axios reports; Listen Labs raised a $69M Series B at a $500M valuation in January.

The reported price is four times January's valuation, a sign of how fast buyers are paying up for agent-driven research tools.


The cua-speedrun benchmark reports its FastCUA harness cut desktop action latency to 2 to 28 milliseconds from the usual 2 to 3 seconds, and trims OSWorld to 50 of 295 tasks.

The authors say the shortened task set keeps a 0.98 rank correlation with the full benchmark while cutting evaluation time by about 85%.


Minneapolis's city council voted 7-4 on 29 September to require a human monitor in the driver's seat of every autonomous taxi, with access to steering, braking and acceleration.

Supporters cited mobility for disabled riders; critics argued the mandate undercuts what driverless service offers, according to HuggingNews's summary of the debate.
