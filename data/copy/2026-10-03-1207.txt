Tom's Hardware reports GPT-6 Astra cleared World of Warcraft's orc starting zone in 40 minutes with no deaths, after building its own pathfinder on a private server from one prompt in OpenAI's Codex.

The run, which Tom's says relied on parsing server network data and quest files, is a stunt that shows how far one-prompt agent builds have come.


Anthropic is adding a Mods system to Claude Code, The Decoder reports: JavaScript or TypeScript functions that hook into tool calls, prompts and UI rendering, working in the CLI, desktop app and partly in VS Code.

The Decoder notes mods run with the user's permissions and are not sandboxed, and organisations can restrict which ones staff may install.


DeepMind's Benjamin Bratton, Blaise Aguera y Arcas and James Manyika propose "Artificial Symbiotic Intelligence", arguing AGI will emerge from networks of humans and agents working together rather than from one superintelligence, The Decoder reports.

The paper reframes alignment as an ongoing negotiation between people, agents and institutions, a pointed alternative to the singularity story.


Theo says Opus 5.5 is the first model to break 50 per cent of traffic in T3 Code, meaning half of all prompts in the app go to it, posting a chart.

It is one app's usage share, measured by its own maker, but it shows which model coders are actually choosing right now.

The finding comes from the outlet's own look at the product, so it is unconfirmed until Meta ships; it hints at a new agent harness.
