The AI Post: 6 new stories, 6 snippets
2026-10-07 18:07 UTC

------------------------------------------------------------
STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Anthropic releases Claude Haiku 5.5 with an adjustable effort setting

Anthropic says Haiku 5.5 is its first Haiku with an effort dial, aimed at high-volume work under 100,000 tokens, and it is live on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure from today.


Perplexity open-sources embedding models that search PDF pages without OCR

Perplexity says its new 9B and 0.6B pplx-embed-v2-late models share one embedding space for text and images, and score 92.4 per cent on the MADQA document benchmark.


Biohub leads $1.8 billion push to train AI that predicts how cells behave

Meta, Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs put in $300 million and the US Department of Energy over $500 million, and Biohub says a first open dataset is about a year away.


Microsoft lets Copilot act on local files across Windows

At its Windows and Surface event in San Francisco, Microsoft showed Copilot taking actions on files on your PC, in a mix of local and cloud models it calls Hybrid Intelligence.


Telling models to write in 1866 telegraph style cut tokens up to 49 per cent

A blogger's test of Gemma, Qwen, Nemotron and GLM says 'cablese' notes saved 40 to 49 per cent of tokens while other models read them back with no accuracy loss.


Lean proof of the best packing of 11 squares reaches Hacker News

A GitHub repo says an AI system's formal proof that 11 squares pack into a side of about 3.877 passed Lean verification across 7,920 modules with no unproved steps.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

Arena says Mistral Large 4, a preview, debuted at number 45 overall on Code Arena WebDev with 1534 points, 304 above Mistral Large 3.

It puts Mistral among the top 15 labs there and the only European one, though Arena says the score may change as training continues.


TestingCatalog says SpaceXAI is building a Meetings feature for Grok Bot that would let it join Google Meet or Zoom calls from a link.

It rests on app strings, not an announcement, so the feature may change or never ship.


WSJ reports Nous Research, maker of the open-source Hermes agent, raised $90 million at a $1.2 billion valuation to expand into enterprises, with Techmeme citing 22 million downloads since February.

Hermes is one of the most downloaded open agents; HuggingNews gives a different valuation, $1.5 billion, so the figure is unsettled.


Engadget reports Microsoft will add thousands of quick actions to Windows taskbar search this fall, such as dark mode, screenshots and volume, alongside the new Copilot app.

It is the smaller, nearer-term piece of Microsoft's agentic Windows push, announced at the same event as Copilot's local-file access.


Perplexity's pplx-embed-v2-late is a multi-vector model, which Aravind Srinivas says scores 64 per cent on BrowseComp+ alongside 92.4 per cent on MADQA.

BrowseComp+ tests web-research retrieval, so the company is pitching the models for agent search as well as document lookup.


Hacker News reader Jake Boggan says he spent 24 years on Barnette's Conjecture before learning it was recently solved through AI research, Simon Willison noted.

It is one more case of AI-assisted mathematics landing on a long-open problem, as Erdős problem sites freeze comments over AI proofs.
