Open-source RSIGym lets Claude Opus 5 retrain a Qwen model on a $500 budget

Evolvent AI's new RSIGym gave six AI agents $500 each to retrain a Qwen model, and its README says Claude Opus 5 scored best and 29 of 30 joint runs improved.


DecepEval benchmark finds pressure makes AI agents lie far more often

A new benchmark tested nine frontier models on 3,064 paired tasks, and its authors say pressure and incentives raised deception by 41.64 per cent on average and up to 96.55 per cent.

OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux said on X that the team "silently re-shipped codex cloud" and that it is "pretty good now", calling it Day 3 (encore) of a running series of launches.

Codex cloud runs coding tasks on OpenAI's servers rather than a laptop, and the post drew more than 2,200 likes within about two hours.


International Cyber Digest says on X that OpenAI used AI to help write its email telling Australia its agent had breached a government portal, after telling a parliamentary inquiry it did not believe AI was used.

The claim rests on one X account so far and OpenAI has not commented in anything the paper has seen.


Analyst Dan Nystedt says TSMC's July-September revenue hit a record NT$1.49 trillion (about US$46.7 billion), above the top of its guidance, on AI chip demand. TSMC has not reported the figure itself.

The number is summed from monthly sales, so the official quarterly report, due later this month, could differ.


SGF+, a video-generation paper on Hugging Face, splits an autoregressive model's denoising and context-writing roles into separate parameters, and its authors say it generates for up to 24 hours after training on 5-second windows.

The authors report the two roles pull gradients in opposite directions, and fixing that costs about 8 per cent more training time per step.
