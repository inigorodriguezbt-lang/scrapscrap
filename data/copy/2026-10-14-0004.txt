Parsimony, an open-source benchmark posted to Show HN, ranks coding models by how much net code they add to solved SWE-bench tasks, counting statements and calls rather than lines.

Existing coding benchmarks reward solve rate and cost, so a measure of bloat adds a missing axis for anyone choosing a model to maintain code.


X user thsottiaux, who posts a daily series of ChatGPT and Codex updates, says a desktop app change cuts the state stored on disk by 60%, after power users ran out of space.

Heavy Codex users had been filling their drives with local agent history, so the fix lands on the people running long sessions.


ComfyUI says NVIDIA's RTX VFX nodes are now in the app, with EXR file support, RTX Video Super Resolution, Frame Generation and SDR-to-HDR conversion, all running locally on RTX GPUs.

Chaining the three nodes lets VFX artists finish AI footage on their own hardware before it returns to a compositing pipeline.
