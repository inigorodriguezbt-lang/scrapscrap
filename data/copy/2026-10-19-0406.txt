No AI agent met a scientist's bar on 135 research tasks, study finds

Researchers gave nine frontier agents 15 open science problems from national-lab scientists, and 114 of the 135 follow-up papers failed an integrity check.


Codex agent that writes its own robot tools tops RoboDojo, HexaFuture says

HexaFuture says a coding agent that repairs its own Python tools after each failure reached 39.7% success on 42 simulated robot tasks, ahead of two learned policies.


SenseNova tests GPT-6-Astra on 12 robot benchmarks, reports 81.9% on RoboDojo

SenseNova says GPT-6-Astra, a general-purpose model, nearly doubled the best published DexJoCo and LIBERO-Ultra-Long results, yet failed plug insertion in all 11 real-robot trials.


OpenMobile-2 phone agents score 79.9% on AndroidWorld, researchers say

Researchers say OpenMobile-2, built on Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.6-27B with open environments and data, lifts AndroidWorld success from 67.1% to 79.9% for the larger model.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

The vLLM project says all six D3 decision models, from 0.6B to 27B parameters, now have merged vLLM recipes, and that D3 ranked first on both Jev Decision Index 0.3.2 boards.

The recipes let teams self-host D3 on vLLM, though the 66.3 text and 71.3 vision scores come from the project's citation of the index.


World launched a World ID suite for banks that includes World ID for Agents, which lets a person delegate their proof of humanity to an AI agent, The Block reports.

It is World's first industry-specific product, and it names no bank customer yet, only design partners including OpenExchange, Bain Capital and VanEck.


Peking University and Ant International researchers say Claude Opus 5 scored highest on BizBench, their 50-task business-agent test, with 64.0% strict success, ahead of GPT-6 Sol at 61.3%.

Only 40.0% of tasks were completed in all three of GPT-6 Sol's runs, which suggests consistency, not peak score, is the hard part for business agents.


Tencent Hunyuan researchers say TreeOPSD, a critic-free training method for long-horizon agents, lifted a Qwen3-30B-A3B model to 21.20 average Deep Research accuracy from 17.59 for TreePO.

The authors also report it beats GRPO by 4.97 points on that average, though the results are the paper's own and no code link is given.


Developer Jared Siegel argues in a blog post, now on the Hacker News front page with 75 points, that engineers should run their own LLM proxy to see keys, models, clients and cache misses.

Provider dashboards show little about agent traffic, he says, so a self-hosted proxy with virtual keys is his answer; he names no specific product.


SemiconductorsX relays that Intel's CEO told Nikkei that Moonshot's Kimi, Zhipu's GLM and DeepSeek are not technically behind Anthropic or OpenAI, and are much cheaper to use.

The claim comes from a chipmaker chief with a stake in the China debate, and it is relayed secondhand, so the Nikkei interview itself was not checked.
