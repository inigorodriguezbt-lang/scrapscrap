The AI Post: 4 new snippets
2026-10-05 06:05 UTC

Google has paused its Open Source Software Vulnerability Rewards Program, saying the pause is due to a significant rise in automated submissions, the vast majority of which are not valid, TechCrunch reports.
AI-generated bug reports are now swamping a bounty programme meant to pay for real open-source security work; Google promises an update in the first quarter of 2027.

Bloomberg reports that Bloomberg Intelligence says the performance lead of American AI companies over China has narrowed to a record low in recent months, as labs such as DeepSeek gained ground.
It is one analyst's reading of the US-China gap, and Bloomberg's summary does not say which benchmarks or measures the finding rests on.

SoftBank chief Masayoshi Son told a Kyoto tech forum that superintelligence could pose a greater threat than conflict between humans if misused, and called for global cooperation on safeguards, Bloomberg reports.
Son is one of the industry's most committed backers, so Bloomberg's tech editor called the caution rare coming from him.

Bloomberg reports Malaysia plans to introduce its first dedicated artificial intelligence law in early 2027, as governments step up efforts to regulate the technology.
It adds another Asian country to the list moving from voluntary guidance to statute; Bloomberg's summary gives no detail of what the bill would cover.
