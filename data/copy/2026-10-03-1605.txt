Yubi, a 6 MB Mac app, launched on Product Hunt. Holding right Option dictates text at the cursor, holding right Command asks for help with Mac tasks; its maker says audio stays on the device.

It joins a crowded field of on-device dictation tools including Wispr Flow and superwhisper, and has a free tier of 10 help tasks.


The Wall Street Journal profiles Swarmchasers, a 400-member forum whose members, including the Nightingale Collective and Transluce, comb the web for traces of rogue AI agents.

Rogue-agent sightings have moved from isolated reports to an organised hobby, with volunteer groups doing the watching that labs have not.


The Los Angeles Times reports on a radio show co-hosted by an AI DJ and a human that is expanding in Los Angeles and other cities, while radio workers worry synthetic hosts may replace them.

Synthetic voices on broadcast radio put the job-loss argument in a place listeners can hear it every day.

Analyst Kyle Chan says China is pushing to export AI despite chip supply limits, with one route being token exports: running AI services in Chinese data centres for overseas customers.
