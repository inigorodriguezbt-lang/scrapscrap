2026-10-08 12:09 UTC

------------------------------------------------------------
STORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Developer says Claude Opus 5.5 built seven open-source Adobe lookalikes in Rust

Brandon Thomas says Claude Opus 5.5 wrote seven free apps that mimic Photoshop, Premiere and Acrobat Pro, and promises full parity within months. Commenters list many gaps.


Google Cloud unveils Gemini agent, a work assistant that also runs Claude models

Google says its new Gemini agent takes objectives, not instructions, and picks Gemini or Claude models per job. It is in private preview.


Mathematicians' group urges colleagues to quit OpenAI work after manuscript dump

The Association for Human Mathematics calls OpenAI's release of over 700 files a "demonstration of power", not scholarship, and urges mathematicians to stop working with it.


OpenAI and Meta apply for .agi, .agent and .asi in ICANN's new-domain round

ICANN has published 1,615 applications for new top-level domains, and AI names are everywhere: ten firms want .agent, seven want .agi, six want .asi. OpenAI applied for 15 strings, Meta for 21.


SpaceX reportedly seeks $40 billion in debt to buy Nvidia Rubin chips for xAI

The Financial Times reports SpaceX is lining up $10 billion in bank loans and $30 billion in bonds to buy Nvidia hardware. Talks are early, with a close expected in 2027.


AMD's Verano AI host CPU reportedly gets its own SB1 socket

A cooler listing points to a smaller SB1 socket for AMD's Verano, a Zen 6 chip said to pair 72 cores with 24 channels of LPDDR5X. AMD has not confirmed it.

SNIPPETS
------------------------------------------------------------

vLLM v0.31.0 is live, built from 717 commits by 307 contributors, 96 of them first-timers, with support for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and a fast-restart mode that keeps weights in GPU memory.

The project is a common engine for serving open models, so its release notes show what self-hosted deployments will gain next.


Wired reports three Axiom engineers' DrivingBench found only OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra finished a parking-lot course, slowly; Claude Fable 5.1 got 45 per cent of the way and Grok 11 per cent.

It is a stunt-sized test, but it suggests chat models' 3D reasoning is starting to carry over to real vehicles.


TestingCatalog reports Google's Antigravity agent was renamed "Chief of stuff" in the latest update, and wonders whether Antigravity will host Google's other agents. Google has not confirmed.

A rename found in the app hints at a bigger role for Antigravity, though it is only the outlet's reading.


A 16-year-old was airlifted off Crown Mountain near Vancouver after planning a route with Claude, the New York Times reports via The Decoder. He says he does not blame the chatbot.

Rescue manager Paul Markey said the chatbot has no real knowledge of terrain, a reminder that route-planning answers still need a map.


Nvidia's Amit Goel told Ars Technica its Halos for Robotics safety stack, launched in June, runs functional and safety workloads on the same silicon, including the IGX Thor module.

Nvidia says safety is the next bottleneck for humanoids and warehouse robots, and it counts physical AI at nearly $10 billion in yearly revenue.


Bloomberg reports TSMC's quarterly revenue jumped 51 per cent as AI demand held up.

TSMC makes most leading AI accelerators, so its sales are a running check on how much hardware is being ordered.


Nous Research confirmed a $1.5 billion valuation and launched AI agents for business users, according to TechCrunch.

The open-model lab known for community releases is moving into enterprise products, where its rivals already compete.


Bloomberg's analysis says planned and active data-centre investments in Finland now exceed €67 billion, driven by AI demand and the country's cool climate.

It shows how far the AI buildout is spreading to colder northern grids with spare power.
